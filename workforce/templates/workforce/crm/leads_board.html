The kanban view of the business sales pipeline — one column per stage, one card per prospective client. Driver applicants live on their own board under CRM Driver in the sidebar; nothing on this page shows them. These cards are the same records as the Business Leads table; the board is where you move work along, the table is where you filter and count. Dragging a card between columns saves the new stage straight away.

What you can do here

Drag a card
Drops it into the new stage and saves immediately. If the save fails the card slides back to where it came from and you get an error.
Open a card
Click it (or press Enter on it) to open the lead detail page with the timeline, notes and WhatsApp conversation.
Chat chip
Shown only on cards whose number has WhatsApp history on record; it jumps straight to that conversation on the detail page.
Search
Filters the cards by company, contact person, phone or product category as you type.
Filters
Narrow by source and by assignee, including Assigned to me and Unassigned. Whatever is engaged is named in a chip on the right of the bar — press its ✕ to drop that one filter, or Reset to clear the lot. The count beside the chips says how much of the board you are looking at.
List / New Lead
Switch to the filterable table of the same leads, or register a lead that arrived by phone, referral or walk-in.
Driver Board
Shortcut to the recruitment pipeline. It is a separate page with its own columns, its own totals and its own rules.
Counters
Each column header shows how many cards it holds and how many of them are late on their follow-up.

The columns

New → Quoted
New, Contacted and Quoted track how far the sales conversation has got.
Negotiating
Price or terms under discussion.
On Hold
Still open, but parked — it keeps counting towards Open.
Won / Lost
Closed. A close date is stamped and the card stops counting as open or overdue.
More columns
These are just the starting set — Columns in the header lets you add your own, name it, colour it and place it anywhere in the row. Columns you add here belong to this board only.

Worth knowing

  • Each outcome column decides how long a closed card lingers — the built-in ones show {{ closed_window_days|default:"30" }} days' worth. Older closed leads are still on the Business Leads table; they have simply aged out of the board. You can change or remove that limit per column under Columns.
  • Moving a lead that came from a pricing inquiry also updates that inquiry's CRM status on the pricing inquiries page.
  • Dropping a lead in Won does not create an account. Accounts are attached by linking the lead to a real business on the Business Verification page, which marks it Won for you.
