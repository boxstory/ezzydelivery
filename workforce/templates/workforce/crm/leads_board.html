The kanban view of the lead pipeline — one column per stage, one card per prospect. There are two boards behind this page: Business is the sales pipeline for prospective clients, and Drivers is the recruitment funnel for people applying to drive for us. Both hold the same kind of record as the Leads table; the board is where you move work along, the table is where you filter and count. Dragging a card between columns saves the new stage straight away.

What you can do here

Board tabs
Switch between the business sales pipeline and the driver recruitment pipeline. Each has its own columns and its own totals.
Drag a card
Drops it into the new stage and saves immediately. If the save fails the card slides back to where it came from and you get an error.
Open a card
Click it (or press Enter on it) to open the lead detail page with the timeline, notes and WhatsApp conversation.
Chat chip
Shown only on cards whose number has WhatsApp history on record; it jumps straight to that conversation on the detail page.
Search
Filters the cards by company, contact person, phone or product category as you type.
Filters
Narrow by source and by assignee, including Assigned to me and Unassigned. Reset clears them.
List / New Lead
Switch to the filterable table of the same leads, or register a lead that arrived by phone, referral or walk-in.
Counters
Each column header shows how many cards it holds and how many of them are late on their follow-up.

Business columns

New → Quoted
New, Contacted and Quoted track how far the sales conversation has got.
Negotiating
Price or terms under discussion.
On Hold
Still open, but parked — it keeps counting towards Open.
Won / Lost
Closed. A close date is stamped and the card stops counting as open or overdue.

Driver columns

New / Applied
New Application is a driver we know of but who has not submitted; Applied means the form is in.
Incomplete
Registered but the application was never submitted.
Uploads done
Every section and document is in — ready for a staff decision.
Under Review
A staff member has picked it up and is checking the documents.
Approved / Rejected
The decision columns. They write back to the driver's real verification status.
More columns
These seven are just the starting set — Columns in the header lets you add your own, name it, colour it, place it anywhere in the row and choose which applicants it collects.

Worth knowing

  • Each outcome column decides how long a closed card lingers — the built-in ones show {{ closed_window_days|default:"30" }} days' worth. Older closed leads are still on the Leads table; they have simply aged out of the board. You can change or remove that limit per column under Columns.
  • The driver board rebuilds itself every time it loads: a card is created for every driver application and each card is snapped to the column whose conditions that applicant matches. Your moves always stick. If you move a driver card somewhere its application status does not justify, the card is pinned (thumbtack icon) and auto-filing stops for that card until you press Resume auto-filing on the lead page. If the column you drop it in sets the driver's real status, no pin is needed — reality now agrees, so the card keeps tracking the application. A column with no conditions is never auto-filed at all.
  • Dragging a driver card into a column that sets the driver's real status — the built-in Approved, Rejected and Under Review, plus any you configure that way — changes the applicant's verification status and sends them a WhatsApp message. You are asked to confirm first, and a rejection can carry a reason — but once sent, the message cannot be taken back.
  • Moving a lead that came from a pricing inquiry also updates that inquiry's CRM status on the pricing inquiries page.
  • Dropping a business lead in Won does not create an account. Accounts are attached by linking the lead to a real business on the Business Verification page, which marks it Won for you.
