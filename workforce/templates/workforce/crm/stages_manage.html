Each row below is one column on the board, left to right. On the driver board a column can file cards by itself: tick the applicant conditions it should catch and any driver matching one lands there on the next board load. Conditions are checked from the rightmost column back, so an outcome column beats a progress column. A column with no conditions ticked is a manual lane — cards only move in and out by dragging, and nothing overwrites them.
