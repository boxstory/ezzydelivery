The kanban view of the driver recruitment funnel — one column per stage, one card per applicant. Business (client) prospects live on their own board under CRM Business in the sidebar; nothing on this page shows them. This board rebuilds itself from the real applicant pool every time it loads: a card exists for every driver application, and each card is snapped to the column whose conditions that applicant matches — unless you have pinned it.

What you can do here

Drag a card
Drops it into the new stage and saves immediately. If the save fails the card slides back to where it came from and you get an error.
Open a card
Click it (or press Enter on it) to open the lead detail page with the timeline, notes and WhatsApp conversation.
Pin marker
A thumbtack means staff moved this card somewhere the applicant's real status does not justify, so auto-filing has stopped for it. Press Resume auto-filing on the lead page to hand it back.
Hand marker
A manual column — it has no conditions, so cards stay exactly where staff put them and are never auto-filed.
Chat chip
Shown only on cards whose number has WhatsApp history on record; it jumps straight to that conversation on the detail page.
Search & filters
Filter by name, phone, source and assignee. Whatever is engaged is named in a chip on the right of the bar — press its ✕ to drop that one filter, or Reset to clear the lot.
List / Verification
The filterable table of the same applicants, or the document review queue where the verification decision is actually made.
Business Board
Shortcut to the sales pipeline. It is a separate page with its own columns and its own totals.

The columns

New / Applied
New Application is a driver we know of but who has not submitted; Applied means the form is in.
Incomplete
Registered but the application was never submitted.
Uploads done
Every section and document is in — ready for a staff decision.
Under Review
A staff member has picked it up and is checking the documents.
Approved / Rejected
The decision columns. They write back to the driver's real verification status.
More columns
These seven are just the starting set — Columns in the header lets you add your own, name it, colour it, place it anywhere in the row and choose which applicants it collects.

Worth knowing

  • Each outcome column decides how long a closed card lingers — the built-in ones show {{ closed_window_days|default:"30" }} days' worth. Older closed applicants are still on the Driver Leads table; they have simply aged out of the board.
  • Your moves always stick. If you move a card somewhere its application status does not justify, the card is pinned and auto-filing stops for it until you press Resume auto-filing on the lead page. If the column you drop it in sets the driver's real status, no pin is needed — reality now agrees, so the card keeps tracking the application.
  • Dragging a card into a column that sets the driver's real status — the built-in Approved, Rejected and Under Review, plus any you configure that way — changes the applicant's verification status and sends them a WhatsApp message. You are asked to confirm first, and a rejection can carry a reason — but once sent, the message cannot be taken back.
