01CORE SERVICES
What Ezzy Delivery does — 12 FAQs
1. What services does Ezzy Delivery Qatar provide?
We offer: E-commerce fulfillment with last-mile delivery, B2B/B2C distribution, personal and corporate storage/warehousing, document archiving, and freight forwarding. We are a full-service logistics partner for businesses in Qatar.

2. Is Ezzy Delivery only for e-commerce businesses?
No. While e-commerce fulfillment is our specialty, we serve any business that needs delivery, storage, or distribution in Qatar — from Instagram sellers to large corporations, from one-time personal deliveries to daily high-volume operations.

3. What is e-commerce fulfillment and how does it work?
E-commerce fulfillment means we handle the entire post-sale logistics for your online store. You ship your inventory to our warehouse. When a customer orders, we pick the product, pack it, and deliver it to them. You focus on sales; we handle delivery.

4. Do you offer warehousing and inventory storage?
Yes. We provide personal storage, corporate/bulk warehousing, and secure document archiving. Your inventory is stored safely in our facility and we fulfill orders directly from the warehouse as they arrive.

5. Can you handle B2B distribution?
Absolutely. We do both B2B (business to business) and B2C (business to consumer) distribution. Whether you need products delivered to retail shops, offices, or end customers, we have the fleet and infrastructure to handle it.

6. Do you offer freight forwarding?
Yes. We coordinate freight forwarding for goods moving into and within Qatar. For international shipments, we work with trusted logistics partners to ensure smooth customs clearance and delivery.

7. Can you handle food and perishable deliveries?
Our primary focus is non-food items (93% of our orders are non-food). However, we handle select food deliveries on a case-by-case basis. Contact us with your specific requirements — temperature-sensitive items may require special arrangements.

8. Do you deliver fragile or high-value items?
Yes. We handle perfumes, electronics, glassware, cosmetics, and other fragile products regularly. We recommend secure packaging before handover, and our riders are trained to handle items with care.

9. Can you handle document and archiving services?
Yes. We offer document archiving and storage for businesses that need secure, organized offsite storage of records, contracts, and files. Retrieval can be arranged on demand.

10. Do you offer personal (non-business) deliveries?
Our service is primarily designed for businesses. However, for personal pick-and-drop deliveries, contact us to check availability. We are expanding this segment progressively.

11. What product categories do you handle most?
Based on our client base: Clothing and fashion (17%), Electronics and gadgets (7%), Cosmetics and beauty (4%), Gift items and hampers (4%), Footwear (4%), Perfumes and oud, Medical supplies, and many more. If it fits in a parcel, we can deliver it.

12. What makes Ezzy Delivery different from other couriers in Qatar?
We are not just a courier — we are a full logistics partner. We offer warehousing + fulfillment + last-mile delivery as one integrated service. Most couriers only do delivery. We also offer competitive rates starting at 15 QAR, COD collection at no extra charge, and same-day delivery across Qatar.

02PRICING & RATES
The #3 most-asked topic — 10 FAQs
13. How much does a standard delivery cost?
15 QAR for deliveries within Doha. 25 QAR for deliveries outside Doha (Industrial Area, Al Khor, Al Wakrah outskirts, etc.). These are our standard rates for regular-sized parcels.

14. Do you offer bulk delivery discounts?
Yes. For clients with 20+ daily deliveries, we offer customized volume pricing. The more you ship, the lower the per-delivery rate. Submit your volume details via our enquiry form for a custom quote.

15. What is included in the delivery price?
The delivery price includes: pickup from your location, delivery to the customer, COD collection (if applicable), delivery confirmation, and basic handling. No hidden fees.

16. Are there extra charges for COD collection?
No. Cash on Delivery collection is included in the standard delivery rate. We collect payment from your customer and remit it to you at no additional charge.

17. How does pricing compare to competitors?
Our 15 QAR Doha rate is very competitive. One international client noted: “In the UAE I pay 13-15 AED per shipment.” Our Qatar rate is comparable, and we include COD and returns handling in our fulfillment packages.

18. Is there a monthly subscription or setup fee?
No setup fee and no monthly subscription required for basic delivery service. You pay per delivery. For full fulfillment services (warehousing + order processing + delivery), pricing is customized based on your volume and storage needs.

19. What are the charges for return deliveries?
Returns are only chargeable when our rider has already reached the customer location and the delivery is refused. If a return is initiated before dispatch, no charge applies. Full return logistics are included in our fulfillment packages.

20. Do you charge for failed delivery attempts?
If the customer is unreachable and the rider has traveled to the delivery location, a charge may apply for the attempted delivery. We always try to contact the customer by phone before marking a delivery as failed.

21. Can I get a custom quote for my business?
Absolutely. Fill out our enquiry form at whatsform.com/rtflsh with your product details, daily volume, and delivery areas. Our team will prepare a tailored pricing proposal within hours.

22. Do prices change during holidays or peak seasons?
Our standard rates remain consistent. During peak seasons (Ramadan, Eid, National Day), delivery times may adjust but pricing stays the same. Plan ahead for peak periods to ensure capacity.

07COD & PAYMENT
Cash on Delivery and billing — 8 FAQs
58. Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes. COD is one of our core services and is included at no extra charge. Our rider collects the payment from your customer at the point of delivery.

59. How and when do I receive my COD payments?
COD collections are settled regularly via bank transfer (QNB or other Qatar banks). Settlement frequency (daily, weekly, or on-demand) is agreed during onboarding. You receive clear statements of all collections.

60. What if a customer refuses to pay or refuses delivery?
The parcel is returned. A return charge applies only when the rider has already reached the customer location. We notify you immediately of any refused deliveries so you can follow up with the customer.

61. What payment methods do you accept for your services?
We accept bank transfers (QNB and other Qatar banks), cash payments, and monthly invoicing for regular business clients. Payment terms are discussed during onboarding.

62. Do you provide invoices and delivery statements?
Yes. Business clients receive regular invoices and detailed delivery statements showing all orders, COD collections, and charges. These are provided on your agreed billing cycle.

63. Can the customer pay by card instead of cash?
Currently, COD is cash-based. Card payment at delivery is not yet available but is being explored. Customers who prefer card payment can pay the seller directly online before delivery.

64. How do you ensure COD money is safe and accounted for?
Every COD collection is logged against a specific order. Our riders report collections in real time. You receive itemized statements, and settlements are made via traceable bank transfers. Full transparency.

65. What is the maximum COD amount you can collect?
We handle a wide range of COD amounts, from small orders (30–50 QAR) to high-value items (1,000+ QAR). For very high-value COD, contact us to arrange appropriate handling.

09RETURNS, COMPLAINTS & ISSUES
Problem resolution — 8 FAQs
71. How do returns work?
When a customer wants to return a product: you notify us, we pick up from the customer, and return-deliver to your location. Return delivery charges apply. We coordinate the entire reverse logistics process.

72. Why are returns charged at full delivery price?
A return involves a separate trip (pickup from customer + delivery back to seller). This is effectively a new delivery. For high-volume fulfillment clients, return costs can be negotiated into the overall package rate.

73. What is your policy on lost or missing parcels?
Lost parcels are extremely rare. If a parcel cannot be located, we investigate immediately, tracing the delivery chain. We take full responsibility for items in our custody and work to resolve any loss or discrepancy.

74. How do I file a complaint about a delivery?
Contact us directly on WhatsApp or call our support line. We take every complaint seriously and aim to resolve issues within the same business day. Your feedback drives our service improvement.

75. What if my delivery was sent to the wrong address?
Contact us immediately. We will redirect the rider if the delivery is still in progress, or arrange re-pickup and correct delivery if already dropped off. Wrong-address cases are rare thanks to our address verification process.

76. Can I cancel a delivery after it has been dispatched?
If the rider has not yet reached the destination, we can attempt to recall the delivery. If the rider is already at the customer location, the delivery/return charge applies. Contact us as early as possible for cancellations.

77. What is your complaint rate?
Based on analysis of 4,474 customer messages, complaints represent approximately 2.5% of all conversations. The most common topics are delivery timing and communication. We continuously work to improve.

78. Do you offer insurance or liability coverage for parcels?
Basic handling care is included in all deliveries. For high-value items, we recommend discussing additional coverage arrangements. We are exploring formal insurance partnerships to offer this as an add-on service.
