## 05:47 — Fix site-wide homepage-canonical bug + purge login-gated sitemap URLs
GSC-style audit revealed 43 of 67 sitemap pages (all 21 SEO landing pages, help/FAQ, about, services, pricing, blog index) emitted `<link rel="canonical" href="https://ezzydelivery.qa">` — telling Google they were duplicates of the homepage and excluding them from the index. Root cause: `get_page_meta()` defaulted `url` to SITE_URL and none of the 32 meta helpers passed `url=`. Fixed by leaving canonical/og:url empty in `get_page_meta()` when no url is given, with head.html falling back to a self-referencing `scheme://host/path` canonical (query-string-free; also applied to the no-seo-context fallback branch). Also removed the 3 login-gated URLs (/business/all/, both workflow guides) from the sitemap by deleting BusinessPagesSitemap/WorkforcePagesSitemap. Re-crawled all 64 sitemap URLs: every page now has a correct self-referencing canonical and og:url, zero issues.
