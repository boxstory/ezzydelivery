## 09:20 — CRM: fix WhatsApp chat matching gap + manual "Link WhatsApp Chat" widget
Root cause found: pricing-inquiry leads with real WhatsApp chats weren't showing the board's "Chat" pill because (1) `normalize_phone()` didn't strip a leading `00` international prefix and phone-variant matching only handled exact 8/974+8-digit numbers, and (2) many of those chats are indexed by WhatsApp's privacy LID rather than the phone JID, and had never been synced into `WhatsAppMessage` at all. Fixed the normalization/variant matching, added a reusable `pull_chat_history()` WAHA-fetch helper (`whatsapp/waha_backfill.py`), and ran a new `backfill_lead_wa_chats` management command against all 89 pricing-inquiry leads — connected 59 previously-unmatched leads, pulling in 724 messages. Also added a manual override: `Lead.wa_chat_override` field + a "Link WhatsApp Chat" search/typeahead widget on the lead detail page (searches the WhatsAppContact directory, POSTs to `crm_lead_link_chat`, which saves the override and immediately backfills that chat) for leads where the phone still doesn't auto-match.

**Files:** `crm/models.py`, `crm/services.py`, `crm/management/commands/backfill_lead_wa_chats.py`, `whatsapp/waha_backfill.py`, `workforce/crm_views.py`, `workforce/urls.py`, `workforce/templates/workforce/crm/lead_detail.html`, `workforce/static/workforce/js/crm_lead_detail.js`, `workforce/static/workforce/css/crm.css`
