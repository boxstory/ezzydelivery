## 17:30 — P2P booking: the booker may be the sender or the receiver
The booking flow assumed the person signed in was always the one handing the parcel over — their name and phone prefilled the pickup block, and the WhatsApp confirm link went to `sender_phone`. Half the real bookings are the other way round (someone orders a delivery to themselves), so the form now opens with "Is this parcel going out or coming to you?", moves the profile prefill to whichever end they pick, and marks that block "This is you". `P2PBooking` gained `booker_role` and froze both people (`receiver_name`, `receiver_phone`) instead of only the sender; every message now goes to `booker_phone`, so a sender who never asked us for anything is not handed the link that releases the job.

Who pays the delivery fee became its own question rather than falling out of the role: `fee_payer` is sender-pays-at-pickup or receiver-pays-on-delivery, defaulting to whoever booked until they say otherwise. The driver side follows it — the pickup card only asks for cash on a sender-pays job (and says "Fee paid on delivery" otherwise), and a receiver-pays job grows a matching "Collect delivery fee at the door" row with a Mark-collected button on the task card, backed by a new `p2p_mark_fee_collected_at_delivery` endpoint.
