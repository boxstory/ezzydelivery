# Dev Log — 2026-10-01

## 09:35 — WA inbox: sends feel instant
Sending from /waha/wa-chats/ was slow because the button waited for the server, and the server queued the send behind inbox refreshes. Each refresh holds one of only 3 gunicorn workers while WAHA fetches messages (p95 12s, max 38s); WAHA's own sendText takes ~100ms. The composer now draws the bubble at once with a pending clock, then marks it sent (✓) or failed (! and the text goes back in the box). Inbox tabs in the background no longer poll, and they catch up when shown again.

**Files:** `whatsapp/wa_chats_view.py`

## 10:55 — WhatsApp: marketing-only labels + staff login on the inbox
Super admins can now tick WhatsApp labels per number at /workforce/whatsapp/label-access/. Chats carrying a ticked label are hidden from staff outside the Marketing department, in Live Chats and the CRM (lead conversation, number picker, AI summary, chat refresh, preview, media, driver thread). The inbox now needs a Django staff login on every endpoint, its POSTs are CSRF-checked, and the browser gets the chat list, labels and read receipts through Django instead of straight from WAHA. Label membership is read from WAHA with a 2-minute cache and a 24-hour fallback copy; if it can't be read at all, staff outside marketing are refused. Still open: nginx keeps proxying WAHA's chat API to anyone with the htpasswd until the catch-all /waha/ block is narrowed (needs sudo; snippet given to the user).

The WAHA webhook and the inbox Resync button saved messages without removing verification codes. Six stored rows had readable codes, all forwarded to our numbers.
