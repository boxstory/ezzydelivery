## 22:07 — Invoice print preview didn't match the screen: two global print leaks
The printed invoice came out with gridlines around every cell, a grey header row and a broken
`#` column, none of which exist on screen. Root cause was two unscoped `@media print` blocks that
`wf_dashboard_base` loads on every workforce page: `workforce.css` paints
`th, td { border: 1px solid #ddd; padding: 8px }` plus a grey `th` (dead leftover — nothing uses
its `.receipt-total` anymore), and `fleet_transactions.css` had `[class*="col-"]
{ display: block !important }` for Bootstrap grid columns, whose substring match also caught the
ledger's `bpi__col-idx` and blew those cells out of the table. Anchored the column selector to
`[class^="col-"], [class*=" col-"]`, fenced the invoice sheet off the border/background leak, and
gave the tfoot totals row a full padding shorthand so it stays column-aligned. Verified by
print-rendering the real logged-in page to PDF, not a replica — the earlier replica only loaded
the two invoice stylesheets and so never reproduced either leak.

**Files:** `workforce/static/workforce/css/cod_payout_invoice.css`, `workforce/static/workforce/css/fleet_transactions.css`, `templates/wf_dashboard_base.html`
