## 08:45 — Driver availability & work preference: closed all remaining gaps
Wired work preference and availability through the whole stack. Drivers can now edit job type + preferred hours in the PWA Settings (new Work Preference card; also fixed a latent bug where submitting the availability form silently turned off task notifications). Staff can edit work preference inline on driver_detail (new driver_set_work_pref endpoint) and filter the drivers list by work preference and working hours (also shown under the availability badge). API: job_type/work_time_slabs added to driver serializers plus a new POST /api/driver/work-preference/ endpoint. Behavior: new-task WhatsApp broadcasts now skip drivers outside their preferred time slabs (current_work_time_slab helper, blank = always); accept_task auto-flips offline/on-break drivers to available; assignment dropdowns (api_drivers_list + dispatch RiderShiftForm) sort available drivers first and label availability/job type. New hourly cron `expire_stale_availability` sets drivers offline after 12h with no GPS ping/activity/update and no active task (dry-run found 2 genuinely stale drivers). Bonus: fixed dispatch/forms.py which crashed on import (PickupLocation.is_active and RiderShift.STATUS_CHOICES don't exist → pickup_status='active' / SHIFT_STATUS).
