## 01:10 — Seller "Ready to Pickup" now confirms and publishes in one action
The client dashboard used to show two overlapping bulk actions, Confirmed (`ready_to_pickup`) and Publish. They are two halves of the same handover — `ready_to_pickup` reserves stock, builds the pick list and opens the first-mile leg but never reaches the fleet, while `publish` creates the delivery task but never reserves anything — so sellers routinely fell into one gap or the other. The seller now has a single **Ready to Pickup** button that posts a new `ready_and_publish` token; the server runs both transitions in order as one unit, and a bare `publish` from a non-staff user is refused. Staff keep the two as separate actions in the workforce console.

Two supporting defects were fixed. The staff row Publish button was gated on `to_publish`, a status that does not exist in `ORDER_STATUS_BY_CLIENT`, so orders sitting at `ready_to_pickup` showed no per-row Publish button even though `/workforce/orders/to_publish/` lists them. And the first-mile dispositions built the delivery leg through `_ensure_delivery_task` without ever touching `order_status`, leaving self-delivered orders reading "Ready to Pickup" while a driver held the parcel — that now syncs to `publish`. Four regression tests added; the kanban Published column is no longer a drop target and a latent `draggedCard`-is-null bug in its drop handler was fixed along the way.
