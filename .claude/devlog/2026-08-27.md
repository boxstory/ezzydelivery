## 11:19 — Fix "WhatsApp send failed" on CRM leads: missing country code
Sends from the lead composer were failing with a useless generic error. The send log showed why: the lead
page passed `lead.phone` raw, and leads are stored in the local 8-digit form, so Evolution probed
`55397661@s.whatsapp.net`, found nothing and returned 400. Confirmed against Evolution's number-check
endpoint — `55397661` does not exist, `97455397661` does. Normalisation now happens in
`send_routed_message`, the one funnel every routed send passes through, so no calling page can lose the
country code again; the lead and pricing-inquiry openers also render the number through `whatsapp_number`
so the modal displays what it will dial. Separately, a failed Evolution send now carries a real reason
("55397661 is not on WhatsApp — check the number and that it carries the country code") instead of every
composer falling back to "WhatsApp send failed" — the unreachable-recipient body is a list of jid probes,
a shape the old `_failure_reason` could not read.

## 12:08 — Pricing engine: multi-select answers, same-day pickup, pickup type
Half of every pricing inquiry ever taken priced its speed dimension at zero: speed, parcel size and pickup type are multi-select bubbles on the public form ("Express (Few Hours), Same Day") but `rank_of` only matched a single exact string, so the rank came back None and no surcharge fired. Added `rank_of_multi` (highest rank wins — you must be resourced for express even if you also offer standard), added the `Next Day` and `2-3 Days` answers the rank table never had, and gave two normalised-but-unpriced inputs real rules: `same_day_required` (+5 QR, dedicated leg) and a new `pickup_type_rank` so a home collection costs more than a fulfilment dock (+2 QR, commercial premises pay nothing). Amounts are placeholders seeded on rate card v1 and editable in admin. 11 new tests; 60/60 pricing tests pass.

## 12:48 — Rate card v2: calibrated to what we actually bill
v1 was suggesting 40-48 QR for leads the desk quotes at 20 flat, and the +60% cap — not the rules — was setting the price on 55% of leads. Checked the ledger: across 673 verified deliveries we have only ever billed 20 or 25 QR, and the median is 20 in every distance band under 30 km, so the rate tracks the account, not the kilometre. Built rate card v2 accordingly: the base is flat across Doha and moves on volume (25 low / 20 mid / 15 high), steps to 30-35 beyond Doha, and volume alone still tiers when distance is unanswered instead of falling back to a flat 25. Surcharges now cover only the rare exceptions (express, bulky, 15 kg+, special handling, six or more pickup points) — same-day delivery, COD, returns, home pickup and multiple daily pickups are priced into the base, because 44-66% of leads tick each of them and a fee nearly everyone pays is a higher base with extra steps. Median suggestion fell 40 → 25, 91 of 122 leads now land at or under 25, and nothing hits the cap. v1 is retired but kept so old suggestions stay explainable. The Pricing Check panel reads the active card and labels those rows "in the base". 70/70 pricing tests pass.
