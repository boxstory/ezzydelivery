# Dev Log — 2026-09-20

## 09:52 — Pickup area is now mandatory on the pricing inquiry form
Step 3 of the 3PL pricing inquiry let sellers through with an empty "Pickup Location Area", which left sales with no idea where to collect from and no way to pick a rate band. Made the field required both in the step-3 JS check and in the server-side PRICING_REQUIRED_FIELDS (the JS alone is bypassable), and marked the label with the usual red asterisk. Sellers with no address of their own in Qatar now get a one-tap suggestion button under the field that fills in "Ezzy Fulfillment Center" — it appears when they answered "No, outside Qatar" in step 1 or picked Fulfillment Center as the pickup type, and the hint line above it changes to match. Also added the missing type_of_pickup_location server-side check, which the JS enforced but the view did not. Verified end to end: a blank submit re-renders step 3 with the error and leaves is_complete False, filling it redirects to the quote page.

**Files:** `webpages/templates/webpages/delivery_pricing_inquiry.html`, `webpages/views.py`, `webpages/static/webpages/css/delivery_pricing_inquiry.css`

## 10:47 — Over-long answers no longer lose a 3PL pricing inquiry
The pricing form wrote POST strings straight onto the model, so an answer longer than its column (a pasted tracking URL past 200 chars) raised DataError in PostgreSQL and 500'd the step — three live submissions hit this at 09:39–09:41 today and lost everything typed. All three step-save helpers now go through `webpages/pricing/answers.py`, which sanitises each answer, saves it cut to the column width, and keeps the full text on the new `PricingEnquiry.trimmed_answers` field so sales still has what was actually submitted. Nothing is ever rejected for being too long: the seller gets a live amber warning under the field while typing, a "we shortened these — please shorten them yourself" notice on the form and again on the quote page, and staff get a "Shortened on save" panel on the inquiry detail page printing the full value. Verified on the live site: a 540-character website URL now advances from step 1 with the notice shown (the test row was deleted afterwards), and 9 new tests in `webpages/tests_pricing.py` cover the clamp, the notice clearing when they fix it, and a complete submission.
