## 16:15 — Driver GPS: fixed the tracking gaps, and cut battery use while doing it
Investigated why driver locations weren't landing reliably. The pings were fine while a driver page was open (median gap 25s) but the trail had holes up to 13 hours, because tracking only ever existed in the foreground: `watchPosition` stopped when the phone locked, the 30s timer got throttled and then frozen, the service worker had no replay path, and any fix worse than 100m accuracy was thrown away rather than sent. Fixes were also stamped with the server's receipt time, so a cached position from four minutes ago was recorded as current.

Rebuilt `EzzyGPS` around three power profiles instead of a permanent high-accuracy watch. Fixes are now taken on a duty cycle so the GPS radio sleeps in between; high accuracy is spent only while a task is actually in flight (decided server-side via a new `driver_on_duty` flag, so it holds across every PWA page, not just the navigation screen); an idle driver is located from cell/wifi every 3 minutes and a stopped one every 2.5. The tasks map had a second continuous watch running alongside the first — it now rides on the fixes EzzyGPS already takes. Net effect is less battery than before, not more.

Coarse fixes are stored instead of dropped, with `DriverLocation.fixed_at` recording when the device actually took the fix and `latest_for_driver()` preferring a precise one so nothing that stamps a position (status points, public tracking, business live map) degrades. Failed pings queue in IndexedDB and replay in batches through Background Sync, so a dead zone no longer loses them permanently. The live map now separates "app has gone quiet" from "driver hasn't moved", which previously looked identical. Keep-screen-on is available on the navigation screen but is opt-in and off by default — it costs far more battery than the GPS does.

Note: the model field went live ~1 minute before its migration ran, producing 6 `fixed_at does not exist` errors in `error.log` between 18:58 and 18:59 (log time). Resolved once the migration applied; none since.
