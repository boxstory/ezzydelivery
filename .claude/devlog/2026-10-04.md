## 10:05 — Live map draws a silent driver at their last GPS fix, not a customer's door
DR001 showed at a Friday address although their phone reported all Saturday: the live map only counts pings from the last 2 hours, and for anyone quieter it fell back to the delivery address of whichever task the queryset returned first, delivered jobs included, with no time shown. ## 10:05 — Driver app locks behind a full-screen "Turn on location" screen
Approved drivers can no longer use the app with location off or blocked. A full-screen navy screen covers every driver page, on both the PWA and the older /delivery/ pages, with step-by-step instructions for Android or iPhone, a "Turn on location" button and a WhatsApp support link. It lifts by itself on the first GPS fix. It never polls GPS itself: it listens to EzzyGPS, which now emits `ezzy:gps-error` on every failure and has `recheck()` for the button. "Position unavailable" only locks after 90 seconds with no fix, so a driver in a basement is not locked mid-delivery. Applicants and staff never see it, and `DRIVER_GPS_REQUIRED=False` in .env switches it off fleet-wide. Prompted by driver 1376 delivering all day with no GPS stored since 27 Sep (685 of 712 app switches carried no position).

## 13:54 — Group WhatsApp media downloads only on click
The inbox no longer downloads group-chat media by itself. The per-minute `archive_wa_media` cron skips any message whose id carries `@g.us`, group resyncs and the `backfill_waha` command ask WAHA with `downloadMedia=false`, and the inbox shows a "⬇ Photo · 93 KB" button in place of group photos, videos and stickers (voice notes and documents load only when played or opened). A click sends `?fetch=1`, and `media_archive.fetch_on_demand` asks WAHA for a fresh copy from WhatsApp and archives it; this works for months-old messages (checked on real rows from 2 days to 8 weeks back). The side panel's Photos/Videos grid shows a download tile for unsaved group files. Person chats are unchanged. Checked on the live inbox in Chromium: opening a group sent no media fetches, and one click sent one.
