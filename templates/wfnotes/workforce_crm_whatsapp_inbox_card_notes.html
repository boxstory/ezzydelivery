The triage queue for people who message our business WhatsApp and are not yet anybody we know. One row per sender, showing their latest message and how many they have sent. Numbers that already belong to a client account, to an open lead, or to a staff or driver profile are filtered out, as is anything previously dismissed — so what is left is genuinely new. Working this list means opening each conversation and deciding one of three things: it is a prospect (promote it to a lead), it is someone wanting to drive for us (promote it as a driver lead), or it is noise (dismiss it).

What you can do here

Session banner
Shows which WhatsApp number the bridge is logged in as, and whether it is connected. If it is not, nothing new is arriving.
Search
Filters the queue by phone number.
Conversation
Opens the recent thread with that sender, both directions, including photos, voice notes and attachments. All the decisions are made from inside it.
Promote
Creates a business lead from the number, carrying over the contact's name and their last few messages as notes. You stay on the inbox and the row picks up a lead reference.
Driver Lead
The same thing, but filed on the driver recruitment board instead of the sales board.
Not a lead
Dismisses the sender. The row disappears and that number never shows up in this queue again.
Resync
Pulls recent chats straight from WhatsApp for senders whose messages never reached us, then refreshes the contact directory and reloads the page.
Contacts
The synced directory of names, numbers and lid aliases — use it when a sender shows as a long unrecognisable number.

Reading a row

Number
The contact's name when we have one, their real number, and underneath it the WhatsApp alias the message actually arrived under.
Last message
The most recent text. When the message had no text you get a label instead — Photo, Voice note, Document, Location and so on.
Msgs
How many messages this sender has sent in total. A single message is usually a wrong number; a long thread rarely is.
Lead #
This sender already has an open lead. Open the conversation and use the link through to it rather than promoting again.

Worth knowing

  • Dismissing is permanent from this page. It is recorded against every form of the number, so the sender cannot resurface under a variant — reversing it needs the Django admin.
  • Promoting a number that already has an open lead reuses that lead instead of creating a second one, so you cannot accidentally split one prospect in two.
  • Group chats and status broadcasts are filtered out of this queue — it only ever lists direct senders.
  • When the notice about the bridge being switched off is showing, no new inbound messages are being captured and everything below is history.
  • Resync works to a time limit and can finish only part way through; run it again if the sender you expected is still missing.
  • WhatsApp deletes its own copies of media within minutes, so older photos and files in a preview may no longer open.
