This is the flat table of the whole lead pipeline — one row per prospect the company is tracking, whichever way they arrived. Leads are raised automatically from a 3PL pricing inquiry, from the WhatsApp quick-inquiry form, from a driver application, or by staff promoting an unknown number on the WhatsApp Inbox. The same records appear as cards on the Leads Board; this view is the one to use when you want to filter, count or hunt for a specific name. Nothing is edited here — Open takes you to the lead detail page where every change is made.

What you can do here

Pipeline tabs
All, Business and Drivers split the table by lead type. The tab also drives the four tallies and which board the Board button opens.
Search
Matches company name, contact person, phone and product category. Filters as you type.
Filters
Narrow by stage, by source, by assignee (including Assigned to me and Unassigned), and to overdue follow-ups only. Reset clears everything including the tab.
Tallies
Total, Open, Overdue and Won for the active tab.
Board
Switches to the kanban view of the same leads, where stage is changed by dragging.
New Lead
Registers a lead that reached you by phone, referral or walk-in. It opens pre-set to the tab you are on.
Open
The lead detail page — stage, assignee, follow-up date, notes, activity timeline and the WhatsApp conversation.

Where a lead comes from

Pricing Inquiry
The 3PL pricing form on the public site. Its stage stays in step with the inquiry's own CRM status.
WhatsApp Form
The short quick-inquiry form on the website.
WhatsApp Inbound
An unknown number that messaged the business WhatsApp and was promoted from the inbox.
Manual
Typed in by staff, and also how driver applications enter the pipeline.

Stages

Open stages
New, Contacted, Quoted, Negotiating and On Hold — anything not yet decided.
Won / Lost
Closed. A close date is stamped, the lead stops counting as open, and it can no longer be flagged overdue.

Worth knowing

  • A row is flagged overdue only when its follow-up date has passed and the lead is still open. Closing a lead clears the flag.
  • The four tallies follow the tab only — they ignore your search, stage, source and assignee filters, so they will not match the row count below.
  • Each row shows the stage name from its own board, so a driver row reads Applied or Uploads Completed while a business row on the same underlying stage reads Contacted or Quoted. The Stage filter follows the tab you are on. Column names and the columns themselves are configured per board under Columns on the board page.
  • A business lead is normally marked Won for you when it is linked to a real account on the Business Verification page — you do not set it by hand.
  • Leads with a follow-up date due or overdue are pushed to their assignee in the daily WhatsApp follow-up digest, so keep the assignee and date honest.
