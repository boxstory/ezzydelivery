Goods coming back from customers after a delivery — the RMA queue. Each row is one return request against one order: what the customer is sending back, why, and how far through receiving and inspection it has got. Newest first.

Status, in order

Requested
Raised, nothing physically back yet. The warehouse is expecting it.
Received
The parcel has arrived and every line is marked received. Inspection is the next step.
Resolved
Every line has been inspected and given a disposition — restocked, held or written off. Nothing further is owed.
Rejected
The return was refused. Nothing goes back on the shelf.

Reading the list

RMA number
The reference for the whole return. It is stamped on every stock movement the return creates.
Order
The delivery the goods came from — a return always belongs to an order, never floats on its own.
Status filter
The quickest way to see what is waiting to be received versus waiting to be inspected.

Not to be confused with

  • Return tasks — those are items coming back off a cancelled order that was already picked. They never left the building.
  • Failed deliveries — stock the driver could not deliver goes back through the order pipeline, not through an RMA.
