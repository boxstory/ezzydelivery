Connects one seller to one warehouse, or edits an existing connection. The link is what allows that business's stock to be received into that building and what makes the warehouse visible to their account. Staff only.

The fields

Seller and warehouse
Both required, and both fixed once created — to change either, make a new link. Only active businesses and warehouses are offered.
Default location
Which of that warehouse's pickup/dispatch points is used for this seller. It must belong to the warehouse you picked.
Priority
0–100. Ranks this warehouse against the seller's others; higher is preferred.
Default warehouse
Marks it as the seller's main building. Give a seller exactly one, or the "main" warehouse becomes ambiguous.
Active
Switch off to suspend the arrangement without losing the record or the history behind it.
Notes
Free text — the commercial context, who agreed it, any condition worth remembering.

Good to know

  • Duplicates are refused. If a link between that seller and warehouse already exists you are sent to it to edit instead.
  • Editing cannot move a link to a different seller or warehouse — create the new one and deactivate the old.
  • Nothing about stock changes here. The link grants permission; it does not move a single unit.
