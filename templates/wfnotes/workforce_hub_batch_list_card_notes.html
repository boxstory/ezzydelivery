A hub pickup batch is the first leg of a two-leg hub delivery: one driver ride that collects several of a client's orders from a single pickup location and drops them all at a hub warehouse. Each row here is one batch. Batches are built on New Batch, driven by a fleet driver, and once the goods land at the hub each order gets its own last-mile delivery task for staff to publish and dispatch as normal.

What you can do here

New Batch
Opens the builder — pick a pickup location and hub warehouse, tick the orders that go on the ride, and optionally assign the driver up front.
Status tabs
Narrow the list to one batch status. All clears the filter. The list is always newest batch first.
Row columns
Batch number, the client pickup point, the receiving hub, the driver (or Unassigned), how many orders are on the ride, and what the driver is being paid for it.
View
Opens the batch — that is where you assign or change the driver, edit earnings, move the status by hand, cancel the batch, and see each order's last-mile task.

Batch status

Pending
Built, but no driver on it yet. Nobody is going to collect it.
Assigned
A driver has been put on the ride but has not accepted it.
Accepted
The driver has taken the ride.
In Progress
Driver is on the way to the client's pickup point.
Arrived
Driver is at the pickup location.
Collected
All packages are in the driver's hands, heading to the hub.
At Hub
Goods handed over at the warehouse. This is what triggers the last-mile tasks. Final state.
Cancelled
The ride was called off. Final state.

Worth knowing

  • Only orders flagged as hub delivery, already verified, and not already sitting in another batch can be put on a ride.
  • Moving a batch to At Hub auto-creates one last-mile delivery task per order and flips those orders to ready-to-pickup. The tasks land for review and unpublished — you still have to publish and dispatch each one.
  • At Hub and Cancelled are terminal. Once a batch is in either state the driver, earnings and status can no longer be changed from the batch page.
  • Driver earnings is a single flat figure for the whole ride, not a per-order rate.
  • No cash is collected on this leg — any COD on those orders is collected by the last-mile driver.
