The ledger of every stock movement in the warehouse, newest first. Each row is one change to one product at one location, with the quantity before and after it. Nothing here can be edited — that is the point: this is the audit trail you check when a stock figure is disputed.

Reading a row

Transaction #
The reference the system gave this movement. Quote it when asking someone to look into a discrepancy.
Type
What caused the movement — receiving, picking, put-away, a count adjustment, a return to shelf, and so on.
Quantity
How many units moved. Whether that added or removed stock is clear from the before and after columns.
Before / after
The balance at that location either side of the change. If after minus before does not match the quantity, something else touched the row in between.
Notes
Free text captured at the time — typically the receipt reference, the received date, or whatever the operator typed.

What you can do here

Filters
Narrow by movement type and by warehouse. They combine, so you can isolate, say, every count adjustment in one building.
Per page
Fifty rows by default. Raise it when scanning a long run of movements rather than hunting one.
One product only
To follow a single item, open its stock card from Inventory instead — same data, scoped to that product.

Good to know

  • Rows are never deleted or amended. A wrong movement is fixed by a new, opposite movement, so both stay visible.
  • A seller account sees only its linked warehouses, so its view of the same period is a subset of the staff view.
