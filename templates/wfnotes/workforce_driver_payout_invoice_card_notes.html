The payout document for one driver settlement — every earning being paid, what came off it, and the net handed over. It is written when a payout is created on the driver's payout desk, and it is the sheet the driver signs. Each line is a published earning or bonus that was ticked at payout time; nothing on this page can be changed afterwards.

What you can do here

Print invoice
Prints or saves just the sheet — the toolbar and these notes are left out of the printed page.
Back
Returns to this driver's transaction list, where every line on the invoice can be traced individually.
Payout code
The settlement reference. Quote it when a driver queries a payment.

Reading the sheet

Lines
One row per earning or bonus, with the delivery task it came from and the day it was booked.
Period
The window the settlement covers. Lines are dated by when the earning was booked, not when the delivery happened.
Deductions
Anything held back from this payout, each on its own line with its description.
Net paid
Total earnings less deductions. This is the figure that was actually handed over or transferred.
Reference
The transfer or voucher number typed when the payout was created. Blank means none was entered.

Worth knowing

  • COD never appears here, by design. Cash the driver collects is money they hand in, not money they are owed — it settles on its own leg and has nothing to do with this document.
  • Only published earnings can reach an invoice. A delivery still sitting in the verification queue is absent because its fee was never agreed, not because it was missed.
  • An invoice is a record of money that already moved — it cannot be edited or reissued from here.
  • An empty line table means the settlement exists but no earning rows are attached to it; check the driver's transactions before paying anything on the strength of it.
