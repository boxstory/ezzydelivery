Puts one rider on one store for a block of time. That pairing is the whole point: dispatch only ever offers a batch to riders whose shift is at the same pickup location as the batch and is currently Active. Creating a shift here does not put the rider to work — it makes them eligible to clock in. The same form is used to correct a shift that has not started yet, and to move its status by hand.

The fields

Rider
Only approved drivers are listed. A driver still pending document review will not appear until they are approved.
Location
The store the rider is tied to for the shift. Active locations only, fulfilment centres listed first.
Schedule
Planned start and end, date and time. The end must be after the start or nothing is saved.
Status
Only shown when editing. Use it to correct the record — mark a shift Completed that was never clocked out, or put one back to Scheduled.
Save
A new shift gets its own code and is logged against your username; an edited shift returns you to the shift page.

What the status means

Scheduled
On the rota, not started. The rider gets no batches yet.
Active
Working. Only now can dispatch hand this rider a batch from that store.
On break
Paused; break time is taken off the hours used for orders per hour.
Completed
Finished. The nightly KPI run only looks at completed shifts.

Worth knowing

  • Setting the status to Active here does not stamp a clock-in time. Actual start and end are only recorded when the rider clocks in and out from the app, and those are the times the KPI hours are built from.
  • If something is missing, the times are unreadable, or the end is not after the start, the page comes back with a message at the top and nothing is saved — retype both times.
  • Overlapping shifts for the same rider are not blocked. Two Active shifts at different stores make the rider's eligibility unpredictable, so avoid it.
  • Editing the hours of a shift that is already running does not take back batches already given to that rider.
  • An Active shift left past its end time is closed automatically by a background check, so forcing Completed here is only for tidying up records.
