This page decides which clients can store goods in which warehouse. Each card is one warehouse; the businesses in its table are the ones allowed to hold stock there. Linking a client is what gives them a collection point in that building, so their orders can be picked from our shelves instead of collected from their own shop. Only clients with the fulfilment service switched on can be linked at all. The three tiles at the top are counts across the whole system, not just what the filters are showing.

What you can do here

Search
Matches a warehouse's name, code or city. The status dropdown narrows to active or inactive warehouses; the cross clears both.
Link Business
Opens a picker of fulfilment-enabled, active clients on that card. Submitting creates the link immediately and reloads the page.
Unlink
Removes a client from that warehouse after a confirmation. The link row is deleted, not just switched off.
View client
The eye icon opens that client's record, for checking their fulfilment setup before you change anything.

What the labels mean

Active
On a warehouse: the building is in use — an inactive one is greyed out. On a link row: that client may use the warehouse right now.
Default
On a warehouse: where goods land when nothing else is specified. On a link row: that client's main warehouse when they are linked to more than one.
Linked On
When the link was created, to the minute — a long-standing arrangement reads differently from one set up this morning.
Active Links
The tile counts every live client-to-warehouse link in the system, across all warehouses.

Worth knowing

  • Linking a client creates their collection point at that warehouse, which is what orders and pickup jobs get routed against.
  • Unlinking is a delete and takes that collection point out of service. Orders already pointing at it will not tidy themselves up.
  • A client missing from the picker almost always means fulfilment is not enabled on their account, or the account is not active. Fix that on the client record, not here.
  • Every link and unlink is recorded against your username.
