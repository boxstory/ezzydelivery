This is the editable version of one delivery task. A single save writes to two records at once — the task itself (driver, status, time slot, pickup point) and the order behind it (customer, address, package, money) — then logs a summary of exactly what changed to the task's status timeline under your username. Saving returns you to the task's detail page.

What you can do here

Task settings
Driver (approved drivers only), status, and a short update note. Leaving the driver blank keeps the current one rather than unassigning — use Unassign on the detail page for that.
Time slot
Morning, afternoon or evening. Any Time clears the slot.
Pickup location
Only the locations belonging to this task's business are offered. [FC] marks a fulfilment centre — goods that are already with us.
Customer
Name, phone, WhatsApp number and the delivery note the driver sees.
Address tools
AI Parse reads the free-text address into Zone / Street / Building; QNAS Validate and Verify QNAS check that plate against Qatar's national address service and fill in coordinates, accuracy and the map link for you.
Coordinates
Latitude, longitude and how trustworthy the pin is. The Map link re-points as soon as you type a pair.
Package & money
Description, quantity, COD amount and delivery fee. The total in the COD card adds up live as you type.
Product items
Change a line quantity — the line and grand totals recalculate — or delete a line outright.

Accuracy values

By client
The customer dropped the pin themselves through the verification link. Usually the one to trust.
By staff
Someone here placed it by hand after a call or a shared location.
Exact
Resolved down to the building — normally what a successful QNAS check gives you.
Street
Right street, unknown building. The driver will have to look.
Landmark
A nearby shop, mosque or junction stood in for the address.
Zone centre
Only the zone is known, so the pin sits in the middle of it. Treat as unverified.
AI estimate
Guessed from the text of the address. Worth confirming with the customer before dispatch.

Worth knowing

  • The task is locked once it has been delivered and its COD has been settled with the business — the form refuses to save and tells you so.
  • Once a driver has actually collected the cash the COD amount is locked for everyone except a superadmin. To correct what was really collected, use Edit COD on the driver transactions page instead.
  • A blank COD field leaves the stored amount untouched — type 0 to clear it.
  • Zone, street and building take whole numbers only; anything else is dropped and the field ends up empty.
  • Deleting a product line happens immediately, before you press Save, and cannot be undone from here.
  • COD appears in two places on this form. Both write the same field, so set them to the same number rather than relying on which one wins.
