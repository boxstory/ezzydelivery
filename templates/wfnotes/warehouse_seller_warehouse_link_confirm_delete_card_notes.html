Confirms removing a seller's permission to use a warehouse. This cannot be undone. The link is what lets that business's stock be received there and what makes the warehouse visible to their account — take it away and both stop.

Before you confirm

Is their stock still there?
Stock records survive the link, but the seller can no longer see them and nothing further can be received.
Was it their default?
Deleting the default leaves the seller without a main warehouse until another link is marked default.
Open work?
Pick lists, returns and put-away tasks in that building still exist — check none of them are mid-flight.
Would deactivating do?
Marking the link inactive suspends it just as effectively and keeps the record, the notes and the history.

Good to know

  • Deletion is logged with who did it.
  • Re-creating the link later starts fresh — priority, default location and notes are not restored.
  • Returns need a link to restock. A customer return for a seller with no active warehouse link cannot put units back.
