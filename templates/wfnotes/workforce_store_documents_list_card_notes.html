The directory of every store's registration record. One card (or row) per business, showing the identifying details we hold on file — name, business code, phone, email, QID/CR number and account status. Use it to find a store and open its detail page, which is where the registration record is actually completed or corrected. Every business is listed here, not only verified or active ones.

What you can do here

Search
Matches store name, business code, phone, email or QID. It runs about half a second after you stop typing, reloads the page and jumps back to page 1.
Card / Table
Two views of exactly the same records. Cards are easier to scan one by one; the table fits far more stores on screen and shows the QID column outright.
View Details
Opens that store's registration page — the completion bar, owner and address details, pickup locations, and the form for editing the record.
Pagination
50 stores per page, newest registration first. Your search and view choice stay applied as you page through.

Account status

Active
A live client, trading with us normally.
Pending
Registered but not yet actioned by staff.
Inactive
Dormant account, no current activity.
Suspended
Deliberately stopped by us.

Worth knowing

  • This page is read-only. Nothing can be edited from the list — every change happens on the store's detail page.
  • The status shown is the account status, not the verification decision. A store can be active with paperwork still unapproved; check All Sellers for the verification side.
  • N/A means the field is genuinely empty on the record, which is usually the reason a store's registration shows as incomplete on its detail page.
  • Despite the name, no uploaded files are listed here — this is the registration record, not a document vault.
