The gate every new driver has to pass. One row is one applicant, with a stamp showing where their verification stands. Open a row and you get the whole case in one place — which parts of their application are finished, their details and documents, and the timeline from registering to being verified — then approve them, park them under review, reject them with a reason, or nudge them on WhatsApp to finish. Approving here is what puts a driver on the road; the working roster afterwards is Drivers.

What you can do here

Search
Matches name, email, phone and driver code.
Filter bar
Narrow by the vehicle they registered, the job type they asked for, a zone group they picked, or the state of their driver account.
Status pills
Switch between the verification states. The counts are worked out after the filter bar, so they move when you filter.
Open a row
Expands the case file — application sections, driver and account details, and the registered / applied / verified timeline.
Approve
Opens a confirmation with two tick boxes: mark the profile verified, and set the driver account to approved. Both are ticked by default.
Mark under review
Parks the application as being looked at, without deciding it. Useful when you are waiting on a document.
Reject
Needs a written reason, and that reason is then shown on the row for whoever picks it up next.
Remind to complete
Sends the applicant a WhatsApp listing what they still have to fill in. It only appears while the application is unfinished, and is greyed out if we have no number for them. The button remembers when they were last nudged.
View progress
Their profile-completion view; Full profile opens the whole driver record.

Reading a case

Pending
Applied and waiting — nobody has picked it up.
Under review
Someone is on it, no decision yet.
Verified
Cleared. The action buttons are replaced by a confirmation line.
Rejected
Turned down, with the reason recorded on the case.
Incomplete
They started but never finished the form. These are the ones to chase rather than judge.
Readiness
A rough completeness meter — the account profile and the driver profile averaged. It is a guide, not a pass mark.
Sections
The six parts of the public application: profile, vehicle, work preferences, zones, timing and documents. Timing always reads as done, because leaving it blank means any shift.
Documents
Counted as done only with a selfie plus two different ID papers actually uploaded — a QID, passport, licence or istimara.
Account status
A separate thing from verification: approved, pending, blocked and so on. The Approved a/c tile counts those.

Worth knowing

  • Verification and account status are two different fields and they can disagree. A case showing the mismatch warning has an approved account but an unverified profile; approving here brings the two back in line.
  • An unfinished application is not a reason to reject. Send the reminder — rejecting locks the case behind a reason the applicant then has to argue with.
  • Approving is what lets a driver accept work at all, so a driver who says they can see no tasks is usually still sitting in this queue.
  • The header tallies and the pill counts follow the filter bar, not the pill you are on — so they will not add up to the rows on screen when a filter is set.
  • Fifty applications per page, ordered by when they applied, and the pager keeps your filters.
