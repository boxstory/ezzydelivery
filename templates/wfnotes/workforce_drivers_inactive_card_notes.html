Everyone who is off the road: applications that were turned down, and drivers who were blocked or suspended after being approved. Nobody in this list can be given work or see anything in the job pool. It is the archive you come to when you need to check why someone was stopped, chase COD or paperwork they still hold, or decide whether to let them back in. Reinstating a driver puts them back on Drivers › Active.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on first or last name, driver code, or phone number. Partial text works.
Read the row
Which of the three off-road states the driver is in, their phone and WhatsApp, and their first registered vehicle.
View
Opens the full record — documents, vehicles, transactions and past deliveries. Changing the status back is done there, not on this list.

Status

Rejected
The application was turned down at review — this person was never an active driver.
Suspended
An approved driver stopped temporarily, normally pending an investigation or a document that expired. Meant to be reversed.
Blocked
An approved driver stopped for cause. Treat it as permanent unless someone senior decides otherwise.

Worth knowing

  • Taking a driver off the road does not clear their money. COD they were holding and earnings they had not been paid stay on the books — settle those on Fleet Transactions and Driver Payouts before you consider the file closed.
  • Nothing is deleted here. Past deliveries, documents and transactions all survive, which is why a blocked driver can still show up in financial reports.
  • Applications that were never decided are not in this list — they sit in Drivers › Pending.
  • A blocked driver may still be attached to a client's pickup fleet. Check Pickup Automation if that client's assigned-pool jobs suddenly stop being claimed.
