How much of our cash is sitting in drivers' pockets right now. One row is one approved driver, and the amount is the COD they have collected from customers but not yet handed in. It is the page to open before a cash handover, and to keep an eye on when a balance climbs. Nothing is settled here — opening a driver takes you to their transactions, and the settlement itself is done on the COD settlement pages.

What you can do here

Period
Preset windows or a custom from/to. This does more than filter — see the note below, it changes what the amount means.
Balance
Show only drivers holding cash, only drivers holding none, or a custom minimum and maximum amount.
Order
Opens on the largest balance first. You can also order by name or by when the driver last handed cash in. The Driver, In hand and Last hand-in headings sort too, and flip direction on each click.
Print
Prints the ledger without the controls — a handover sheet for a cash collection round.
Export CSV
Downloads a CSV built from what is on screen. Ctrl or Cmd + E does the same thing.
Open a driver
Open at the end of a row goes to fleet transactions filtered to that driver — every collection, settlement and adjustment behind the number.
Settlement report
The button in the header goes to the COD settlement report, where money actually gets reconciled.

What the numbers mean

QAR outstanding
In the header: everything the filtered drivers are holding, added up — our exposure at this moment.
Holding cash
How many of the approved fleet are holding anything at all. Drivers on zero are still listed, greyed, and do not count here.
Longest held
Days since the oldest uncollected hand-in anywhere on the page, and who is holding it. Age is the risk, not size — old money is the money to chase.
Last hand-in
The most recent settlement across the whole fleet. If this is months old, hand-ins have stopped happening, not just this driver's.
The bar under a name
That driver's balance against the largest on the page, so you can see whether the exposure sits with one person or is spread. The yellow one is the largest holder.
Days held
Age of the oldest collection that driver has not handed in. Amber past 7 days, red past 14. Hover it for the date.
Deliveries
How many unsettled drops sit behind the amount, prepaid ones included — so a driver can show deliveries with zero cash.
Wallet
The driver's earnings wallet, as it stands now. A separate pot from COD — do not net one against the other.
Last hand-in (row)
When that driver last handed cash in. never on a driver holding money is worth chasing.

Worth knowing

  • With no dates set, the amount is cash still owed to us. The moment you set a date range it becomes COD collected in that window whether or not it has since been settled — the column heading changes from "In hand" to "Collected", the Days held column disappears, and the header line above says which reading you are on. Do not use a date-filtered figure to decide what a driver owes.
  • Only approved drivers appear in the ledger. Cash held by a driver who was blocked or suspended after collecting it is called out in the red band above the table, with a link to those drivers — it is deliberately not added into the totals, which keep meaning "the approved fleet".
  • Nothing on this page settles anything. Marking cash as received happens on the settlement pages; this is a read-only picture.
  • Export writes out only the rows on the current page, not the whole filtered set. Fifty drivers per page.
  • The summary cards follow your filters, so they are not fleet-wide totals unless the filters are clear. The one exception is Last hand-in, which is always fleet-wide.
