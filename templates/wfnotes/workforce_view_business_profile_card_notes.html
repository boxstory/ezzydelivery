This is the file you read before letting a shop start sending us orders. It lays out everything a client applicant has filled in — the person behind the account at the top, then the business itself: name, contact numbers, trade licence or QID, what they sell, and how far along their account is. You arrive here from the verification queue by clicking View Progress on a business card, and you go back there to approve or reject. Nothing on this page can be changed; it is here so the decision is made against the actual submission.

What you can do here

Ring at top
A single figure for how complete the business side of the application is. Red under half, amber part-way, green when every field is in.
Two bars
The upper bar scores the person's own user profile, the lower one the business profile. Both must be healthy before an account is worth activating.
Starred fields
Fields marked with a red asterisk are the ones we require. A green tick means it was supplied; Not provided means it is still missing.
Profile status
Shows where the application sits in the review queue and when it was submitted — useful for spotting one that has been waiting too long.
Back
Returns you to the list you came from with your filters and page position intact.

Two different statuses

Verification
The person's standing in the review queue — pending, under review, verified, rejected or incomplete.
Approval pending
The default state of a new business account. It exists, but cannot trade with us yet.
Active
Live. Orders from this client flow into the system and appear in staff queues.
Inactive
Switched off deliberately — a client who has stopped trading with us.
Suspended
Blocked, usually over unpaid balances or a dispute. Different from inactive because it is a sanction, not a closure.

Worth knowing

  • This page is read-only. Approve, review and reject all live on the verification list; the client's own settings are edited from the client record.
  • Approving the user in the queue switches the business account straight to active, so make sure the licence or QID number here actually checks out first.
  • The percentages count filled-in fields, not verified facts. A 100% profile can still carry a made-up licence number.
  • The business code is generated by the system and is what appears on orders, invoices and COD statements — note it before you leave the page.
  • If it says no business profile was found, the person applied as a client but never filled the business form in. There is nothing to activate yet.
