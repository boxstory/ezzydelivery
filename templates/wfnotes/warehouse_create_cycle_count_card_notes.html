Sets up a stock count. Choose the warehouse, optionally narrow it to one zone or bin, and give it a date — the count sheet is then filled in for you from whatever currently holds stock there. Creating it changes nothing in inventory; it only decides what will be counted.

The fields

Warehouse
Required. Staff see all active warehouses; a seller account only its linked ones.
Location
Optional. Leave it blank to count the whole warehouse. Choosing a zone pulls in every bin beneath it, not just the zone itself.
Scheduled date
Required. It orders the list and says when the count is meant to happen — it does not block counting earlier or later.
Assign to
Optional, staff users only. A pointer to who should do it, not a lock.

What gets counted

Only stock on hand
A line is created for every product with more than zero units in scope. Products showing zero are not offered — a count cannot find what the system says is not there.
System quantity is frozen
Each line stores the figure at creation time. That is what your count is compared against.
Next step
You land on the count sheet. Start it, enter what you find, submit, and a staff member approves the differences.

Good to know

  • Count small and often. A zone at a time finds errors sooner than a whole-warehouse count nobody finishes.
  • Time it for a quiet moment. Receipts and picks during counting show up as differences that are not really errors.
