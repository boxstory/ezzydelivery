The staff queue for stock moving in and out of our fulfilment warehouses. Each row is a request raised by a client (or by us on their behalf) to either send goods into a warehouse or pull goods back out of it, with a list of products and quantities attached. Your job here is to work the queue: check a request, approve it so the warehouse can act, then mark it completed once the stock has physically moved. Clicking any row opens the full request detail with its item lines.

What you can do here

Approve
The tick button. Moves a request from Pending to Approved and stamps your name and the time on it. Only shown while the request is pending.
Complete
The double-tick. Closes an approved request as done and records who closed it. Only shown once the request is approved — you cannot skip approval.
New inbound
Opens the create form to raise an inbound request yourself, for a client who has sent goods without logging them.
Type filter
All, inbound only or outbound only.
Status filter
Multi-select — tick any combination of pending, approved, completed and cancelled. Leaving it empty shows every status.
Business
Narrows to one client. The list only offers clients who have fulfilment service switched on.
Open a request
Click the row to see the item lines, warehouse, and who raised, approved or completed it.

Type

Inbound
Client goods arriving at one of our warehouses to be stored and picked from later.
Outbound
Client goods leaving a warehouse back to the client or elsewhere — not a customer delivery.

Status

Pending
Raised and waiting on a staff decision. This is the only state that can be approved.
Approved
Cleared for the warehouse to handle. The goods have not necessarily moved yet.
Completed
The movement is finished and the request is closed. No further action buttons appear.
Cancelled
Dropped without being actioned. It stays in the list for history but offers no buttons.

Worth knowing

  • There is no undo on this page. Approve and Complete only move forwards, and neither button reappears once used — a mistake has to be corrected on the request itself.
  • Approving and completing record status and audit stamps. Treat Completed as your confirmation that stock physically moved, and reconcile quantities in the warehouse screens.
  • The stat tiles read differently on purpose: Pending Inbound and Pending Outbound count every such request in the system, while Approved and Completed count only what your current filters returned.
  • With type set to All, inbound and outbound come from two separate record sets merged by date, so request numbers will not run in one continuous sequence.
