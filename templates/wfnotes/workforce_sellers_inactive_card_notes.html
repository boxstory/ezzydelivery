Client accounts that have been switched off — churned customers, accounts closed on request, and ones paused over a dispute or unpaid balance. They are not trading: their orders stop flowing and they drop out of the active roster. This is where you come to confirm an account really is dormant and to decide whether to bring it back. Reactivating one returns it to Sellers › Active, provided its profile is still verified.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on business name, email address or phone number. Partial text works.
Read the row
Business name with its account code underneath, the contact details on file, and the account's join date.
View
Opens the full account — team members, API settings, pickup locations and documents. Switching the account back on is done there, not on this list.

Worth knowing

  • The list is built from the account's status alone, so it does not tell you why the account was switched off. That context lives on the account's own page.
  • The Last Active column actually shows the date the business joined, not the date it last traded. For real recency, check the account on Seller Transactions.
  • Deactivating does not settle money. COD collected for a dormant client is still owed to them, and it will keep appearing in settlement reports until it is paid out.
  • An unverified account is not here — it stays in Sellers › Pending whatever its active flag says.
  • API keys and integrations are not revoked by deactivating. If the client must be fully cut off, deal with their API settings on the detail page too.
