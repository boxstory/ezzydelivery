The full stock history of one product: where its units are sitting now, and every movement that got them there. Use it when a number looks wrong — the transaction list shows who changed what, when, and what the quantity was before and after. Nothing on this page can be edited.

Stock levels by warehouse

One row per place
A row for each warehouse and location holding this product. Several rows for one warehouse means the stock is split across bins.
On hand / reserved
Present at that location, and how much of it is already committed to orders. The difference is what is free.
Reorder point
The level at which this location raises a low-stock alert. Set per stock row, not per product.

Transaction history

Before / after
The quantity at that location either side of the movement. Reading down the column shows the balance as it changed.
Type
What caused it — a receipt, a pick, a put-away, a count adjustment, a return. The full ledger is on the Transactions page.
By
The user the system recorded for that movement. Blank means it was made by an automatic process rather than a person.
Last 50 only
Newest first, capped at fifty rows. Older movements are still on the Transactions page.

Good to know

  • A seller sees a narrower page. Only warehouses linked to their business appear, so their totals can be lower than a staff view.
  • Quantities never change from here — correct a wrong figure with a cycle count, which writes a proper adjustment.
