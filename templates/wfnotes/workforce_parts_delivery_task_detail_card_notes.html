This is the whole record of one last-mile delivery task — the leg that carries a parcel from the seller's pickup point (or our hub) to the customer's door. Everything the client, the driver app and staff have done to it is gathered on one screen: address and coordinates, COD, driver, status timeline, uploaded proof, and the first-mile pickup leg if the order had one. Rows on Delivery Tasks open here; Edit Task opens the editable version of the same record.

What you can do here

Edit task
Opens the edit form — driver, status, time slot, pickup location, customer details, address, package and money.
Publish to fleets
Makes the task visible to drivers so it can be claimed. Once published the button turns into a static Published to Fleets marker.
Assign driver
Picks from approved drivers. When one is already on the task the same modal offers Change and Unassign.
Update status
Sets the task's stage by hand, along with a delivered date and a COD amount.
Fix address
AI Parse splits the free-text address into Zone / Street / Building; Verify checks that plate against Qatar's national address service and writes coordinates back to the order.
Shared pin
Paste a WhatsApp or Google Maps link — or drop the shared location onto the strip — and Save stores the coordinates on the order.
WhatsApp
Sends a message to the customer through the chosen sending number. Last Message pulls the most recent message on that thread.
Refund COD
Hands collected cash back to the customer and posts the matching return so the driver stops carrying it.

Reading the record

COD state
Collected or pending, with the time it was taken. A delivered zero-COD task counts as collected — there was simply nothing to take.
Accuracy
How the coordinates were obtained — by the client, by staff, an exact building pin, street level, a landmark, the zone centre, or an AI estimate.
Completion GPS
Where the driver's phone was when the task was closed. Only shown for delivered, failed and cancelled tasks, and only when the phone gave a fix.
Pick list
Appears only when the order is picked from a warehouse, with the picker, bin locations and how many lines are done.
Pickup leg
The first-mile job that brought the goods in. Absent for orders collected by hand or already sitting in our warehouse.
Payment split
Cash, POS, Fawran or card — worked out from the driver's actual transactions, not from a field someone typed.

Worth knowing

  • Changing the status does not clear the COD. Move a task off Delivered while cash is still marked collected and it keeps showing in COD Collection and in the driver's wallet until you fix it in Fleet › Transactions › Correct COD.
  • The refund button only appears while the cash is still the driver's liability — a driver is on the task, nothing has been refunded yet, and the business has not been paid for it. Once the client has been settled, the refund belongs on their side.
  • Publishing to fleets cannot be undone from this page.
  • Seller comments come from the client's own dashboard — they are visible to the client, so keep internal remarks out of them.
  • The page reloads itself once an hour, so a form left half-filled can be wiped.
