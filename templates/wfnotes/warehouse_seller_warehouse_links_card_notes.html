Which sellers may store stock in which warehouses. A link is the permission that makes everything else possible — without one, a business's stock cannot be received into a building, and its staff cannot see that warehouse at all. One seller can be linked to several warehouses, and one warehouse to many sellers.

Reading a link

Default
The seller's main warehouse — where the system assumes their goods live when nothing else says otherwise. Defaults sort first.
Priority
Orders the seller's warehouses when they have more than one. Higher comes first.
Default location
Which door at that warehouse is used for this seller's pickups — one of the warehouse's pickup/dispatch points.
Active
Deactivating hides the warehouse from that seller and blocks new receipts, while leaving history intact.

What you can do here

Search
Matches the seller's name and the warehouse name or code.
Filters
By seller, warehouse, active state and default state — useful for finding sellers with no default set.
Counters
Total, active and default links across the current filter.

Good to know

  • Staff only. Sellers cannot see or change their own links.
  • One link per seller and warehouse pair. Adding a duplicate sends you to the existing one to edit instead.
  • Missing links are the usual cause of "this seller's stock cannot be received here" and of returns that will not restock.
