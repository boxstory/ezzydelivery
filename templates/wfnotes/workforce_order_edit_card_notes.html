The correction screen for a single order — where staff fix a wrong phone number, sharpen a vague address, adjust the cash to collect, or move the order along by hand. The order number, client order code, business and order date are shown for reference but cannot be changed; everything else on the page can. You reach it from the order's detail page, and saving takes you straight back there.

What you can do here

Customer
Name, phone and WhatsApp. These feed the driver's screen and the customer's messages, so a typo here is a failed delivery.
AI Parse
Reads the free-text address and proposes zone, street and building. Check the numbers before you trust them.
Verify QNAS
Checks the zone/street/building triple against the national address service and fills in the zone name, coordinates and accuracy. The green badge only appears once the address is actually verified.
Coordinates
Latitude and longitude can be typed if you have a reliable pin, but the accuracy box is filled by the verification, not by you.
Pickup location
Where the goods are collected from. Only this client's own locations are accepted; fulfilment centres are marked FC.
Package
The one-line description, quantity and COD the driver sees. Changing it also refreshes the driver's copy on the delivery task.
Product items
Searchable item lines with quantity and price. The tick saves that one line immediately, the bin removes it — they do not wait for the main save. A product must be picked before a line can be saved.
Money
COD status, COD amount and the delivery fee. The total beside them is just the two added together.
Statuses
Order status and address verification status, set by hand. Use them to unblock an order, not to invent history.
Task fields
Preferred delivery time and payment method appear only once a delivery task exists, and are written onto the task rather than the order.

Reading the fields

COD amount
What the driver must collect from the customer at the door. Nothing else on the page changes it.
COD status
Whether the customer still owes the money. Switching it to paid-online zeroes the COD amount, and setting an amount above zero on a paid or no-COD order flips it back to unpaid.
Delivery fee
What we charge the client for the job. It is not collected from the customer and is not part of COD.
Order status
Where the order sits in its life, from review through to delivered or cancelled.
Verification
Whether the delivery address has been confirmed with the customer. Separate from the order status.
Accuracy
How precisely the coordinates were resolved — a rough match is worth a phone call before dispatch.

Worth knowing

  • Every save writes a field-by-field diff into the order timeline with your name on it. Nothing you change here is invisible.
  • Product lines are the exception to the save button — they are already stored the moment you press the tick or the bin. Cancel will not undo them.
  • Package changes are pushed onto the delivery task so the driver sees the same thing, but a task the driver has already finished is left alone.
  • Changing the delivery fee here does not re-price anything already settled or invoiced; money that has moved has to be sorted out on the payout side.
  • Saving fires the staff-edit automation, which can send messages out. Do not use an edit as a scratchpad.
  • Cancel returns to the order without saving the form — but see the product-line point above.
