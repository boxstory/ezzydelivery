Every first-mile pickup in the system, live. A pickup is the leg where a driver collects goods from the client's own shop and brings them into our network; one is raised automatically when an order is created, following the rule set for that client on Pickup › Automation. This page is where staff step in when the automation is not enough — nobody has claimed a job, the wrong driver has it, or a driver has gone quiet. You can work one row at a time or tick several and act on them together.

What you can do here

Assign
On an unclaimed row. Opens a driver search under the row — type a name or code, click the driver, and the job becomes theirs.
Reassign
The same picker on a job that already has a driver. The new driver is told it is theirs and the old one is told it was taken away.
Back to pool
Strips the driver off and puts the job back to Pending so someone else can take it. Only offered before the goods are collected.
Cancel
Closes the pickup leg and tells the driver not to collect. The order and its delivery task are not touched — use it when the goods are coming to us another way, not when the order is off. You can add a reason; it is written to the order's timeline.
Delete
Removes the pickup row for good — for jobs that should never have been raised (duplicate, wrong client, test). Superadmins only, refused once the goods are collected, and nothing re-creates it. The order itself stays. If in doubt, cancel instead.
Tick rows
The checkbox column selects a row for a bulk action. Only rows that can still be acted on have one. Shift-click a second box to take everything in between, and the header box takes the whole page.
Bulk bar
Appears once anything is ticked. Search a driver to hand the entire selection to them in one go, send the selection back to the pool, cancel or delete the selection, or clear it.
Filters
Status (or Active only, everything still in play) and mode. Both apply as soon as you change them.
Search
Matches order number, client name, or the driver's code — the code, not the driver's name.
Age
How long since the job was raised. A pending row with a big age is one nobody has picked up.

Status

Pending
Nobody has it. Offered to the client's own fleet or to every approved driver, depending on the mode.
Accepted
A driver owns it but has not set off yet.
In Progress
The driver is on the way to the client.
Arrived
At the client's counter, goods not yet in hand.
Collected
Goods are with the driver. From here the after-collection rule takes over and staff can no longer reassign or recall it.
Dropped
Delivered into the hub. The pickup leg is finished.
Handed Off
Passed to another driver who has confirmed. While the confirmation is outstanding the row still reads Collected and names the driver it is waiting on.
Cancelled
Dropped — either the order itself was cancelled, or staff cancelled the pickup leg from this page.

Worth knowing

  • Assigning does not ask the driver — it puts the job straight into Accepted under their name and notifies them. If they are unreachable, nothing gets collected, so reassign rather than assume.
  • Only approved drivers appear in the pickers, and a driver already on the job is refused rather than reassigned to themselves.
  • Back to pool stops working once the goods are collected. At that point the driver is holding the client's stock and the after-collection flow has to finish it.
  • Returning an assigned-fleet job to the pool re-notifies that client's drivers. If the client has no active pickup drivers, it will sit unclaimed until staff assign it.
  • Bulk actions are best-effort: rows in a state that cannot move are skipped and listed back to you, and the page reloads if anything did move. Read the skipped list.
  • Cancelling a pickup does not cancel the order. If the order is off, cancel the order itself — that closes the pickup automatically.
  • The tallies count the whole system, not the filtered page.
  • The pickup leg carries no COD and no driver earnings — both belong to the delivery leg.
