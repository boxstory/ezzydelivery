This screen is a scaffold. Despite the name, it shows no earnings at all — its whole body is the shared QR / barcode reader component, a camera panel that decodes a code and prints the result underneath it. No view or URL in the workforce app currently points at this template.

What you can do here

Scanner
Asks the browser for camera access and reads a QR code or barcode held in front of it.
Result
The decoded value is printed in the strip below the camera. That is the whole of it — nothing is looked up, matched or stored.

Worth knowing

  • No money is shown or moved here. Nothing on this page reads a wallet, a delivery charge or a payout.
  • Real driver pay lives on Fleet › Drivers Earnings and, for the approval step before payout, Fleet › Earnings Verification.
  • The camera needs permission and a secure connection; on a laptop without one, the panel simply stays blank.
