This is the front door. Everyone who signs up — a shop owner, a driver, or someone joining another client's team — lands here as a card and stays locked out of the real work until a staff member approves them. One card is one person, newest sign-up first, showing who they are, which roles they claim, how much of their profile they have actually filled in, and where they are in the queue. Approving from here is what switches a business account live and puts a driver into the approved pool. For the paperwork behind a driver — licence, ID, vehicle — open their full record from the card.

What you can do here

Status pills
Jump straight to one slice of the queue. The counts next to each pill are live and ignore your search, so they always show the true backlog.
Search & filters
Search covers name, email, username, phone, WhatsApp and user number. The role dropdown splits business owners, drivers, and plain users. The date pair filters on when they applied — or when they registered, if they never applied.
Approve
Opens a confirmation dialog listing everything the approval will switch on. Tick the team roles you also want to activate, then confirm.
Review
Parks the user in Under review — a holding state meaning someone has picked the card up and is chasing something. No accounts change.
Reject
Needs a written reason of at least a few characters. The reason is stored and shown back on the card in a red callout.
Team rows
Where someone has been invited into a client's team, each membership gets its own row you can activate, suspend, or reject without touching the person's own verification.
View links
View Progress shows a field-by-field breakdown of what is still missing from their profile; View Full Details opens the full client or driver record.

Verification status

Not applied
Registered but never submitted an application. Nothing is waiting on you — these are usually abandoned sign-ups.
Pending
Applied and untouched. This is the real work queue.
Under review
Someone has it in hand. Usually means a document or a call is outstanding.
Verified
Approved. The card shows who signed it off and when, and the action buttons are replaced by a confirmation bar.
Rejected
Turned down, with the reason on the card.
Incomplete
Applied with too little filled in to judge. Read the completion bars to see which side is short.

Team membership

Pending
The person accepted the invite; staff have not confirmed it yet.
Active
Live — they can act inside that client's account with the role shown.
Suspended
Access paused, link kept. Reversible with Activate.

Worth knowing

  • Approving does more than flip a badge: a business account goes active, a driver goes approved, and any vehicles they registered go live with them. Only approve once the documents really check out.
  • The tick boxes in the approve dialog are a checklist to read, not switches — the business and driver activations always happen. Only the team-role ticks change what is sent.
  • Approving a driver fires the driver-approved WhatsApp flow, so they are told straight away.
  • Approving clears any previous rejection reason and back-dates the application timestamp if they never formally applied.
  • The two completion bars measure self-reported profile fields, not documents. 100% does not mean the paperwork was checked.
