One driver's complete money ledger. Pick a driver and every financial row attached to them appears — cash they collected from customers, the hand-ins where they gave that cash back to Ezzy, and the earnings we owe them for the work. This is where COD is chased and signed off, and where a wrong COD figure gets corrected. The other direction — actually paying a driver — starts on Driver Payouts.

What you can do here

Pick a driver
Nothing loads until one is chosen. Once open, the pencil beside the name swaps to another driver while keeping your filters.
Filters
Date range or preset, transaction type, settled or pending, business, payment method, task number, and a minimum/maximum amount. Clear drops them all but keeps the driver.
Sort
Click the Date, Type or Amount headers to sort, and click again to reverse.
View
Opens a side drawer with the full detail of a COD row — every task inside a hand-in, its total, and a print button for a paper receipt.
Confirm hand-in
In the drawer, marks a submission as both received and verified in one step — the point at which the cash is officially back with Ezzy.
Approve / Reject
On the Submit COD to Admin tab only, for submissions not yet verified. Rejecting asks for a reason, which is written to the log.
Edit COD
Corrects the amount and payment method on a delivery task whose COD is not settled yet. It updates the task, the linked transaction and the driver's balances together.
Settle selected
Tick earnings rows to build a settlement receipt with a payment breakdown, choose the settlement date, then confirm to pay them off as a batch.
Export / Print
Export CSV lets you choose columns and covers the whole filtered set, not just this page. Print produces a report carrying the active filters in its header.
Recalculate
Rebuilds this driver's running COD balances from their tasks. Use it when the running totals look wrong after a correction.

The three tabs

COD with fleet
Collections and hand-ins together — the full cash story. It also lists prepaid, zero-COD deliveries separately so the manifest is complete.
Submit COD
Only the driver's hand-in submissions, which is the queue to approve or reject.
Earnings
What the driver has earned and what has been paid out. This is the tab the settle action belongs to.

Row status

Pending
Not settled. For COD it means cash is still out; for earnings it means the driver has not been paid for it.
Received
Staff have physically taken the money, but nobody has checked it against the tasks yet.
Verified
Checked against the tasks it claims to cover. Received and verified together read as Settled.
With Ezzy
Paid electronically — Fawran, POS, bank or ATM. That money reached us at the moment of collection, so the driver never held it and there is nothing to hand in.
Settled
Closed. On an earnings row it links straight to the payout invoice that paid it.
Changed
The COD amount was edited after collection. Both the original and the corrected figure are shown so the edit is never hidden.

Worth knowing

  • Only cash is ever "in hand". Electronic COD reaches Ezzy at collection, so those rows are structurally zero in the balance column — that is correct, not a bug.
  • Rows already settled, deposited, or of type settlement cannot be ticked. If a checkbox is greyed out, that row has been dealt with.
  • Editing COD recalculates wallet balances immediately, so the totals at the top of the page can move as soon as you save. Add a reason — it is the audit trail.
  • The driver picker is not just approved drivers: anyone who has a ledger stays reachable however they are flagged now, because a blocked driver still holding cash is exactly who this screen exists to chase.
  • An empty tab does not mean an empty driver. The tabs filter by type, so a driver with rows can still show nothing under COD Submit — the empty message tells you their real total.
  • Searching a task number also surfaces matching tasks that have no transaction at all, so a delivery with a missed COD entry can still be found and fixed.
