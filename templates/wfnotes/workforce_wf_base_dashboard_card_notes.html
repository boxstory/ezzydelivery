The staff home screen and the start of the working day. Nothing is edited here — every tile is a live count that links straight to the page where the work gets done. The left column is the queues that need clearing, the right is the headline numbers, the seven-day trend and the shortcuts. If a count is not zero, something is waiting for someone.

The queues

Ready to pickup
Orders sitting ready for collection.
Pickup pool
First-mile collection jobs currently live — offered to drivers or already claimed.
Temp orders
Rows synced in from clients' sheets and stores that nobody has imported yet.
Pending publish
Orders that exist but have no delivery task raised, so no driver can see them.
Fleet pending
Tasks created but not yet pushed to the fleet — the second half of publishing.
Follow up
Deliveries that need a human to chase them.
Location issues
Deliveries in the last 30 days with no zone or a weak coordinate, split out underneath. These are the ones drivers cannot find.
Approvals
Customer verifications and driver applications waiting on a staff decision.

The rest of the screen

Metric cards
COD currently held by the fleet, orders created today, active drivers and active sellers — each links to its own page.
Trend chart
Orders created against deliveries completed over the last seven days, counted by their own dates — the two lines are not the same orders.
Quick actions
Shortcuts to add an order, publish, tasks and the live map, incomplete deliveries, follow-ups, verifications, COD and settlement, drivers, bulk import, dispatch, pricing inquiries and the finance dashboard.
Recent orders
The last ten orders touched, newest change first — not the ten newest orders.
Refresh
Reloads the figures. Nothing on this page updates on its own, so a screen left open all morning is stale.

Worth knowing

  • Green means the queue is empty, amber and red mean something is waiting. The colour is driven by the count, not by how old the items are.
  • The temp orders count only includes businesses with importing switched on, and skips rows whose source has been deleted — so it matches the Temp Orders page and the sidebar badge, not a raw table count.
  • Publishing is two steps. An order can be past pending publish and still be sitting in fleet pending, invisible to drivers.
  • COD with fleet is a live balance across drivers, not a figure for today.
