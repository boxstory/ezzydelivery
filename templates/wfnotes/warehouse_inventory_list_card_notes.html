What is on the shelves right now, one row per product per storage location. A product stored in two places is two rows, so the same item can appear more than once. Staff see every warehouse; a seller account sees only warehouses their business is linked to. This is a reading page — quantities change through receiving, picking and counting, not by editing here.

Reading a row

On hand
Physically present at that location, including units already promised to an order.
Reserved
Held for orders that are not yet picked. Still on the shelf, but not free to sell.
Available
On hand minus reserved — the number that actually matters when someone asks "can we sell this?"
Value
On hand × the product's price. The three tiles above the table total the whole filtered set, not just this page.
Low stock badge
Shown when on hand has fallen to or below the row's reorder point plus what is reserved — i.e. reordering is already overdue.
Location
The bin code the stock sits in. Blank means the stock was received into the warehouse without a location being chosen.

What you can do here

Search
Matches product name, SKU, brand, product id and barcode — a scanned barcode finds the row directly.
Filters
Warehouse, category and — for staff — business. The low-stock switch keeps only rows carrying the low-stock badge.
Column sorting
Name, brand, SKU, category, warehouse, location, price, on hand and reserved sort the whole filtered list, not the visible page.
Details
Opens the row in a popup — full product record, stock figures and its most recent movements, without leaving the list.
View
Goes to the product's stock card: every warehouse it sits in plus its transaction history.
Receive stock
The only way to add units from this page. Editing a product record never changes a quantity.

Good to know

  • Incoming is not here. Units still expected show on the dashboard, and only become on hand once received.
  • A missing row is not zero stock — it means no stock record exists for that product and location pair yet.
