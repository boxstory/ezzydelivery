The rota for dispatch. A shift locks one rider to one pickup location for a block of time, and that lock is what makes them eligible for batches: when a batch is released, only riders on an Active shift at that same location are considered. Each row is one shift with its planned hours, the hours actually worked, and how much the rider got through. Batches themselves are on the dispatch dashboard; the scoring of all this lands on the KPI dashboard.

What you can do here

New shift
Schedules a rider at a location between two times. Only approved drivers and active locations can be picked.
Status
Filters to Scheduled, Active, On Break or Completed. Cancelled and no-show shifts exist but are not in this dropdown.
Location
The store the rider is tied to for the shift.
Date
Matches the scheduled start day. Leave it empty and the list shows today and everything upcoming — that is why past shifts seem to be missing until you pick their date.
Edit
Only offered while the shift is still Scheduled. Once a rider has clocked in, times can no longer be changed from the list.
Shift code
Opens the shift: rider details, actual hours and every batch carried during it.

Shift statuses

Scheduled
Planned but not started. The rider is not yet eligible for batches at this store.
Active
The rider clocked in from the app, which stamps the actual start time. This is the only status that lets batches be handed to them.
On break
Paused. Break minutes are deducted from the working time used to calculate orders per hour.
Completed
Clocked out, or auto-closed after the scheduled end. Only completed shifts are picked up by the nightly KPI run.

Worth knowing

  • A shift does not start itself. It stays Scheduled until the rider clocks in from the app, so a store with a full rota can still have no one eligible for its batches.
  • An Active shift left running past its scheduled end is closed automatically by a background check a few minutes later, with the actual end set to that moment.
  • The Batches and Orders columns only move when a rider completes a batch — an in-progress trip is not counted yet.
  • The rider is locked to the one location on the shift. Batches from any other store are never offered to them, however idle they are.
  • Nothing here prevents two overlapping shifts for the same rider; that is a rota discipline thing, not a system check.
