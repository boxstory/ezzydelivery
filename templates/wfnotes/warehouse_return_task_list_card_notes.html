Stock that was picked for an order that then fell through, and now has to go back on the shelf. A return task is created automatically when an order is cancelled after its items were already picked. Until the task is worked, those units are off the shelf but belong to nobody.

Task status

Pending
Nobody has picked it up. Opening the task claims it and starts it in one move.
In progress
Someone is walking the items back. Lines are confirmed one at a time.
Completed
Everything is back on its shelf and the stock figures have been put right.

Reading the list

Order and pick list
The cancelled order and the batch it was picked in — enough to trace why the units left the shelf.
Reason
Currently always an order cancellation; the note on the task carries the detail.
Status filter
Use it to keep only open work. Newest tasks first.

Not to be confused with

  • Customer returns (RMA) — goods a customer sends back after delivery. These never left the warehouse.
  • Put-away — newly received stock being shelved for the first time, not stock coming back.
