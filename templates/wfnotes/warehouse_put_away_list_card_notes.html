Stock that has been received but not yet shelved. Every receipt opens a put-away task automatically, and the task is what moves those units out of the receiving area and into a bin. Until it is done, the stock exists on the books but nobody knows where it physically is.

Task status

Pending
Nobody has opened it yet. Opening the task claims it for you and starts it — there is no separate assign step.
In progress
Someone is shelving it now. The name shown is whoever opened it first.
Completed
Every line has been placed and confirmed, so the stock now sits against real bins.

Reading the list

Task number
Quoted on the movements it creates, so a bin transfer can always be traced back to the receipt it came from.
Inbound request
Shown when the stock arrived against a seller's request. Blank means it was received manually.
Status filter
Narrow to open work. Newest tasks are listed first.

Good to know

  • Put-away moves stock, it does not add it. The quantity went up at receiving; this step only changes where it sits.
  • Leaving tasks open costs you later — pickers get bins with no stock and stock with no bin.
