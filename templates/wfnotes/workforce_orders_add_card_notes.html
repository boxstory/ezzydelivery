Type an order into the system by hand, in a client's name — for the orders that arrive by phone, WhatsApp or walk-in instead of through an integration or an import. Pick the seller first; the form only appears once one is chosen, because the pickup locations and the product list depend on them. The order you create lands in Orders with a fresh order number, marked to review and awaiting address verification, exactly as if the client had sent it themselves.

What you can do here

Select seller
Reloads the page against that client. Changing it after you have started typing loses what is on the form, so pick it first.
AI auto-fill
Paste the customer's message as it came in and let it split out name, phone, address and COD into the fields below. Always read back what it filled — it is a time-saver, not an authority.
AI parse
Next to the address box. Pulls zone, street and building numbers out of a free-text address and then validates them.
Validate
Checks the zone/street/building triple against the national address service and fills in coordinates and the zone name. The plate above the fields mirrors what you have typed.
Shared location
Paste or drop a WhatsApp or Google Maps link to set coordinates directly. Latitude and longitude cannot be typed by hand.
Products
Optional item lines with quantity and price, totalled as you go. Add Product gives you another row, the cross removes one. For fulfilment clients the picker is fed from warehouse stock.
Money
COD is what the driver collects from the customer; delivery fee is what we charge the client. They are separate figures and neither is calculated from the product lines.
Scheduling
Tick Scheduled Delivery to reveal a date and time. The free-text preference box beside it is a note for staff, not a schedule the system acts on.
Notes
Three boxes: what the customer said, what the seller said, and staff-only remarks.

Choices explained

Pickup location
Where the goods are collected from. Fulfilment centres are listed first and pre-selected — those goods are already with us, so no pickup run is raised.
Normal delivery
The usual job: collect from the client, deliver to the customer.
Pick and drop
A point-to-point run rather than a shop-to-customer delivery.
Standard
The 48-hour service. Use it unless the client has paid for speed.
Same day
Marks the order for same-day handling so dispatch treats it as urgent.
Order code
The client's own reference. Leave it blank and one is generated — fill it in when the client has a number of their own, so the two systems can be matched later.

Worth knowing

  • Arabic names and addresses are translated to English on save and Arabic digits converted, with the original text kept alongside. Do not re-type them yourself.
  • Leaving WhatsApp blank copies the phone number into it, so the customer still gets messages. Fill it in only when the two numbers differ.
  • The order is created as to review with address verification pending — it is not dispatchable straight away and still needs the usual checks in Orders.
  • Customer and seller notes are merged into one short line on the order and get truncated. Put anything long in internal notes.
  • There is no draft. Nothing exists until Create Order succeeds, and after it does you are taken to the orders list — corrections happen through Edit Order.
