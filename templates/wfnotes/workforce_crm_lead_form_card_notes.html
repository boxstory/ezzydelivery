The manual way into the CRM. Most leads arrive on their own — from the pricing form, the WhatsApp quick-inquiry form, an inbound WhatsApp message or a driver application — but anything that reached you another way (a phone call, a referral, a walk-in, a card handed over at an event) is typed in here. Saving takes you straight to the new lead's detail page, and the lead shows up on the Leads Board and the Leads table with its source recorded as Manual.

What you can do here

Lead type
Decides which pipeline the lead joins — the business sales board or the driver recruitment board.
Company
The business name. It becomes the card title on the board and the account name if the lead later converts.
Contact person
Who you actually speak to. Used as the card title when there is no company name.
Phone
Enter it with the country code. It is stored as digits only and is what links the lead to a WhatsApp conversation — and, for a driver lead, to the real application.
Category
What they sell or ship. Free text, and searchable from the leads table.
Assign to
Optional. The assignee is who the daily follow-up reminder goes to, so leaving it blank means nobody is chased.
Next follow-up
The date you plan to chase. Once that date passes the lead is flagged overdue everywhere until it is closed or the date is moved.
Notes
How the lead arrived and what they need. Visible and editable on the detail page afterwards.

Lead type

Business
A prospective client. It starts at stage New and is worked through the sales pipeline by hand.
Driver
Someone who wants to drive for us. The card is matched to a real driver application by phone number and then follows that application's progress on its own.

After you save

Detail page
You land on the new lead, where stage, assignee, follow-up and notes can all be changed.
Timeline
A "Lead created manually" entry is written against your name so it is clear where the record came from.

Worth knowing

  • At least one of company, contact person or phone is required. If all three are empty the form comes back with a warning and nothing is saved.
  • There is no duplicate check. If the number already has a lead you will end up with two records — search the leads table first.
  • The source is always Manual and cannot be set here. Leads from the pricing form, the quick-inquiry form and inbound WhatsApp are created for you, not typed in.
  • An unreadable follow-up date blocks the save rather than being ignored — leave it empty if you have no date in mind.
