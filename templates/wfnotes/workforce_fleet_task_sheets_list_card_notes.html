A task sheet is the paper round-list a driver carries: their deliveries for one day, with the customer and order details for each drop and the total COD to collect. This page is the picker — one card per approved driver, showing how many tasks that driver has on the chosen date, with a print button beside each. Use it at the start of a shift once the day's tasks have been assigned.

What you can do here

Select date
Reloads the page for that day and recounts every driver's tasks. Today jumps back to the current date.
With Tasks
Hides drivers who have nothing on that date. This is the view the page opens on, so a driver you cannot see simply has no tasks.
All Drivers
Shows every approved driver, including the empty ones — useful for spotting someone who should have been given work.
Print Sheet
Opens that driver's sheet for the selected date in a new tab, ready to print. Disabled while the driver has no tasks.
Print All
Opens a tab for every driver who has tasks that day, half a second apart. It appears only when at least one driver has work.
Eye icon
Opens the driver's own record — profile, documents, vehicle and history.

What the numbers mean

Drivers with tasks
How many approved drivers have at least one task dated to the selected day — that is, how many sheets there are to print.
Active drivers
Every driver in approved status, whether or not they have work. Drivers who are pending, rejected or blocked never appear.
Selected date
The day the counts and the sheets are for. It is the task's own scheduled date, not the day the order was placed.
Task count
Tasks assigned to that driver for that date, counted regardless of status — delivered drops are still on the sheet.

Worth knowing

  • The sheet is a snapshot at the moment you print it. Tasks reassigned or added afterwards will not be on the paper — reprint after a reshuffle.
  • Print All opens one browser tab per driver, so the browser must be allowing pop-ups for this site or most of them will be silently blocked.
  • Nothing on this page assigns work. If a driver shows zero, the tasks have not been dated or assigned to them yet — fix that on the task list, then come back.
  • The COD total on a sheet is what the driver is expected to bring back; it is printed for the handover, not a settlement record.
