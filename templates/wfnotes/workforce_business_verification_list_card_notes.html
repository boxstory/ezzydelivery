The approval queue for new client sign-ups. One card per business whose owner has registered an account, showing what they submitted, how complete their profile is, and where they sit in the verification flow. Approving here is the moment a prospect becomes a live client: it assigns their business code, marks the profile verified and switches the business account to Active. Day-to-day edits to an existing client belong on All Businesses instead.

What you can do here

Search
Matches business name, business email or phone, and the owner's login email.
Sort
By application date (the default), or by when the owner first joined, newest or oldest first.
Filter pills
Narrow the list to one verification state. The number on each pill is the total in that state, not the count within your current search.
Approve
Opens a dialog that demands a business code. The confirm button stays locked until you press Check and the code comes back unused — even a pre-filled one must be re-checked.
Review
Parks the application as Under Review after a confirmation — use it when you are waiting on the client for something.
Reject
Requires a written reason, which is stored and shown on the card from then on.
Link CRM Lead
Attaches an open business lead to this account and marks that lead Won. The picker pre-selects a lead whose phone number matches. Already-linked businesses show the lead number instead.
View Progress
The owner's profile-completion breakdown — what they have filled in and what is still missing.
Full Profile
The complete seller record in a new view, for when the card does not tell you enough.
Timeline
When they registered, when they applied, and when they were verified — with the staff member who did it.

Verification states

Pending
Applied and waiting on us. This is the working queue.
Under review
Someone has picked it up but cannot decide yet — usually waiting on the client.
Verified
Approved. The action buttons are replaced by a confirmation bar, and the business account is Active.
Rejected
Turned down, with the reason kept on the card. It can still be approved later.
Incomplete
The account exists but the application was never finished — chase the client rather than deciding.

Worth knowing

  • Approving does far more than change a badge. It verifies the owner's profile, writes the business code you typed, and sets the business account to Active in one go.
  • The business code cannot be changed casually afterwards — it is what staff, exports and reports identify the client by. Get it right before confirming.
  • The completion bars are informational. A profile at 60% can still be approved, and a profile at 100% is not automatically verified.
  • Linking a CRM lead closes that lead as Won. Check the pre-selected match is really the same company before confirming — it is matched on phone number alone.
  • Every action reloads the page, so your place in a long list is lost after each decision.
