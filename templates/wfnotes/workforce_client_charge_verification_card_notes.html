This console decides what each delivery costs the business. One row is one completed delivery — COD or prepaid, it makes no difference. The system's calculated charge sits read-only next to an editable figure you can agree, adjust or refuse, with the driver's activity trail beside it so the whole job can be judged, not just the fee. The agreed figure is what the Client Payout desk deducts from the COD it hands back. This is the client-side twin of Driver Earnings: one says what we pay the driver, this says what we bill the client.

What you can do here

Filters
Narrow by business, by verification status and by period. The page opens on Pending over the last 90 days, so rows you have already dealt with are out of the way.
Verified figure
Type the charge you want billed for that one delivery. It replaces the calculated figure. Zero is a valid answer and will be respected.
Set selected to
Writes one amount across every ticked row at once, so a flat rate does not have to be retyped line by line.
Verify
Saves the figures on the ticked rows and stamps them with your name and the time. They stay editable.
Verify & publish
Agrees the figure and locks the row. Use it once the number is final.
Reject
Marks the charge as refused so it is visibly unresolved. It does not zero the amount or stop the payout.
Log
Opens the driver's activity trail for that job — waiting time, re-attempts, partial delivery — the context behind a disputed charge.

Statuses

Pending
Nobody has looked at it. If it stays this way, the calculated charge is what the payout deducts — silence still bills.
Verified
A figure has been agreed and attributed, but it can still be changed.
Published
Committed. The row is greyed and its amount is read-only — bulk actions skip it and tell you how many they skipped.
Rejected
Refused during review. Still editable, and still billable until someone sets it to what it should be.

Worth knowing

  • Publishing books nothing. No transaction is written here — the charge is only posted as a deduction line when the business payout runs.
  • Rows already paid out to the business are locked too, even if never published: their figure is on an issued invoice and rewriting it would make the document disagree with the money that moved.
  • Verifying without typing anything keeps the calculated charge and treats it as agreed.
  • Charges over 10,000 QAR and negative amounts are refused as data-entry mistakes.
  • Only 500 deliveries can be actioned in one go, and the business dropdown lists only accounts with deliveries inside the chosen period.
