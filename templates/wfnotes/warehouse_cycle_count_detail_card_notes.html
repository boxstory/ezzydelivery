The count sheet for one cycle count. Each line is a product at a location with the quantity the system believes is there; you enter what you actually find. Nothing in stock moves while counting — the differences are applied in one go when a staff member approves the count at the end.

The four steps

Start
Moves the count to in progress. Quantities cannot be entered before this.
Count
Enter the physical quantity per line. The variance is worked out for you: counted minus system.
Submit
Only accepted once every line has a number. The count goes to pending review; stock is still untouched.
Approve (staff)
Writes the counted figure into stock for every line that differs, each as an adjustment carrying the count number and your reason. This is the only step that changes inventory.

Reading the sheet

System quantity
What was on the books when the count was created — not live, deliberately.
Variance
Positive means you found more than expected, negative means less. Zero-variance lines are left alone at approval.
Variance reason
Free text saved on the line and copied into the adjustment. It is the only explanation anyone will see later — use it.
Sorted by location
So the sheet reads as a walk through the bins.

Good to know

  • Count what is there, not what should be there. Copying the system figure defeats the exercise.
  • Zero is a valid count and is the honest entry when a bin is empty.
  • A line with no matching stock row is skipped at approval, so it makes no adjustment.
