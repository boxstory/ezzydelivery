The full staff record for one client business. Everything the client filled in at sign-up — licence details, contact numbers, address — plus the switches only staff control: whether the account is active, and whether we hold their stock in our warehouse. Use it to correct a mistyped QID, chase a wrong phone number, suspend an account, or turn on fulfilment. Approving a brand-new client for the first time is done on Business Verification, not here.

What you can do here

Edit a panel
The pencil on a panel header unlocks just that panel's fields. Save writes only that section; Cancel puts the original values back.
License
Business name, QID or licence number, product category and trading-since date. The business code is fixed and read-only here.
Contact
Phone, WhatsApp, email and the social pages. The WhatsApp number is the one automated messages to the seller go to.
Address
Street address, city and country. This is the company address, not a pickup point — collection points are managed as pickup locations.
Account status
Active, inactive, pending or suspended. This is the switch that decides whether the client can keep trading with us.
Fulfilment
Not requested, requested, or active. Setting it to active makes you choose a warehouse location, which is then linked to the client.
Products
Read-only list of every SKU on the client's catalogue with brand and price. Nothing here can be edited.
Stats row
Lifetime order count, delivered count, orders still in progress, and the date the account was registered.

Account status

Active
Trading normally. Only active clients appear in staff pickers such as pickup automation and the export tools.
Pending
Registered but not yet approved. Set automatically until a staff member verifies the account.
Inactive
Dormant by agreement — kept on file, not trading.
Suspended
Blocked by us, usually over unpaid balances or a compliance problem.

Fulfilment service

Not requested
The client stores and packs their own goods; drivers collect from their shop.
Requested
They have asked for warehousing and are waiting on us. No warehouse is linked yet.
Active
Their stock sits in one of our warehouses. Orders ship from there instead of raising a collection from the client.

Worth knowing

  • Panels save independently. Opening two at once and pressing Save on one writes only that one — the other panel's edits are still unsaved.
  • Turning fulfilment on is not just a label: it links a warehouse and creates a pickup location marked as a fulfilment centre for that client. Orders from a fulfilment centre do not raise a first-mile collection job.
  • Choosing Active for fulfilment without picking a warehouse is refused — the warehouse is mandatory.
  • Account status here is the business record only. It does not change the owner's login or the verification decision recorded on Business Verification.
  • Every successful save reloads the page, so unsaved edits in another panel are lost.
