Everything we hold on file for one store, on a single page: the business record, who owns the account, the address details, and the pickup locations we collect from. It is the place to complete or correct a client's registration — the same details that get printed on labels, used to contact them, and checked during verification. You arrived here from Store Documents, and every store in that list has a page like this.

What you can do here

Edit store
The Edit Store Information button opens a form under the cards. Save Changes writes immediately and reloads the page; Cancel closes it and discards what you typed.
Editable fields
Name, category, phone, WhatsApp, email, QID/CR, account status, and — when the client has a profile — founder name, city and country. Nothing else on the page can be changed from here.
Quick actions
Jump to the full seller profile, this client's orders, or their licence record; and call or WhatsApp them directly if a number is on file.
Contact links
Phone, WhatsApp and email in the Contact card are live links — tapping them dials, opens a chat, or starts an email.
Back to Stores
Returns to the store directory. It does not remember the search you came from.

Reading the page

Registration
The bar counts six required fields — name, phone, WhatsApp, email, category and QID/CR. Anything still missing is listed beside it.
Verified
Shown only when the client's verification has been approved. Its absence does not mean rejected — it may simply never have been applied for.
Fulfillment
The client is on our fulfilment service, so we hold their stock.
Stats strip
Lifetime totals for this client: orders placed, orders delivered, and how many pickup locations they have configured.
Fulfilment centre
A pickup location flagged with a warehouse icon sits in our building — goods are already with us, so no driver collection is raised for it.

Worth knowing

  • Changing Status to suspended or inactive affects the live account, not just this page — it is the client's real account state, so treat it as an operational decision.
  • The completion bar only counts whether fields are filled. It says nothing about whether the QID is genuine or the paperwork was approved — that is the verification side.
  • Editing here does not re-open or reset verification, and it does not notify the client that their details changed.
  • Pickup locations and the owner account cannot be added or changed from this page; use the client's own dashboard or the seller profile.
