Every picking job in the warehouse, open and finished. A pick list batches several orders into one walk of the shelves, grouped by product so the same item is collected once rather than order by order. Lists containing a same-day order are pushed to the top of this page, then the newest first.

Status, in order

Pending
Created, nobody on it yet. Start claims it for you and begins picking in one step.
Assigned
Claimed by a picker but not started. The name is in the Assigned To column.
In progress
Being walked now. Continue opens it at the products still to collect.
Completed
Every line picked. Set automatically by the last tick — the next step is packing.
Packing / packed
Sorted into orders at the pack station; packed means every order in the list is boxed and ready for dispatch.

What you can do here

Filters
Status, business and zone. Zone is the part of the warehouse the list was raised against.
Same Day badge
At least one order in that list is a same-day delivery. That is why it sits above older lists regardless of age.
Items
Lines picked out of lines total — progress, not units. One line can be several units of the same product.
Create pick list
Batches orders that are ready for pickup and have no list yet into a new one.
Drop to shelf
Superadmin only. Tick lists, and every picked unit goes back to its shelf location with a return movement logged; pick and pack progress is cleared and the list returns to Pending. Use it when a batch was picked in error.

Good to know

  • Ticking a line is progress, not stock. On-hand falls when the order is fulfilled, not when the picker ticks it.
  • A seller account sees only its linked warehouses' lists.
