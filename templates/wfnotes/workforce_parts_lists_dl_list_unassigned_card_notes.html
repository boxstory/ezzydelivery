Published delivery tasks that nobody is on yet. A task on the Unassigned tab is invisible in the driver app — it waits here until staff give it to a driver or send it to Public, or a Task Automation rule does. The Public tab shows tasks sitting in every app driver's New tab that no one has taken yet.

What you can do here

Assign
Tick rows and press Assign Driver. Pick a driver to give them the tasks, or pick Public — all drivers to put them in everyone's New tab.
Run rules now
Applies the active Task Automation rules to everything on the Unassigned tab — handy right after adding a rule.
Open a task
Its Assign Driver box offers the same two choices, one task at a time.

How tasks get here

Arrive
Publish to Fleets, bulk publish, auto-import, return legs and return pickups — whenever no rule claims the task. Also when staff unassign a driver.
Leave
The moment a driver or Public is chosen, or the task is closed or cancelled.
