A read-only audit of one historical problem. Drivers used to include Fawran and POS collections in their cash hand-ins, even though that money went straight to Ezzy and was never in the driver's pocket. Every card below is one such hand-in: the amount recorded against it is larger than the notes the driver could physically have counted out. Submissions now exclude electronic collections, so this is a closed set that cannot grow — the job here is matching the cash desk and the bank, not fixing balances.

What you can do here

Filters
Narrow the set by driver, deposit date range, payment method, business, the order's COD status, or a free-text search over deposit code, task number, order number, customer and payment reference. Apply reloads, Reset clears everything.
Legs to list
Choose whether each card prints only the electronic legs or every leg in the deposit. This changes what is printed, never what is counted.
Export CSV
Opens the column picker and exports the electronic legs that match your filters — one row per leg, with deposit code, driver, task, order, business, method, amount, collection time, reference and COD status.
Row links
Task numbers open the delivery task; Transactions on a card header opens that driver's COD submission history.
COD Ledger
The live, full COD view — go there for anything that is not part of this historical set.

The four figures

Declared
What the hand-in was recorded as — the driver's submitted amount.
Electronic
The part of it collected by Fawran, POS, bank or ATM. That money never existed as cash in the driver's hands.
Physical cash
Declared minus electronic — what should actually have been counted at the desk for that hand-in.
Beyond legs
Declared minus every linked task in the deposit. A separate anomaly — money handed in that no task accounts for. It is shown apart so it is not confused with the electronic sweep.

Order COD status

With Ezzy
Collected and held by us, not yet paid over to the client.
With business
Already settled to the client. Shown for information only — client settlement is out of scope on this page.
With driver
Still flagged as sitting with the driver. On an electronic leg that is wrong by definition and worth chasing on the task itself.

Worth knowing

  • The page has no save, edit or write action of any kind. Restating these deposits would move figures that have already been settled and reported.
  • Driver balances are already right. What is overstated is the recorded size of the hand-in, so this is a cash-desk and bank matching exercise.
  • Filters change which deposits and legs are printed. Each deposit's own figures always cover its complete leg set, because a hand-in only reconciles as a whole.
  • The driver and business dropdowns list only names that appear inside this affected set, so a driver missing from the list simply has no affected deposit.
  • The CSV covers electronic legs only, and is capped at 5,000 rows.
