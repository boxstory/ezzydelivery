Everything we hold about one order, on one page: who the customer is, where it goes, what is in the box, what money is involved, and what has happened to it so far. This is the page you work from when a client calls about an order or a driver cannot find an address. The actions in the header change with the order's state — you only see what the order can actually do right now.

What you can do here

Edit
Opens the full edit form for the customer, address, package, money and statuses.
Publish to Task
Turns the order into a delivery task drivers can be given. Only offered while the order is still under review or waiting for pickup.
Repeat Order
Copies this order into a brand-new draft — for the customer who orders the same thing again. It does not touch this order.
Cancel
Kills the order. Hidden once it has been published, delivered or already cancelled.
WhatsApp
Opens a composer pre-filled with the customer's number and an order message, so you can send from the right business number.
Parse Address
Reads the written address and proposes zone, street and building.
Verify QNAS
Checks those numbers against the national address service and writes back the coordinates it resolves.
Update Location
A map where you can drag the pin, or paste a shared WhatsApp or Maps link, to set exactly where the driver should go.
Verify link
The address-verification message sent to the customer. Send it now, re-send it, or jump to the whole queue.
Timeline
Every status change and staff edit, in order, with who did it. Start here when you are asked what happened.
View Task
Jumps to the delivery task once one exists — driver, attempts, proof and cash all live there, not here.

Verification link states

Queued
Waiting to go out, possibly at a scheduled time. Send now jumps the queue.
Sent
Delivered to the customer's WhatsApp; no reply yet.
Pinned
The customer shared their location and the order now has a confirmed pin. This is the outcome you want.
Manual review
They replied with something that could not be read automatically. A person has to look at it in the queue.
Send failed
The message never left. The reason is shown next to it — usually a bad number or the WhatsApp connection being down.
Cancelled
Dropped and will not be sent.

Worth knowing

  • Publish to Task is the point of no return for the order side — once a task exists, the driver-facing work is managed on the task, not here.
  • COD is what the driver collects; the delivery fee is what we bill the client. The Total on this page is only the two added together and is not an amount anyone ever pays.
  • Address tools write to this order. If the task is already with a driver, tell them — a corrected pin does not reach a phone that has already loaded the job.
  • A partial delivery shows what came back and how much cash was actually collected, which will be less than the original COD. Do not treat the original figure as owed.
  • The pickup card only appears when a first-mile collection was raised for this order; orders shipped from our own warehouse never have one.
  • Nothing on this page is a draft — every action takes effect straight away.
