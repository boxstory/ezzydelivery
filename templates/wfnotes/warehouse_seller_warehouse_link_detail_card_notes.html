One seller-to-warehouse arrangement in full: the terms of the link, and the context around it — the seller's other warehouses, the warehouse's other sellers, and which doors are available for pickups. Staff only.

What is on the page

The link
Default and active flags, priority, chosen pickup point, notes, and who set it up.
Seller's other warehouses
Where else this business keeps stock — the quickest way to spot two defaults or a missing one.
Warehouse's other sellers
Who else shares the building, which matters when judging space and workload.
Available locations
The active pickup and dispatch points at this warehouse, any of which can be made this seller's default.

Good to know

  • Edit changes the terms, not the parties. Seller and warehouse are fixed for the life of the link.
  • Deactivating is the gentle option — it suspends the arrangement while keeping the record.
  • Deleting removes the permission entirely, and with it that seller's access to the warehouse.
