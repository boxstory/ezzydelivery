The client accounts that are live and trading with us. A business only appears here when it clears two separate bars: its account status is active, and its profile has been marked verified during review on Sellers › Pending. These are the accounts that can raise orders, hold COD and receive payouts. Newest sign-ups lead the list.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on business name, email address or phone number. Partial text works.
Total orders
Every order ever raised against the account, for the whole of its life — not a recent count, and not filtered by status.
Member since
The date the business joined. It is also what the list is sorted by, so the newest account is at the top.
View
Opens the full account — team members, API settings, pickup locations and documents. Everything that actually changes an account happens there, not on this list.

Worth knowing

  • An account that is active but not verified will not show here — it stays in the pending queue. If a client insists they are live and you cannot find them, that mismatch is usually why.
  • Order count is a lifetime figure and says nothing about whether the client is still trading. Use Seller Transactions to see current activity and money.
  • Money owed to a client is never shown here. Outstanding COD per account is on Seller Transactions, and payouts are settled from the COD business settlement report.
  • Deactivating an account moves it to Sellers › Inactive but does not touch its orders, its COD balance or its API keys.
