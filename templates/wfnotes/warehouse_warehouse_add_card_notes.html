Creates or edits a fulfilment centre — the building itself: what it is called, where it is, and how to reach it. This is superadmin work and the first of four steps; on its own a new warehouse can hold nothing until its internal layout is configured and sellers are linked to it.

The fields

Name
What staff will see in every warehouse picker on the system. Make it recognisable at a glance.
Code
Short reference used in bin codes and on records. Leave it blank and one is generated. Changing it later does not rewrite existing bin codes.
Address and contact
Where drivers actually go and who to ring. Worth filling in properly — it is the operational contact, not decoration.
Latitude / longitude
Optional. Used for anything map-based; leave blank if you do not have real coordinates rather than guessing.
Active
Only active warehouses are offered on the receiving, counting and dispatch pages.
Default
The fallback building. Setting a second default is confusing rather than useful — keep one.

After saving

You land on the warehouse
From there, configure capacity, then generate the locations.
Then link sellers
A warehouse with no seller links cannot receive anybody's stock.

Good to know

  • Superadmin only. Other staff can view warehouses but not create or change them.
  • Editing uses this same form, so the notes apply either way.
