Every fulfilment centre on the system, active or not. A warehouse is the building; what makes it usable is everything hanging off it — a configured internal layout, generated bins, and seller links saying whose stock may live there. Staff see them all; a seller account sees only the ones it is linked to.

Reading a row

Code
The short reference used across stock, pick lists and bin codes. Auto-generated if it was left blank at creation.
Default
The building the system falls back to when nothing else decides. Keep exactly one.
Active
Inactive warehouses stop appearing in the pickers on other pages, but their stock records stay.
Not configured
No internal layout yet — no zones, racks or bins, so stock can only sit in the building generally, not in a place.

Setting one up

1. Create it
Name, address and contact details. Superadmin only.
2. Configure capacity
How many zones, racks, shelves and bins, and how they should be named.
3. Generate locations
Turns that plan into real bins you can put stock into.
4. Link sellers
Until a business is linked, none of its stock can be received here.

Good to know

  • Creating and editing warehouses is superadmin-only. Everyone else can look.
  • Deactivating is not deleting. Stock, movements and history remain against the building.
