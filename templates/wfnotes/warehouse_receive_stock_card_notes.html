Where stock enters the building. Receiving adds units to a warehouse, writes a movement into the ledger, and opens a put-away task so someone shelves what just arrived. There are two routes in: pick a pending inbound request the seller raised, or type the products by hand. Everything else on the page is the same either way.

  1. A seller raises an Inbound Request listing what they are sending.
  2. The goods arrive at the warehouse.
  3. Staff pick that request above and verify the quantities that actually turned up.
  4. Submitting receives them into inventory and completes the request.

No request behind the delivery? Choose a business instead and enter the quantities by hand — the product list, with each item's photo and what is already on the shelf, loads once a business and warehouse are picked.

The two routes

From an inbound request
Selecting a pending request fills the form with what the seller said they were sending, so you only correct the quantities that actually turned up.
Manual entry
Clear & manual entry drops the request and lets you add products yourself — for stock that arrived without a request.
Business first (staff)
The product list is empty until a business is chosen, because products belong to a seller. A seller account skips this — it only ever sees its own products.
Warehouse and location
Warehouse is required. Location is optional, but stock received without one sits in the warehouse with no bin against it.
Reference, date, notes
All written into the movement record. Leave the reference blank on a request and the request number is used.

What happens on submit

Stock goes up
Each line adds to the on-hand figure for that product, warehouse and location, creating the stock row if it did not exist.
A movement is recorded
One receive transaction per product, with the before and after quantity and your name against it.
A put-away task opens
Created automatically for what you just received, and you land on it — that is the step that moves stock to its bin.
The request updates
Fulfilled quantities go up. A pending request becomes approved, and only closes as completed once every line is fully received — short deliveries stay open for the rest.

Good to know

  • Receive what arrived, not what was promised. Partial receipts are normal and keep the request open.
  • There is no undo. A receipt entered wrongly is corrected by a cycle count, which leaves both records visible.
  • Zero and blank lines are ignored, so an untouched row costs nothing.
