Every vehicle a driver has registered with us shows up here — plate, type, model, colour, and who drives it. This is the register you check when you need to know what a driver actually turns up in: whether the plate on a delivery photo matches, whether someone claiming to run a van has one on file, or which drivers can take a big-item job. Rows are vehicles, not drivers, newest registration first. Nothing is edited on this page — open a row to change it.

What you can do here

Search
Matches plate number, vehicle type, model, driver first or last name, and driver code. It fires half a second after you stop typing and reloads the list, so the result stays in the address bar and can be shared.
Card / Table
Two views of the same rows. Cards are easier to scan one by one; the table puts colour and registration date side by side for comparing several vehicles at once.
View Details
Opens that driver's vehicle sheet, where the plate, type, model, colour and status can actually be changed.
Pagination
Fifty vehicles a page. Your search stays applied as you page through.

What the fields mean

Active
The vehicle is live on the driver's record and counts as one they can work with.
Inactive
Registered but not live. This is the state every vehicle starts in, and where it goes back to if staff park it.
Type
One of bike, car, van, pickup, pickup 3-ton, or pickup for big items — this is the field that tells you whether a driver can physically take a bulky order. None means it was never set.
Created
When the vehicle was added to the system, not when the vehicle itself was registered with the authorities.

Worth knowing

  • New vehicles arrive inactive. They are switched to active automatically when the driver is approved on the verification page — you rarely need to do it by hand.
  • A driver with two vehicles appears as two rows, and both open the same driver sheet showing all of them together.
  • Search does not cover colour, phone number, or driver status — searching for "red" or "approved" will come back empty.
  • Despite the page name, no document files are held here. This is the vehicle register; driver paperwork lives under Driver Documents.
