The inside of each warehouse as a tree: zones hold racks, racks hold shelves, shelves hold bins. A bin is the only level stock actually sits in — everything above it exists to give that bin an address. Most of this structure is created in one go by the warehouse capacity setup; this page is where you inspect and adjust it.

The four levels

Zone
A section of the floor. Counting a zone in a cycle count pulls in everything beneath it.
Rack
A run of shelving inside a zone.
Shelf
A level on that rack.
Bin
The slot itself. Stock rows, put-away destinations and pick routes all point at bins.

Working with the tree

Warehouse filter
Narrows to one building. The counts by type at the head of each tree are that warehouse's totals.
Codes
Built by joining the levels, so a bin carries its whole address — that is what a picker reads on the shelf.
Add / edit
For one-off changes — a new bay, a renamed rack. Bulk structure comes from the capacity generator instead.
Delete
Removes the location and everything under it. Check nothing is stored there first.

Good to know

  • An empty tree means capacity was never generated for that warehouse — stock can only sit in the building generally.
  • These are not pickup points. Driver-facing addresses live on the Pickup/Dispatch Locations page.
