The dials that decide how aggressively each store batches. One row per pickup location that has ever had its dispatch settings saved: how many orders may share a trip, how long the engine waits for a second order before giving up, the delivery time it is aiming at, and whether batching and automatic rider assignment are switched on there at all. Every batch you see on the dispatch dashboard was built using the numbers on this page.

What you can do here

Edit
Opens that location's settings form. This is the only control on the page — nothing is editable in the table itself.
System defaults
The values a location falls back on before anyone has saved settings for it. Reference only, not editable from here.

The columns

Max batch
How many orders may share one trip. The moment a batch reaches this number it is released without waiting any longer.
Hold window
How long a lone order waits, in seconds, for a second order to the same zone. Longer means more batching and later deliveries.
Max hold
The longer window used instead during that location's peak hours, when a partner order is likely to appear.
SLA
The delivery time being promised. Each order's deadline is its creation time plus this many minutes.
SLA buffer
The rescue margin. Once an order is this close to its deadline its batch is force-released, whatever the timer says.
Batching
Off means no grouping at all at that location — every order goes out on its own.
Auto-assign
Whether released batches are handed to riders automatically or left for staff.

Worth knowing

  • An empty table does not mean batching is off. A location gets its row the first time its settings are opened or its first order is batched; until then it quietly runs on the system defaults shown below the table.
  • Changes take effect on the next batch. Batches already holding keep the window and the SLA deadlines they were created with.
  • Turning Batching off does not stop deliveries — every order at that location simply becomes its own single-order batch and is released straight away.
  • Turning Auto-Assign off leaves released batches sitting in Ready until someone moves them by hand. Watch the dispatch dashboard if you do this.
  • Every save is written to the dispatch log against your username.
