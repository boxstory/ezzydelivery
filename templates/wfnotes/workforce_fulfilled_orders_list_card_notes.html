The closed-out end of the order book: every order that has reached delivered or fulfilled, newest first. Nothing is edited here — this is the page for answering "what did we complete", "for which supplier", "over what dates" and "how much cash was riding on it". To work on an order use the main orders list; to chase what is still moving use Tasks.

What you can do here

Search
Matches our order number, the client's own order code, the customer name and the customer phone.
Supplier
Narrows to one client. Applies as soon as you pick — no need to press Apply.
From / To date
Filters on the day the order was delivered, not the day it was created. Either end can be left blank.
Status
Split the list into delivered or fulfilled. Also applies on change.
Clear
Drops every filter and takes you back to the whole archive.
Eye icon
Opens the full orders list already searched for that order number, where the items, address and history live.
Go to Tasks
Jumps to the delivery task list — the operational side, including everything still in flight.

What the numbers mean

Total orders
How many rows the current filter matches — the two status counts below it always add up to this.
Fulfilled
Orders sitting in fulfilled status within that same filter.
Delivered
Orders sitting in delivered status within that same filter.
Total COD
The COD amounts on the filtered orders added up, counting only orders that actually carry a COD figure.
Delivered at
When the order was closed out. It can be blank on older or manually closed records, which is why some rows show N/A.

Worth knowing

  • All four tiles move with the filters. Read them as "for what I am looking at", never as a company-wide total.
  • Setting a date range drops every order with no delivered-at timestamp, so a date-filtered count can be lower than the same list unfiltered.
  • Total COD is what was due on those orders — it is not proof the cash was collected, handed in or settled with the client. Use the COD and settlement pages for money that has actually moved.
  • The supplier dropdown only lists active clients with the fulfilment service switched on, so an order can appear in the table while its client is missing from the filter.
  • Fifty orders per page, and the pager keeps your filters.
