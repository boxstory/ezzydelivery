The handover sheet for one dispatch batch. Work down the orders with the driver in front of you, marking each one as it physically changes hands, then confirm the dispatch when they leave. The two counters at the top are orders in the batch and orders already handed over.

Working the batch

Hand over an order
Marks that single order as given to the driver and timestamps it. Do it as the parcel is passed, not in advance.
Automatic status
When the last order is marked, the batch becomes handed over on its own.
Confirm dispatch
The final step — the driver is leaving. The batch becomes dispatched and you return to the queue.
COD on a row
What the driver must collect for that order. The batch total is the cash they are accountable for.

Good to know

  • Handover cannot be undone from this page. If you mark the wrong order, raise it with a supervisor rather than working around it.
  • A short handover is not a failure — leave the orders the driver did not take unmarked and the batch stays open.
  • Confirming dispatch does not change stock. Stock leaves the books when the order is fulfilled, not when the van leaves.
