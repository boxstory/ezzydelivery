The directory of every client business on the platform — not just the active ones. Each row or card is one company, showing its code, licence number, category, status and the date it registered. This is the way in to a client's staff record: open one and you can correct their details, change their account status, or switch on warehouse fulfilment. New sign-ups waiting to be approved are handled on Business Verification.

What you can do here

Search
Matches business name, business code and QID. It fires shortly after you stop typing and reloads the list from the server, so partial words are fine.
Card view
One tile per client with the status badge, QID, category and registration date. Better for scanning a short, filtered list.
Table view
The same clients as dense rows. Better for comparing codes and statuses across many businesses at once.
Open a client
Clicking a card, or View in the table, opens the full staff record for that business.
Pagination
Fifty businesses per page, newest registration first. Your search and view choice carry across pages.

Status badges

Active
Trading normally. Only these appear in staff pickers such as pickup automation and the task export tool.
Pending
Registered but not yet approved by staff.
Inactive
Dormant by agreement — on file but not trading.
Suspended
Blocked by us, usually over an unpaid balance or a compliance issue.

Worth knowing

  • This list deliberately shows every status, so a client you cannot find in a dispatch dropdown will usually turn up here as inactive, pending or suspended.
  • Search does not look at phone numbers or email addresses. Use Business Verification when you only have a contact detail to go on.
  • The count in the header is the total across all pages, not the number of rows in front of you.
  • Nothing on this page changes a client. Every edit — details, status, fulfilment — happens on the detail record you open from here.
  • Search and view type live in the address bar, so a filtered list can be bookmarked or pasted to a colleague.
