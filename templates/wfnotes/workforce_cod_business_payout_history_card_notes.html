The permanent record of every COD payout ever made to a business — the third and last leg of the cash journey, after the customer pays the driver and the driver settles with EzzyDelivery. One row is one payout: the COD released to that account, what was withheld from it, and the net that was actually transferred. New payouts are made on the Client Payout desk; this page is where you come back to them afterwards to open an invoice, check a transfer reference, or find the code of a payout you need to void.

What you can do here

Account
Narrow the list to one business. The dropdown is built from the payouts currently on screen, so once you have filtered to a single account you must Clear to get the full account list back.
From / To
Limit by the date the payout was made — not the date the deliveries were completed. Either end can be left empty.
Invoice code
Opens the printable invoice for that payout: every delivery in it, the COD and charge on each, the deduction lines and the net paid.
New payout
Goes to the Client Payout desk, where an account is chosen and the next payout is counted out.

Reading a row

Deliveries
How many delivery tasks were attached to this payout.
COD released
Gross customer cash covered by the payout, before anything came off it.
Charges
Everything withheld — delivery charges plus any fulfilment, handling or other lines staff added at payout time. Itemised on the invoice.
Paid out
The net that left the account. This is the figure the bank transfer or cash handover should match.
Voided
A struck-through row with a void date. The COD was clawed back and those deliveries returned to the payout desk as unpaid.

Worth knowing

  • The footer totals exclude voided payouts — the money came back, so it is not counted as transferred.
  • A voided payout is never deleted. It keeps its row and its invoice, stamped with the void date, so the paper trail stays intact.
  • Voiding is done on the Client Payout desk under Void a payout — copy the invoice code from here and paste it there.
  • The charges shown are frozen at payout time. Editing a delivery charge later in Client Charges does not restate an invoice that has already been issued.
  • Invoice codes are generated by the system when the payout is made; they cannot be edited.
