Qatar has no street names, so addresses are written as a zone, street and building number — the national QNAS scheme. This tool asks QNAS where such an address actually is and hands back a map coordinate. Use it to sanity-check an address a customer gave, to find a pin for a delivery whose location looks wrong, or to settle an argument about which zone a street belongs to. It is a scratch tool: it reads, it never writes.

What you can do here

Zone
Required. The QNAS zone number, not an area name.
Street
Required. The street number within that zone. The lookup is done on the zone and street pair alone.
Building
Optional. If you give one and QNAS knows it, you get that building's own coordinate. If you leave it out, or it is not on the street, you get the first building QNAS returns instead.
Lookup
Queries QNAS live and shows the coordinate, the building it matched and how many buildings that street has.
Google Maps
Opens the coordinate on a map in a new tab, so you can see whether the pin lands where the customer says they are.
Copy
Puts the latitude and longitude on the clipboard, ready to paste into an order or send to a driver.

What the result says

Exact match
The building number you typed was found. The coordinate is that building's own.
Street level
No building number was given, or the one given is not on that street. The pin is the street's first building — near, not exact.
No results
QNAS holds no buildings for that zone and street. Almost always a wrong zone or a mistyped street, not a fault.
Invalid coordinates
A building was found but its coordinate is missing or unreadable in the QNAS record. Try a neighbouring building number.
Lookup failed
The QNAS service itself did not answer. Nothing is wrong with the address — try again shortly.

Worth knowing

  • Nothing here is saved. Looking an address up does not change any order, address record or delivery task — copy the coordinate across yourself if you want it used.
  • A Street level result is not a delivery address. Sending a driver to one without checking the building is how a job ends up at the wrong gate.
  • Buildings Found tells you how big the street is. A high number with a street-level match means the pin could be a long way from the door.
  • QNAS is the same source order address verification uses, so a zone and street that fail here will fail there too.
