This is where cash comes back off the road. Every driver still holding COD is listed with the amount in their hands; you tick the ones standing in front of you, record how they paid it in, and settle. Settling writes a hand-in against each driver, empties their COD in hand, and leaves a permanent row in the COD Ledger. It is only the driver leg — paying the money onward to the client is a separate job on Business Payout.

What you can do here

Date filter
Sets the window used for the Total Unsettled COD figure, based on when deliveries completed. It defaults to the last week.
Driver cards
Every driver currently holding COD, largest amount first, with their ID and phone number so you can identify who is at the counter.
Select All
Ticks every driver on the page at once. The Selected Amount tile and the count on the Settle button update as you go.
Payment Method
How the money is being handed in. Applies to every driver in that one settle run.
Reference
Optional receipt or transaction number, stored on each hand-in. This is what finance matches against the bank for non-cash methods.
Export PDF
Opens a printable settlement sheet for the selected drivers. It reports only — nothing is settled by exporting.
Settle
Shows a confirmation with the total, the method and the driver list, then records the hand-ins.
Pending requests
Hand-ins and settlements the drivers themselves raised from the app that nobody has verified yet.

Payment methods

Cash
Physical notes handed over at the office.
Bank transfer
Driver moved the money to our account. Record the transfer reference.
ATM deposit
Paid in at a machine. The slip number belongs in the reference field.
Fawran
Instant local transfer. Again, keep the reference.

Request states

Received
Staff have acknowledged the money arrived, but it is not fully verified yet.
Pending
Raised by the driver and untouched — nobody has confirmed receiving anything.

Worth knowing

  • Settling always takes the driver's entire COD in hand. There is no partial settlement here, and the amount is recalculated on the server at the moment you confirm — so it can differ from the card if they delivered something in between.
  • The date filter does not filter the driver cards. Every driver holding COD is listed regardless of the dates; the filter only moves the Total Unsettled tile.
  • Settlement is immediate and cannot be undone from this page. A mistake has to be corrected with an opposing entry in the ledger.
  • A maximum of fifty drivers can be settled in one run.
  • Settling clears the driver's cash position only. It does not mark the client as paid — that is the separate business payout leg.
