This is the address check for one order — the moment staff confirm that where the parcel is going is real, complete and worth sending a driver to. Orders reach it from the Orders list, usually flagged as pending verification. Confirming here stamps the order itself, so the delivery task, the driver app and the map all inherit what you approve.

What you can do here

Current address
The zone and address exactly as the client sent them, with a map link when the client supplied one.
Customer pin
Shown only once the customer has opened their verification link — the coordinates and address they confirmed themselves, and when they did it. Compare this against the client's address before you approve.
Order summary
Status, order date and COD amount, so you can sanity-check the record without leaving the page.
Notes
Free text kept on the verification record — what you rang about, what the customer said, why you accepted it.
Confirm
Marks the order address-verified with your username and the time, writes an entry to the order's verification log, and returns you to the Orders list.
Cancel
Leaves without touching the order — nothing is saved until you confirm.

Worth knowing

  • The form on this page sends your notes only. Everything else falls back to what the order already holds, so confirming records the current address as valid rather than correcting it. Fix a wrong address on the task edit page first, then come back and verify.
  • Verifying the same order twice overwrites the earlier verification record — it does not stack up a second one — but each confirmation still leaves its own log entry.
  • A customer-dropped pin is stronger evidence than a typed address. Where the two disagree, trust the pin and correct the text.
  • This only settles the address. Money, package contents and whether the order becomes a delivery task are handled elsewhere.
