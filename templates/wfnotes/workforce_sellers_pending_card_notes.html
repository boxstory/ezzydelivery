Businesses that have registered but whose profile is still waiting on verification. Each row is an account a member of staff has to look at — check the trade licence and other documents, confirm the contact details are real, then verify or turn it down. Once verified and set active, the account appears on Sellers › Active and can start trading.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on business name, email address or phone number. Partial text works.
Read the row
The business name with its account code underneath, whatever contact details were supplied, and the date the account was registered.
Review
Opens the full account — documents, team members, pickup locations and API settings. The verification decision is made there, not on this list.

Worth knowing

  • This queue is driven purely by verification status, not by the account's active flag. Setting an account active while it is still unverified leaves it stuck here and off every other seller list — verify it as well.
  • A code shown as N/A means no business code has been assigned yet. That is normal for a fresh registration and not a reason to reject.
  • Verifying is what unlocks live trading, so treat it as the real gate: documents first, decision second.
  • Accounts here can still have team members and pickup locations set up in advance — check the detail page before assuming an application is empty.
