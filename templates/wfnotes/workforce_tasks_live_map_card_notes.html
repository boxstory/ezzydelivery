The whole operation on one map. Every pin is a delivery task plotted at the customer's coordinates, coloured by where it has got to; every car is a driver plotted at their last GPS ping. It opens on the last three days showing only the work still in play, and refreshes itself every half minute. Use it to see whether a driver is anywhere near the job they are holding, to spot a cluster of failures in one zone, or to answer "where is my order" without opening anything.

What you can do here

Date range
Sets which task dates are plotted. Defaults to the last three days. Apply reloads the pins without leaving the map.
Deliveries chip
Shows how many tasks are loaded, and opens a list of pickup locations so you can watch one client's outbound work alone.
Drivers chip
Shows how many drivers are loaded, and opens a driver list so you can follow one or a few. Show All clears the choice.
Status buttons
Multi-select, not tabs — each one toggles that group of pins on or off. All turns the lot on, or off again if everything is already on.
Drivers button
Hides or shows the driver layer, for when the cars are covering the pins you are trying to read.
Live switch
On, drivers sit at their GPS position and everything refreshes every 30 seconds. Off (Static), each driver is shown at their most recent task instead and nothing auto-updates.
Recenter
Zooms back out to fit every loaded pin — the way back after chasing one driver across the map.
Task popup
Click a pin for the task number, status, date, driver, customer and address. The phone number dials, and View Task opens the full delivery task in a new tab.
Driver popup
Click a car for how fresh their GPS is, their speed, and the list of jobs they are carrying — each one a link to that task.

Pin colours

New
Awaiting review or still pending — nobody is on it yet.
Active
Assigned, accepted or picked up. A driver has it in hand.
In transit
On the road — ride started, out for delivery, or the customer has been contacted.
Issue
Failed, rejected, cancelled or the customer was unreachable. Off by default, so turn it on when you are hunting problems.
Delivered
Done. Also off by default, to keep finished work out of the way.

Driver markers

Live
A GPS ping within the last ten minutes. Trust the position.
Stale
Last ping is older than ten minutes. They have almost certainly moved since — the popup says how long ago.
No GPS
No ping at all. The car is drawn at one of their task locations as a fallback, which is a guess, not a position.

Worth knowing

  • The map is read-only. Nothing here reassigns, cancels or updates anything — open the task itself to act on it.
  • A task with no coordinates on its order or address cannot be plotted. The deliveries count includes it, so a missing pin is usually a missing coordinate, not a missing job.
  • A driver marker is only ever as good as their last ping. Never tell a customer a driver is "five minutes away" off a stale or no-GPS marker.
  • Only tasks for active businesses are loaded, and only approved drivers are listed, so a suspended client's work will not appear here.
  • Drivers with recent GPS but no jobs still show up — that is how you find someone free and nearby.
