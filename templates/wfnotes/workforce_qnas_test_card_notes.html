A diagnostics tool, not an operations page. QNAS is Qatar's national addressing service — we call it to turn a zone, street and building number into map coordinates, which is how a delivery task gets a pin the driver can navigate to. This page fires one live lookup with values you type and prints exactly what came back, so you can tell a broken integration apart from a bad address. Nothing is saved and no order is touched.

What you can do here

Zone / Street
Both are required. These identify the street segment QNAS will return buildings for.
Building
Optional. Give one to pin an exact building; leave it blank to get the street as a whole.
Test connection
Sends the lookup through our own server, so it proves the whole chain — our endpoint, the stored token and QNAS itself.
Result panel
On success: latitude, longitude, the building that was matched, the match type, and how many buildings that street has.
Google Maps
Opens the returned coordinates on a map — the quickest sanity check that the pin is in the right part of Qatar.
Raw response
Toggles the untouched JSON. Paste this into a bug report rather than describing the screen.

Reading the result

Exact
The building number you asked for exists on that street and the coordinates are that building's own.
Street level
The building was not found, so the first building on the street was used instead. The pin is in the right street, not at the right door.
Total buildings
How many QNAS knows on that street. A long street with a street-level match can be far off.
API error
We reached QNAS but it gave nothing usable — usually a zone and street combination that does not exist.
Connection failed
The request never completed. That is our side or the network, not the address.

Worth knowing

  • QNAS sits behind a bot challenge. If everything fails, open qnas.qa in another tab, clear the challenge, then come back and test again before assuming the integration is broken.
  • A street-level match is still a usable coordinate, but it is not proof the address is correct — treat it as approximate when a driver reports not finding a customer.
  • Persistent failures across every zone usually mean the QNAS token is missing or expired on the server, not that an address is wrong.
  • Nothing here writes to an order. To fix a real delivery, correct the address on the order or the task itself.
