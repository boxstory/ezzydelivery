One batch, in full. A batch is the trip a rider makes with orders that all leave the same pickup location for the same delivery zone — normally two orders, sometimes just one. This page shows how the batch was built, who is carrying it, which orders are inside with their SLA deadlines, and a timestamped log of everything that happened to it. It is the page to open when someone asks why an order left late or went out alone.

What you can do here

Release now
Offered only while the batch is holding. Ends the wait immediately and closes the batch with whatever it has, so it can be picked up by a rider. Recorded against your username as a manual override.
Cancel batch
Offered while the batch is still pending, holding or ready. Marks it cancelled so no rider can be given it.
Order links
Each order number opens that order in a new tab, where the delivery task, driver and money live.
Activity log
Read-only history for this batch: created, orders added, released with a reason, assigned, started, completed or cancelled.

Fields explained

Release reason
Why the batch stopped waiting. Batch Full — it reached its order limit. Hold Window Expired — the timer ran out with no partner order. SLA Risk — an order inside was close to its deadline and was rescued. Manual Release — staff pressed the button. Off-Peak Single Release — batching is switched off for that location, so it went out alone.
Total COD
The cash the rider carries for the whole batch — the sum of the orders' COD. A rider without enough credit headroom is skipped when the batch is being assigned.
Seq
The order of drops inside the batch — the first order in is delivered first.
SLA deadline
Set when the order joined the batch: the time the order was created plus the location's SLA target. AT RISK means the deadline came close enough that the system force-released the batch.

Worth knowing

  • Cancelling stops the batch, not the orders. Their delivery tasks are left exactly as they are — handle those on the order or task pages.
  • Releasing does not choose a rider. The batch moves to Ready and a background pass assigns it, usually within seconds, to someone on an Active shift at this location.
  • When a batch is assigned, the delivery tasks of its orders are switched to that rider and marked in-transit automatically.
  • Nothing here can add or remove an order once the batch exists — the engine decides membership at the moment each order is verified.
  • SLA compliance is judged on the whole batch's completion time against each order's own deadline, which is why one slow drop can breach both orders.
