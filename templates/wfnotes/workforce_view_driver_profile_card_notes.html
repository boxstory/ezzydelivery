This is the case file you read before deciding whether to let someone drive for us. It gathers everything a driver applicant has submitted — contact details, licence number, identity documents, vehicles, and where they were standing when they applied — onto one read-only page. You get here from the verification queue by clicking View Progress on a driver's card, and you go back there to actually approve or reject. Nothing on this page changes anything; it exists so the decision is made with the evidence in front of you.

What you can do here

Requirements
The rail on the left counts how many of the nine things we ask for are actually on file. Click any line to jump to that part of the file.
Documents
Thumbnails of every identity document uploaded. Click one to open it full size in a new tab — do that rather than judging from the thumbnail.
Vehicles
Every vehicle they registered, with type, model, colour, plate, and whether it is live yet.
Location
Where their browser said they were when they submitted, with a Google Maps link. Treat it as a sanity check, not evidence.
Back
Returns to whatever list you came from, filters and page number intact.

Reading the file

On file
Something was submitted for that requirement. It does not mean anyone has checked that it is genuine or legible — that is your job.
Missing
Nothing submitted. Chase the applicant before deciding, rather than rejecting outright.
Verification
The badge in the header is the user account's state in the review queue — pending, under review, verified, rejected or incomplete.
Account status
A separate thing: the driver record itself is pending, processing, approved, rejected, blocked or suspended. Only approved drivers can take work.
Percentages
How much of the user profile and the driver profile has been filled in. They count fields, not quality — a fully filled form can still be a bad application.

Worth knowing

  • This page is read-only. Approve, reject and under-review all happen on the verification list; the driver record itself is edited from the fleet pages.
  • Documents with no file attached are hidden entirely, so an empty documents section means nothing was uploaded — not that it failed to load.
  • Approving this driver in the queue also switches their registered vehicles from inactive to active, so check the vehicle list before you sign off.
  • The registration location is self-reported by the applicant's phone and can be spoofed or simply wrong. Never treat it as proof of residence.
  • If the page says no driver profile exists, the person created an account but never started the driver application — there is nothing to approve yet.
