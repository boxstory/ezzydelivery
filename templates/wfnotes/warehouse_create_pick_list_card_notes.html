Turns orders into one picking job. Tick the orders you want collected together and the system builds a pick list grouped by product, so a picker walks the shelves once instead of once per order. Only orders that are ready for pickup and do not already belong to a pick list appear here — the fifty most recent.

How it works

Pick the orders
Any mix you like. Orders going to the same warehouse end up on the same list.
Grouped by product
Ten orders needing the same item become one line for ten units. They are split back out per order at the pack station.
Items count
The number beside an order is how many of its products can actually be picked — order lines with no product record are skipped.
After creating
You land on the new list, ready to start picking.

Why an order is missing

Not ready yet
Only orders at ready to pickup qualify. Anything earlier in the pipeline is not offered.
Already on a list
An order can only belong to one pick list. Find it from the Pick Lists page instead.
No products
Orders whose lines are not linked to product records have nothing to pick, so they produce no items.
Another business
A seller account sees only its own orders; staff see everyone's.

Good to know

  • Batch by shape of work — orders drawing from the same area make a shorter walk than a random selection.
  • Nothing moves yet. Creating a list only plans the work; stock is untouched until the orders are fulfilled.
