Every stock count, scheduled and done. A cycle count is how the recorded quantity gets checked against what is really on the shelf — someone walks a zone, counts what they find, and any difference is applied to the books once a staff member approves it. Listed by scheduled date, newest first.

Status, in order

Scheduled
Created with its lines already drawn from current stock, waiting for its date.
In progress
Counting has started. Quantities can only be entered while a count is in this state.
Pending review
Every line counted and submitted. Nothing has changed in stock yet — this is the checkpoint.
Completed
Approved by staff, and the differences have been written into stock as adjustments.

Reading the list

Count number
Carried on every adjustment the count produces, so any corrected figure can be traced back here.
Location
The zone or bin being counted. Blank means the whole warehouse was in scope.
Assigned to
Who is expected to do the counting. It does not stop anyone else opening the count.

Good to know

  • A count is the proper way to correct stock. It leaves an adjustment in the ledger with a reason attached.
  • Lines are fixed when the count is created — stock that arrives afterwards is not in that count.
