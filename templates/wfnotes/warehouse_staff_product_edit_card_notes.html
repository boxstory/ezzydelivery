Edits the product record itself — name, brand, SKU, barcode, category, price, unit, image — on behalf of the seller who owns it. It is the same product form the seller uses, reachable from the warehouse so ops staff can fix a detail without asking the client to do it. Nothing here changes a stock quantity.

What you can do here

Fix identifiers
A wrong SKU or missing barcode is the usual reason to be here — both are what Inventory search matches on.
Category and price
Category drives the Inventory filter; price drives the stock value figures. Changing price re-values the stock immediately.
Save changes
Returns you to wherever you came from, or to Inventory if you arrived directly.

Who can edit what

Staff
Any product, whichever business owns it.
Warehouse users
Products belonging to businesses linked to their own warehouses — so a 3PL operator can maintain their clients' catalogue.

Good to know

  • The edit is the seller's data. It shows in their dashboard and on their orders, not just in the warehouse.
  • To change a quantity, receive stock or run a cycle count — those write a movement, this does not.
