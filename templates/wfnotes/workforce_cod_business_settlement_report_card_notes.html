This is the desk where COD is handed back to the client — the last leg of the cash journey. A delivery only appears here once the customer has paid the driver and the driver has settled that cash with EzzyDelivery, so the money is sitting with us and belongs to the business. You pick one account, tick the deliveries you are paying for, decide what comes off the top, and hand over. The payout writes an invoice you land on straight afterwards, and it is listed forever on Payout History.

What you can do here

Account
Choose the business to pay. Nothing is listed until you do. Each option already shows how much COD is held for that account, so you can see who is owed money without opening them one by one.
From / To
Restrict to deliveries completed in a date window. Leave both empty to pay out everything held.
Count all
Ticks or clears every delivery in that account's panel. Individual rows can still be unticked afterwards.
Delivery charges
On by default — recovers the delivery fee for the ticked rows out of the COD being released. Untick it to hand over the full COD and bill the fees separately.
Add deduction
Extra invoice lines for this payout: fulfilment, cargo/handling or any other charge, each with its own description and amount.
Hand over
Opens a receipt listing every ticked order, then asks how it was paid (bank, cash, Fawran) and for a reference before it commits.
Void a payout
Paste a payout's invoice code with a reason to claw the COD back — use it when a settled order comes back.
Statement PDF
Downloads a manifest of the COD still awaiting payout, for the selected account or all of them.

The charge on each row

Pending
Nobody has agreed this charge yet. The system figure will be deducted as it stands unless someone changes it in Client Charges first.
Verified
Staff have checked the job and agreed the figure, but it is still editable.
Published
The agreed figure is locked and can no longer be edited.
Rejected
The charge was refused during verification. The row still pays out — fix the figure in Client Charges before handing over.

The money strip

COD held
Everything on the account awaiting payout, whether ticked or not.
Net payable
COD selected, less delivery charges, less any lines you added — the exact figure to transfer.

Worth knowing

  • The net is recalculated on the server from the rows it locks, so what the screen shows is what gets paid — but the totals do not refresh on their own if someone else settles the same account while you are looking.
  • Deductions can never exceed the COD selected. The button disables and the payout is refused.
  • Hand-added deduction lines belong to one invoice, so a payout carrying them can only cover one account at a time.
  • The charge each row pays is frozen onto that delivery at payout, so a later edit in Client Charges cannot restate an invoice that has already been issued.
  • A payout is not undone by editing anything here — use Void a payout, which returns those deliveries to this desk as unpaid.
