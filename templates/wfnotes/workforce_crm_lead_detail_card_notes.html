Everything known about a single lead — a prospective client, or a driver applying to work with us. The left column shows where the lead came from, its editable contact details, an optional AI read of the conversation, and the full WhatsApp thread with any shared photos and files. The right column is where you actually work it: stage, assignee, follow-up date, internal notes and the running activity log. You get here from the Leads Board or the Leads table, and every edit made here is what those two views display.

What you can do here

Call / WhatsApp
Shown when the lead has a phone number — dials it, or opens a WhatsApp chat with that number in a new tab.
Pipeline Stage
Click a stage to move the lead. It saves on the click — there is no separate confirm — and the move is written to the timeline.
Save Changes
Saves the assignee, next follow-up date and internal notes together. Only fields you actually changed are recorded.
Save contact
A separate form for company, contact person, phone and product category. The phone is stored as digits only.
AI Summary
Has AI read the WhatsApp thread and activity log and write a short sales summary. The panel only appears when an AI provider is configured.
Link WhatsApp
Search the synced contact directory and attach the right chat when matching on the stored phone missed it. Saving pulls that chat's history in at once.
Log Activity
Add a Note or a Follow-up entry describing what was said, with your name against it.
Timeline
The full history of the lead. You can delete entries you wrote; superadmins can delete any.
Source links
Jump to the original pricing inquiry, the linked business account, or the matched driver profile where one exists.

Timeline entries

Note
Free text you wrote, plus the automatic entries for contact and notes edits.
Follow-up
A record of an actual call or chase, and the follow-up date being set or cleared.
Stage Change
Written automatically on every stage move, including moves made by dragging on the board.
Assignment
The lead being handed to a staff member or unassigned.
Conversion
The lead being attached to a real business account.

Driver leads

Application
The extra panel shows the applicant's real progress — registered, applied, verified — and which form sections and documents are still missing.
Decisions
Under Review, Approved and Rejected write back to the driver's verification status and message them on WhatsApp.

Worth knowing

  • On a driver lead the stage is a mirror of the real application: opening this page re-syncs it, so a stage you set by hand can be corrected back. Fix the application, not the card.
  • Setting a driver lead to Approved or Rejected changes their live verification status and sends them a WhatsApp message. That message cannot be recalled.
  • Attaching a lead to a real business account is done on the Business Verification page, not here. That action also marks the lead Won.
  • The conversation panel only appears once WhatsApp messages have been matched to this number. If it is missing, use Link WhatsApp Chat — and remember that editing the phone changes which chat matches.
  • WhatsApp clears its own copies of media within minutes, so older attachments may no longer open even though the message is still listed.
  • The AI summary is stored on the lead and reused until you press Refresh — it does not update itself when new messages arrive.
  • Deleting a timeline entry is permanent; there is no undo.
