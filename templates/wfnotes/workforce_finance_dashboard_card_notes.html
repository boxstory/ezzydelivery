The money overview for the whole operation: how much cash the fleet is holding, where every riyal of COD currently sits, what drivers have earned, and how deliveries performed. Nothing on this page can be edited — it is a roll-up of driver wallets, driver transactions and orders, and every figure links out to the page that can actually act on it. Almost everything respects the period picker in the top right; a few figures do not, and those are listed below.

What you can do here

Period
The dropdown in the hero sets the window for every section at once. The small 7d / 30d / 90d / 1yr pills beside each section heading do the same thing — they are shortcuts, not per-section filters.
COD with fleet
Cash sitting with approved drivers right now — the total, the average per driver, how many drivers are carrying anything, and earnings still owed to them.
COD pipeline
The flow bar and the six tiles trace collected money through to settled. Each tile's button opens the working page for that stage.
Metrics
Earnings, settlements, pending, charges, payables and receivables for the period, then delivery counts, average order value, delivery fees, active drivers and the success rate.
Top COD
The ten drivers holding the most cash, with their wallet balance beside it. Driver names open the driver record.
Breakdown
Every transaction type in the period with its count and total, plus the last twenty transactions in full.
Quick links
The strip at the bottom jumps to COD in Hand, Driver Earnings, All Transactions and the COD Settlement report.

COD pipeline terms

Collected
Everything drivers took from customers in the period. The top of the funnel.
In hand
What drivers are carrying right now. This is a live balance, not a period figure.
In bank
Deposits drivers made into the bank during the period.
Settled
Driver-side closure — deposits plus cash handed in at the desk. The driver no longer owes it.
To settle
Collected minus what the drivers have cleared minus what has gone out to clients. Never shown below zero.
Returns
COD given back — refused deliveries and reversed collections.

Worth knowing

  • In hand and pending earnings are live balances across all approved drivers. Changing the period does not change them, so they will not tie back to the other tiles.
  • Client settlement is counted gross here — charges withheld on a payout invoice are added back, otherwise "to settle" could never close to zero.
  • COD in Hand is a driver's own balance, while the settlement report is per client. The same money appears under both until it is paid over — it is not double counting.
  • Failed deliveries count by when the order was last touched, not when it was attempted, so a short period can show a failure that belongs to an older order.
  • Average value adds COD and the delivery fee together, so it is the value carried, not revenue.
  • Recent Transactions is only the latest twenty. Use View All for anything you intend to reconcile against.
