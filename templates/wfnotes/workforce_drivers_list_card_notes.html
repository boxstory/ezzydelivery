The full driver roster. One row is one driver — who they are, how to reach them, where they stand with us, whether they are free right now, what they drive and how they are rated. The page opens filtered to approved drivers only, which is the working roster; the tally segments above the filters switch that. To act on a driver — approve, block, edit, check documents — open them with View.

What you can do here

Tally cards
Each one is a shortcut filter: all drivers, approved only, awaiting approval (pending and processing), or inactive (rejected, blocked and suspended). The counts are always of the whole roster, not of what is filtered.
Search
Matches first name, last name, username, driver code and phone. It runs half a second after you stop typing, on top of the other filters.
Driver status
Tick as many statuses as you like — this one is multi-select, unlike the rest.
Work preference
Full time, part time or flexible, as declared by the driver when they applied.
Working hours
Narrows to drivers who signed up for that shift band — morning, afternoon, evening or night. A driver can hold several.
Sort by
Newest or oldest joiner, name A–Z, rating high to low, or driver code.
CSV button
Opens the column picker — choose the fields you want and download the current list as a spreadsheet.
View
Opens the driver's record: profile, documents, vehicle, wallet and task history. Every change to a driver happens there, not here.

Status and availability

Approved
Cleared to work — can be assigned tasks and can claim from the pool.
Pending
Applied, nobody has looked at them yet.
Processing
Under review — documents being checked, not yet cleared.
Rejected
Application turned down.
Blocked
Barred from working, usually after an incident.
Suspended
Stood down for now, expected back.
Availability
The live duty state the driver sets in their app — available, on delivery, on break, returning, or offline. It says nothing about whether they are approved.
Zones
The zone groups the driver prefers to work, shown under availability. Two are listed and the rest counted.

Worth knowing

  • Landing on this page you are seeing approved drivers only. A driver you cannot find is usually pending or blocked — press All Drivers before concluding they do not exist.
  • Work preference, working hours and zones are what the driver asked for, not a roster we enforce. They do not stop anyone being assigned outside those hours.
  • The vehicle column shows the driver's first registered vehicle only; a driver with two shows one here.
  • The CSV export carries the search and a single status across, and is capped at 2,000 rows — narrow the list before exporting a large roster.
  • Fifty drivers per page; the pager keeps your filters.
