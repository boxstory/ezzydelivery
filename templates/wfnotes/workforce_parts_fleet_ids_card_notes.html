This screen is a scaffold. Its whole body is the shared QR / barcode reader component — a camera panel that decodes a code and prints the result underneath it. There is no list, no filter and no save action, and no view or URL in the workforce app currently points at this template.

What you can do here

Scanner
Asks the browser for camera access and reads a QR code or barcode held in front of it.
Result
The decoded value is printed in the strip below the camera. That is the whole of it — nothing is looked up, matched or stored.

Worth knowing

  • Nothing is saved. The scanner is not attached to a driver, a document or a task, so a scan here has no effect anywhere in the system.
  • Driver identity documents are reviewed on Documents › Driver IDs, which is the page you almost certainly want.
  • The camera needs permission and a secure connection; on a laptop without one, the panel simply stays blank.
