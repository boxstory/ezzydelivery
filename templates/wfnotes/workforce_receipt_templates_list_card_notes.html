This is the library of printable receipt layouts used elsewhere in the fleet money pages — the paper a driver walks away with after a settlement, a COD deposit or an earnings payout. Each card is one saved template: its name, what it is for, and the paper it is designed to print on. Nothing here touches money or transactions; you are only editing how a receipt looks when it is printed.

What you can do here

All Types
Narrows the grid to one kind of receipt. The choice is kept in the URL, so a filtered list can be shared or bookmarked.
Create Template
Opens the template editor — name, type, paper size, logo, company block, colour, font, signature line, QR code, footer text and optional custom CSS.
Preview
Opens the layout in a new tab filled with sample data — a made-up driver, settlement and a few COD lines. It never reads a real settlement.
Edit
Reopens the same editor on an existing template and saves over it.
Delete
Removes the template after a confirmation prompt. There is no undo and no archive — the record is gone.
Default badge
A star on the card marks the template flagged as default. Set or clear the flag from the editor, not from this list.

Template types

Settlement
The receipt for a completed driver settlement — period, gross, deductions, net.
COD deposit
The slip for cash a driver hands in, listing the collections being deposited.
Earnings
A statement of what a driver earned over a period rather than a cash movement.
Transaction
A single-transaction receipt, for one-off entries rather than a batch.

Paper sizes

Thermal
80mm or 58mm rolls — the narrow slips from a counter printer. Keep the company block and footer short or they wrap badly.
Sheet
A4, A5 or Letter — full-page layouts for anything that gets filed or emailed.

Worth knowing

  • Editing a template changes future prints only. Receipts already printed or saved are untouched.
  • Marking a template as default does not automatically clear the flag on the other templates of the same type — check the list afterwards so only one carries the star.
  • The card thumbnail is a generic mock-up, not a render of your layout. Use Preview to see what actually prints.
  • Custom CSS and custom HTML are applied as written. A broken rule shows up as a broken receipt at the counter, so preview before leaving the editor.
