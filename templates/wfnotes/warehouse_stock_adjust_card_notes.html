Where the books are made to agree with the shelf. An adjustment does not move stock anywhere — it corrects the recorded figure when the recorded figure is wrong, and leaves a movement in the ledger explaining why.

  1. Pick the business, the warehouse and — if it matters — the storage location.
  2. The sheet loads every product with its photo and the figure currently on record.
  3. Type what you counted. The change is worked out for you and shown per row.
  4. Give a reason, submit, and each corrected line writes an adjustment movement.

How the sheet behaves

Counted, not the difference
Enter the physical count. The delta is derived at submit time from the live figure, so a receipt or a pick landing while you type is never overwritten with a stale number.
Blank rows are skipped
Only rows you fill in are considered, and a row that matches the current figure writes nothing at all.
Per location
The count applies to the location chosen above. Leave it on default and you are counting the stock held against no bin.
Reason is required
Damaged, lost, found, expired, returned to supplier, data correction or other — it is written into the movement, so the ledger can be read months later.

What happens on submit

The stock figure is replaced
Set to what you counted, creating the stock row if the product had never been counted into that location.
A movement is recorded
One adjustment in or adjustment out per corrected product, with the before and after figure and your name on it.
Reserved stock is flagged
Counting a product below what open orders have reserved still applies, but you are told which products — those orders may no longer be fulfillable.

Good to know

  • There is no undo. A wrong adjustment is corrected by another one, and both stay visible in the ledger.
  • Counting a whole warehouse? Use a cycle count instead — it is built for a full sweep and keeps the "last counted" record.
  • Adjustments are not receiving. Goods that arrived from a seller belong on the Receive Stock page, which also opens a put-away task.
