Everything about one driver's pay on a single desk. The four cards across the top follow the money through its stages and mark the one holding it right now. Below that are the two legs of the job: Ready to pay is money already agreed and waiting for a payout to be written, and Verification queue is the delivered work whose fee has not been settled yet. Set the fees in the queue, publish them, then pay them out — the payout produces an invoice listed under Recent payouts.

What you can do here

Stat cards
The first two are filters — click one to show only those deliveries. Ready to pay jumps to the payment panel; Last paid opens that payout's invoice.
Period
Limits the verification queue to work done in the last 7, 30, 90 or 365 days. It does not touch the Ready-to-pay panel, which shows everything unpaid regardless of age.
Rail filters
Cut the queue down to the rows that left the flat rate — priced from the client charge, or adjusted by staff. Show all clears them.
Driver fee
Type the amount to pay for that one delivery. Zero is accepted and respected — it will not snap back to the calculated figure.
Set selected to
Writes one amount across every ticked row at once.
Verify
Saves the fees on the ticked rows without committing them. They stay editable.
Verify & publish
Commits the fee and turns it into money owed — the row leaves the queue and its earning appears in Ready to pay.
Reject
Marks a fee as refused so the row is visibly unresolved instead of quietly sitting in the queue.
Log
Opens the driver's activity trail for that job — attempts, waiting, partial delivery — the context behind an unusual fee.
Create payout
Pays the ticked lines in Ready to pay, using the Paid by method and reference entered below the table. Untick any line to hold it back for later.

How the fee is priced

Flat rate
The default for an ordinary delivery, and for a hub leg. Most rows sit here, which is why the rail counts the exceptions rather than these.
Metered
Pick-and-drop orders pay 80% of the charge billed to the client, so the fee moves with the job instead of the flat rate.
Adjusted
Staff typed a figure that differs from the calculated one. The row shows what it would otherwise have been.

Queue statuses

Pending
Nobody has agreed the fee. Nothing is owed to the driver yet.
Verified
A figure is agreed and attributed, but still editable and still not payable.
Published
Committed. The delivery leaves this queue for good and the money shows up in Ready to pay.
Rejected
The fee was refused during review; the row stays in the queue until someone sets it properly.

Worth knowing

  • The COD column is read-only context only. That is cash the driver holds for EzzyDelivery, not money owed to them — it is settled on its own leg and is never part of a payout total.
  • Publishing is the point of no return for the queue: the delivery disappears from it and becomes a payable line. Fix a fee before publishing, not after.
  • Deduction lines show struck through and come off the net. The payout pays what the panel's arithmetic says, not the earnings total.
  • The Ready-to-pay panel is only drawn when there is something to pay — an empty stat card already says there is not.
  • The Period filter can hide old unverified deliveries. If a driver claims for work that is not listed, widen it to a year before concluding it does not exist.
