The book of record for cash on delivery. Every movement of COD money is written here as its own row and never changed afterwards: the customer paying the driver, the driver handing in to Ezzy, Ezzy paying the client, and any returns or reversals along the way. This is the page you open to answer "where is this money now" for a single order, driver or client. Acting on that money happens elsewhere — Driver Settlement collects from drivers, Business Payout pays clients.

What you can do here

From / To
Limits rows by the date the transaction was recorded, not the delivery date.
Type
One leg of the cash journey — collection, hand-in, client payout, return or reversal.
Payment
How the money moved: cash, POS card, Fawran, bank transfer or ATM deposit.
Driver / Business
Narrow to one driver or one client. Rows carry the client either directly or through the order behind the task.
COD Status
Filters on the order's current COD position — with the driver, handed to Ezzy, settled with the business, and so on.
Search
Free text across transaction code, reference number, order number, customer name, driver first name, business name and notes.
Summary tiles
Collected, handed to Ezzy, paid to business, returned, and the row count — all recalculated for whatever filter is active.
Running balances
Each row records the driver's COD in hand and wallet balance after that transaction, so you can walk a balance forwards or backwards.
Reset
Clears every filter and returns to the full ledger.

Transaction types

COD Collection
The customer paid the driver on delivery. Money is now in the driver's hands.
COD Deposit
The driver handed money in to Ezzy. This is the type real hand-ins carry.
Driver Settlement
The older label for the same hand-in leg; both are counted together as "handed to Ezzy".
COD Return
Cash going back out — a refund or a failed delivery whose money is returned.
Client Settlement
Ezzy paid the collected COD across to the client business.
Reversal
A client settlement that was undone. It sits next to the original rather than replacing it.

Worth knowing

  • The ledger is read-only and append-only. A mistake is corrected by recording the opposite movement, never by editing or deleting a row.
  • The tiles follow your filter. A figure quoted from this page means nothing without saying which dates and which filters produced it.
  • Rows only cover COD. Driver earnings, deductions and delivery charges are separate transaction types and are deliberately excluded here.
  • The COD in hand and wallet figures are the balances at the moment of that row, not the driver's balance today.
  • Electronic payments (POS, Fawran, bank) reached Ezzy directly and were never physical cash in the driver's hands — check Legacy Variance before chasing a driver over one.
