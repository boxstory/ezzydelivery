The shelving screen for one receipt. Each line is a product with a suggested bin: carry it there, and confirm. Confirming is what actually transfers the stock out of the receiving area and into that bin. Opening this page claims the task and starts it.

Placing an item

Suggested location
Where the system thinks it should go. It is a suggestion, not a rule.
Change
Put it somewhere else and pick that bin instead — the movement records the bin you actually used, not the suggested one.
Confirm
Deducts the units from where they were and adds them to the chosen bin, writing a transfer out and a transfer in against this task number.
Order of work
Lines are sorted by suggested bin so the page reads as a route rather than a jumble.
Finishing
Confirm every line and the task completes itself.

Good to know

  • Confirm at the bin, not at the desk. The record says the stock is there; if it is not, picking fails later.
  • A confirmed line cannot be confirmed again — it is done. A genuine mistake is corrected by a cycle count.
  • Only bins are offered as a destination; zones, racks and shelves are containers, not places stock sits.
  • Total on-hand does not change here, only its location.
