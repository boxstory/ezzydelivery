One rider's stint at one store. A shift is what makes a rider eligible for that location's batches, so this page answers two questions: was the rider actually available when we needed them, and what did they carry while they were. The left column is the record of the shift itself, the table on the right is every batch handed to them during it. The scored version of the same work — batching rate, SLA, orders per hour — is on the KPI dashboard.

What you can do here

Edit
Appears only while the shift is still Scheduled — change the planned hours or move the status. Once the rider has clocked in the button is gone.
Batch links
Each batch code opens that batch: the orders inside, their SLA deadlines and the full activity log.
Performance
The two counters on this shift — batches finished and orders delivered. They only move when a batch is completed.

Reading the record

Scheduled
The hours planned when the shift was created. Nothing is judged against these except the automatic close at the end time.
Actual
Stamped when the rider clocks in and out from the app. Blank means they never clocked in — which also means they were never offered a batch.
Assigned
When the batch was handed to the rider. From here the orders' delivery tasks sit with them as in-transit.
Completed
When the rider closed the batch. A dash means the batch is still out, or was cancelled.
COD
Cash carried on that batch. A rider's COD headroom is checked before a batch with cash is given to them.

Worth knowing

  • The table lists batches tied to this shift — a batch assigned to the rider while no shift was active is not linked here and will not show up.
  • Working hours for KPI purposes come from actual start to actual end minus break minutes, not from the scheduled times. A shift with no clock-in contributes no hours and skews orders per hour.
  • Only completed shifts are picked up by the nightly KPI run — a shift stuck on Active never gets scored.
  • A batch of one order is a single delivery that failed to find a partner. Lots of those on a shift pull the rider's batching percentage down without being their fault.
  • Nothing on this page moves money. COD and driver earnings settle on the order and payout pages.
