The chase list. Every row is a delivery that is still open — created, maybe assigned, maybe halfway across town, but not finished — sorted so the ones nobody has touched in the longest time surface first. Nothing here is delivered or cancelled; those drop off the list on their own. The counters in the header split the same queue by how long it has been since the last update, so you can see the shape of the backlog before you start working it.

What you can do here

Driver filter
Narrow the list to one approved driver — the quickest way to prepare for a call with them.
Date range
Filters on the task's delivery date. With no dates set it defaults to the last 30 days, so older stragglers are hidden until you widen it.
Status pills
Tick any number of statuses to show only those. Every change submits straight away; Clear filters resets the lot.
Sorting
Task number, last updated, driver, pickup, status and COD are all clickable — click again to flip the direction. Default is most recently updated first.
Update status
The dot button beside the status badge opens the status modal for that task, pre-filled with its driver, customer and last failure reason.
Bulk actions
Tick rows to reveal the bar: assign a driver, print, or set one status across the whole selection.
Retry
Only on failed tasks with fewer than three attempts — puts the delivery back in play after you confirm.
WhatsApp
Opens a ready-made message to the customer with their location-verification link — the usual first move on a stale row.
Open a task
Click the row or the eye icon for the full delivery-task page.

Reading a row

Fresh
Touched in the last 24 hours. Someone is on it — leave it alone.
Aging
One to three days since the last update. Worth a call today.
Stale
More than three days untouched. These are the ones that turn into complaints.
Unassigned
Shown in red in the driver column — the delivery has nobody on it at all, whatever the status says.
Pickup
The first-mile leg, if the client uses pickup automation: its stage plus the plan — Hub (drop at the warehouse), Self (same driver delivers) or Transfer (handed to another driver). A dash means there is no pickup leg.
COD
Amount to collect, then where the cash is — Not Collected, Partial, Collected, With Driver, With Ezzy or Settled.
Zone
A hollow pin means the order has no coordinates. The accuracy label says who set the point — client, staff, driver, or an estimate.

Worth knowing

  • "Updated" is the last time anything on the task changed — including a change you make here. Touching a row resets its tier to fresh whether or not the delivery actually moved.
  • The tier counts describe the whole filtered queue, not just the page in front of you.
  • Delivered, cancelled and failed tasks are not on this list by design. Look for those on the delivery-task lists or the reported tasks page.
  • Only tasks for active clients appear here.
  • The date filter uses the delivery date, not the last update — a very old task updated this morning still falls outside a narrow range.
