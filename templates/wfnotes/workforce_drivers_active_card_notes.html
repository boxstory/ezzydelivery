Every driver whose application has been approved — the people who can actually be given work. A driver only reaches this list after their documents were reviewed and accepted on Drivers › Pending; if they are later rejected, blocked or suspended they drop off it and appear under Drivers › Inactive. Use it as the roll-call for who is on the road right now and who is free to take the next job.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on first or last name, driver code, or phone number. Partial text is fine — typing part of a number finds the driver.
Read the row
Live availability, phone and WhatsApp, the driver's first registered vehicle, their star rating with the number of ratings behind it, and their wallet balance.
View
Opens the driver's full record — documents, vehicles, transactions and delivery stats. Every actual change to a driver is made there, not on this list.

Availability

Available
On shift and free — safe to hand the next task to.
On delivery
Carrying at least one job. Still assignable, but you are adding to a load they already have.
On break
Signed in but paused. Expect a delay before they pick up.
Returning
Heading back to the hub — good candidate for a pickup on the way in.
Offline
Approved but not on shift. They will not see new offers until they come back online.

Worth knowing

  • Availability is set by the driver in their own app, not by staff. A stale Available usually means someone forgot to go offline, not that they are waiting.
  • The vehicle column shows only the first vehicle on file. A driver with more than one has the rest on their detail page.
  • Wallet balance is not the same as money owed. What a driver is actually due is on Fleet › Driver Payouts, and COD they are still holding is on Fleet Transactions.
  • Suspending or blocking a driver moves them straight to the Inactive list — they will stop appearing here, but their tasks, COD and unpaid earnings stay exactly as they were.
