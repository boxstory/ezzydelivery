The day-by-day view of everything our import sources have collected. Each row is a raw order line pulled from a client's own system — a shop, a spreadsheet or a public order link — and is not a real order yet. Unlike Browse Temp Orders, which digs into one client's full history, this page pulls fresh data from every source at once and then lets you slice it by date. Use it for the morning sweep: fetch today, see what came in, and work through the New rows.

What you can do here

Sync & Refresh
Pulls from every import source right now and reloads. It runs while you wait rather than in the background, so give it up to a couple of minutes.
Sync & Fetch
The same sweep, then applies your date range and filters. A banner afterwards says how many rows were new and how many were updated.
Quick dates
Today, Yesterday, This Week and This Month fill both date boxes and submit the form for you.
Filters
Business, status and source, on top of the date range. Clear drops all of them.
Eye button
Opens the full record for that row, including the raw data exactly as the source sent it — the fastest way to see why a field came through wrong.
Select rows
Tick rows (or the header box for the whole page) to reveal the action bar at the bottom and the Import button in the header.
Preview & Import
A two-step popup: step one shows how each row will map and lets you drop rows from the batch; step two shows the same fields as editable boxes before you transfer.

Row status

New
Collected and waiting — the working queue.
Imported
Already dealt with. The row is greyed out in the table.
Skipped
Deliberately passed over and kept only for the record.

Sources

OD / GS
A OneDrive or Google spreadsheet the client keeps their orders in. The Row column is the line number in that sheet.
SP / WC
A Shopify or WooCommerce shop, pulled through the client's approved store connection.
PL
A public order link — a form the client hands out to their own customers.

Worth knowing

  • Opening the page with a date range set triggers a sync by itself. If it feels slow on first load, that is the sweep running, not a hang.
  • Dates are matched against the order date the source supplied, which is plain text. A source that writes dates in an unusual format can fall outside your range even though the row exists.
  • The status tallies and the per-client badges follow the date range only — they ignore the business, status and source filters.
  • Edits you type in the verify step are not sent with the transfer; only the row ids are. Fix the data at source and resync instead of correcting it in the popup.
  • If transferring returns an error, use Auto-Import on Browse Temp Orders instead — it is the maintained route from a raw row to a real order.
  • Ticking the header box only selects the rows on the page in front of you, not the whole filtered set.
