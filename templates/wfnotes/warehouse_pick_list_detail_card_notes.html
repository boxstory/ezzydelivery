The picking screen for one list. Products are grouped by item and bin, and sorted by location so the list reads as a route through the warehouse: go to the bin, take the quantity shown, tick it off. Several orders needing the same product appear as one line with the combined quantity.

Picking

Start picking
Moves the list to in progress and puts your name on it if nobody had claimed it.
Ticking a line
Marks the full quantity picked. Enter a smaller number when only part was found — the line stays open for the remainder.
Scan
Scanning a product's barcode finds its line instead of hunting for it by eye.
Undo a tick
Resets that line to nothing picked. If the list had completed, it drops back to in progress.
Automatic completion
When the last line is ticked the list completes itself and the pack station opens up.

Reading the page

Location column
The bin to walk to. It only appears when at least one product on the list has a bin recorded.
Orders on a line
Which orders those units are for. You do not sort them now — that happens at the pack station.
Progress
Lines picked out of lines total for the whole list, not for the product group you are looking at.
Previous / next
Steps to the neighbouring pick list without going back to the list page.

Good to know

  • Ticking does not move stock. On-hand falls when the order is fulfilled; this screen records picking progress.
  • Short pick? Enter what you actually took. Leaving the line open is what tells the next person something is missing.
