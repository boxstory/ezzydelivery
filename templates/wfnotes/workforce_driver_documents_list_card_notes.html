Every identification document held against a driver — QID, passport, licence, vehicle registration, selfie — in one list, newest first. One row (or card) is one document, so a driver with three papers on file appears three times. Documents are uploaded during driver onboarding and reviewed on the driver's own record; this page is the flat view across all of them, for finding a paper by number or checking what is about to expire.

What you can do here

Search
Matches document number, document type, and the driver's first name, last name or driver code. It fires half a second after you stop typing and reloads the list from the first page — it is a database search, not a filter of what is on screen.
Card / Table
Two ways to read the same list. Cards lead with the driver and document type; the table adds issuing authority, expiry and created date side by side, which is the better view for scanning expiry dates.
View details
Opens the single document, where the type, number, issuing authority and expiry date can be corrected and a replacement scan uploaded.
Pages
Fifty documents per page. The pager keeps whatever you searched for.

Document types

QID
Qatari ID card — the main identity document for a resident driver.
Passport
Used where a QID is not yet issued, or as a second proof of identity.
Driving Licence
Permission to drive. The expiry date here is the one that matters most.
Istimara
Vehicle registration card, tying the driver to the vehicle they use.
Selfie
The face photo captured at sign-up, checked against the ID papers.
National ID
A home-country identity card, when that is what the driver supplied.

Worth knowing

  • Nothing here approves or rejects a driver. Editing a document does not change the driver's status — that is done on the driver's verification record.
  • An expiry date shown in the past does not stop the driver working; the list does not flag or block anything, so expiries have to be chased by eye.
  • Type, number, issuer and expiry are free to edit and there is no history of what they were before — correct them, do not use them as a scratch pad.
  • Replacement scans must be JPG, PNG, GIF or PDF and under 10 MB. Uploading a new one replaces the old file rather than adding to it.
  • The list is not filtered to active drivers — papers belonging to rejected or dormant drivers stay visible.
