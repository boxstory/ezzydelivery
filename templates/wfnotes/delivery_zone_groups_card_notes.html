A zone group is a bundle of zone numbers given a name people actually use — West Bay, Industrial Area, Lusail and so on. Groups exist because nobody thinks in zone numbers: drivers pick the groups they are willing to work in, and the zone filters elsewhere in the dashboard are built from them. Each card here is one group, with its zones listed inside it. This page is for looking things up, not editing — the groups themselves are maintained in the admin.

What you can do here

Search
Matches a group's name or its description. Clear puts every group back.
Read a card
Each card shows the group's description, how many zones it holds, its display order, and chips for the zones themselves — the first ten by name and number, then a +n more chip if there are others.
Counters
Total and active groups across the whole system, regardless of what the search is showing.

What the labels mean

Active
The group is in play — it appears in driver preferences and in the group filters on the zone list and zone map.
Inactive
Kept for the record but hidden from those pickers. It still shows here.
Zones
How many zone numbers the group covers.
Display order
Where the group sits in every list that shows groups — lower numbers come first, and that is the order these cards are in.

Worth knowing

  • Groups do not affect how an address is resolved to a zone. That is done from zone boundaries, on the zone list and polygon editor. Groups only describe coverage.
  • A zone can belong to several groups, and plenty belong to none — a zone missing from every card is not an error.
  • Making a group inactive quietly removes it from driver-preference and filter dropdowns, which changes who sees what. Nothing on this page warns you about that.
  • The chips only show the first ten zones. Use the zone list, filtered by group, to see them all.
