Every row is one order — what a client sold and wants us to deliver. This is the step before delivery: orders are reviewed here, then published, which raises the delivery task a driver can be given. The same screen is reused for several different order queues — all orders, orders waiting to be published, already-published orders, fulfilled and non-fulfilled clients, and orders pending verification. The heading always reads "All Orders", so it is the menu item you clicked (and the address bar) that tells you which slice you are on; the filters, buttons and status words below behave the same on all of them.

What you can do here

Filters
Client, DL code, and a name/mobile search, plus dropdowns for order status and delivery status. Whatever is applied is echoed back as removable tags under the bar, each with its own cross.
Date buttons
Today, Yesterday, 3 Days, Last Week, Month or Custom. These filter on when the order was created, not its delivery date. Custom reveals the two date boxes.
Sorting
The COD and Date column headings are links — click to sort, click again to reverse.
Export CSV
Opens a column picker so you choose exactly which fields go into the file. The export follows the filters currently applied.
Bulk toolbar
Tick rows to enable it: parse addresses out of free text with AI, or set a status across the selection — hold for review, ready to pickup, publish to delivery, delivered, cancelled or reported.
View
The eye icon slides the order open in a side panel without leaving the list; the order number itself opens the full page.
Edit
Opens the order form — the place to fix an address, zone, COD amount or items.
Publish
Only on orders still To Review or To Publish. It creates the delivery task, which is what makes the order visible to the delivery side.
Cancel
Hidden once an order is published or already cancelled. Asks for confirmation first.
Delete
Superadmins only, and permanent — the confirmation says so.
WhatsApp
Two options: the green icon opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message and the customer's location-verification link; the paper-plane sends from one of our own numbers through the gateway, with the text editable before it goes.

Order status (OD)

To Review
Held back deliberately — something needs checking before it goes anywhere.
To Publish
Cleared, but no delivery task raised yet.
Ready / Publish
Ready for collection, or queued to go out.
Published
A delivery task exists. From here on the delivery pages own it.
Delivered
The goods reached the customer. Fulfilled is the warehouse equivalent.
Cancelled
Dropped — it will not be delivered.

Other row markers

DL badge
The delivery task's own status, shown only once a task exists. No DL line means nothing has been published.
Stock flags
Out of Stock or Low Stock — reconfirm with the client before dispatching these.
Transferred
The order came across from another client account.
Address
A hollow pin means no coordinates were ever captured. The accuracy label says who set the point — client, staff, driver, or an estimate — and anything weaker than street level is a delivery a driver will struggle with.

Worth knowing

  • Publishing is two steps. This page raises the delivery task; the task still has to be pushed to the fleet before any driver can see it. The Tasks to Fleet button in the header goes to that second backlog and its badge is a live count.
  • Cancel and delete are not the same. Cancelling keeps the order and its history; deleting removes it for good and is superadmin-only.
  • The date buttons filter on creation time. An order created last week for delivery today will not appear under Today.
  • Bulk status changes apply to every ticked row at once, with no undo — check the selected count before confirming.
  • Fixing an address here does not re-notify a driver who already has the task. If it is already published, follow it up on the delivery-task page too.
