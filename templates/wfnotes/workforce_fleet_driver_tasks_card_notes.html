One driver's whole delivery record in one place. Pick a name from the roster strip and the page fills in: the headline docket of what they have done, a chart of the last few working days, and every task that has ever been assigned to them, with the COD position sitting alongside. Use it to answer questions about a single driver — how they are performing, what they are carrying, what went wrong on a given day. Fleet-wide cash sits on COD with Fleet; a single task opens from the task number here.

What you can do here

Roster
Every approved driver, with a dot for their live duty state — green available, amber on delivery, grey anything else. Click one to load their ledger.
Search
Matches task number, order number, customer name and customer phone — within this driver's tasks only.
Status
Narrows to one delivery status, from For Review through to Cancelled.
Date range
Today, yesterday, last 7 or 30 days, or a custom from/to. It works on the task's scheduled date, not when it was delivered.
Clear
Drops the filters but keeps the driver selected.
Task number
Opens the full task — timeline, proof, address, COD and status controls. The arrow at the end of the row does the same.
Driver profile
Opens the driver's record for documents, vehicle and standing.
Print task sheet
Opens the printable round-list in a new tab, for the from date of your filter.

Reading the docket

Tasks
Everything matching the current filter — the four counts below split this total.
Delivered
Delivered and partial deliveries together.
In progress
Live work — accepted, picked up, riding, out for delivery, contacted or unreachable.
Failed
Failed, rejected and cancelled counted as one bucket.
Completion
Delivered as a share of all tasks in the filter. Unstarted work drags it down, so read it with a date range set.
Earnings
The task prices added up for delivered and partial tasks only.
COD in hand
Cash collected on these tasks and not yet handed in.
Settled
Cash from these tasks already handed in and reconciled.
Daily chart
The last ten days that had any activity in the current filter. Column height and the figure above it are the tasks dated to that day; the segments show how they closed, with grey for work never started.

Worth knowing

  • The docket and the chart are worked out on the whole filtered set, while the table shows 25 rows a page. The counts will not match what you can see, and that is correct.
  • Every figure moves with the filters. A driver's real cash position is only on screen when the date filter is clear — a date range can hide COD they are still holding.
  • Earnings here is the sum of task prices, not a payout. What is actually owed and paid is decided on the payout console.
  • The roster is approved drivers only, so a blocked or suspended driver's history cannot be reached from here.
  • This page is read-only. Status changes, COD marking and reassignment all happen on the task itself.
