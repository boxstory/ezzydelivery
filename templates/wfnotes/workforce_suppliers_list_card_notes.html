This page sorts every client by where they stand on our fulfilment service — the arrangement where we hold their stock in our warehouse and pick and pack their orders, instead of collecting from their own shop each time. The three sections are the three states a client can be in: already on the service, asking to join, or not on it. Use it to spot who is waiting for a decision and to check how much work the active clients are actually sending us.

What you can do here

Search
Matches name, business code, email or phone, and filters all three sections at once. The cross clears it.
Purchase Orders
On active rows only — opens the fulfilled-orders list already filtered to that client, so you can see what we have picked and packed for them.
View Details
Opens the client's licence record, which is where their fulfilment state is actually changed. Nothing on this page is editable.
Hero tallies
Total, Active, Requested and Non-Active counts. They follow your search, so a search narrows the numbers too.

The three sections

Active
On the service now. Sorted by most recently activated, and shows the date the service was switched on.
Requested
The client has asked for fulfilment and is waiting on us. This is the queue that needs working through.
Non-Active
Not on the service and not asking for it — most clients live here.

Order columns

Fulfilled
Orders that reached delivered or fulfilled — work finished.
Pending
Orders still in the early stages: to review, ready to pick up, or published.
Total
Every order that client has ever placed with us, not just fulfilment ones.

Worth knowing

  • Fulfilment state and account status are different things. The Non-Active section shows an Account Status column precisely because a suspended or inactive account can still appear there.
  • Fulfilled + Pending will usually not add up to Total — orders in other states (cancelled, in transit, failed) are counted in Total only.
  • Counts are lifetime figures for the client, not this month's, and are not limited to fulfilment orders.
  • To move a client between sections, open their licence record — the state cannot be changed from this page, and searching or reloading will not refresh it until it is changed there.
