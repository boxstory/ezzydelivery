The complete staff record for one client business — the seller file. The dossier at the top is the identity and the headline figures; everything below it is split into tabs, and most of it can be edited from here. This is the page to open when someone asks "who are they, are they approved, what have they sent us, and how do their orders reach us". It is reached from the Sellers list, and the back link returns there.

What you can do here

Overview
Order and COD summaries, recent activity, a completion checklist of what is still missing on the account, and shortcuts to their orders, documents and licence.
Orders
Their recent orders with status and COD, each opening the order record. Where the client has a connected store you can also pull the last 10, 25 or 50 orders live from that platform to compare against ours.
Details
Contact, address and profile information, plus their pickup locations — add, edit or remove a location without leaving the page.
Documents
Licence, ID and other files on record — view, download, replace or delete each one, with expiry dates flagged when passed.
Team
The people who can log in for this client. The dropdown on a row changes that member's status and saves immediately.
Products
Their catalogue, with a CSV import for bulk loading and a per-row delete. Where a store is connected, products can also be listed straight from the platform.
Integrations
Every store or sheet connection on the account. You can test a connection, edit its credentials, mark it verified or unverified, and delete it.
Edit
The full business form — code, name, QID, category, account status, contacts, social links, address and the fulfilment service switch. Saving reports back on the page without reloading.

Account standing

Active
Trading normally. Only active businesses appear in the pickers on the import and dispatch pages.
Awaiting
Signed up but not yet approved by staff.
Suspended
Deliberately stopped. Use it rather than deleting an account that has order history.
Inactive
Dormant — no longer trading, but the record is kept.

Verification

Verified
Documents were checked and accepted. This is about the paperwork, not about whether the account can trade.
Pending
Documents are on file but nobody has reviewed them.
Unverified
Nothing on record at all.

Worth knowing

  • Account standing and verification are two different stamps. A business can be active and unverified, or verified and suspended — do not read one from the other.
  • Setting the status to anything other than active takes the business out of the dropdowns on other staff pages, which is usually what someone means by "they disappeared".
  • Marking an integration verified is what lets it be used for importing. Unverifying it stops the imports without deleting the credentials.
  • Deleting a document or an integration is immediate and cannot be undone from here.
  • The tabs show recent records, not everything. Use View All Orders and Manage All Documents when you need the full set.
  • Deleting an order from the Orders tab is permanent and only offered to super admins.
