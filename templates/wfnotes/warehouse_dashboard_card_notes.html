The live state of the warehouse on one screen — how much stock is sitting on the shelves, what is spoken for, what is running out, and what work is still open. Nothing is edited here: every tile and row is a link into the page that does the actual job. What you see depends on your account — staff see every active warehouse, while a seller account sees only the warehouses their business is linked to.

The eight tiles

Items on hand
Every unit physically in stock, counted across all locations. Opens Stock Levels.
Reserved
Units already promised to an order and not free to sell, even though they are still on the shelf.
Low stock alerts
Products that have dropped to or below their reorder point and still have an open alert.
Seller links
Businesses connected to a warehouse, with the active count in brackets. A link is what lets a seller's stock live here.
Pending pick lists
Pick lists still waiting, assigned or being picked — in other words, picking work that is not finished.
Pending cycle counts
Stock counts that are scheduled or half done. Counts are how the recorded quantity gets corrected against reality.
Incoming stock
Units expected but not yet received. They are not on hand and cannot be picked until the receipt is confirmed.
Pending pickup tasks
Delivery tasks waiting for a driver to collect — pending, assigned or accepted. Opens the staff dashboard.

The rest of the page

Active warehouses
Each live warehouse with its code, city and layout. Not configured means no zones have been set up yet, so nothing can be stored there by location.
Storage capacity
The location tree — zones hold racks, racks hold shelves, shelves hold bins. A bin is the smallest place a product can sit.
Zone usage bar
Bins in that zone holding stock, out of the bins it has. It measures how many bins are in use, not how full each one is.
Quick navigation
Every warehouse page in one place, grouped as Inventory, Operations and — for staff — Setup and Add New.

Good to know

  • On hand is not sellable. Subtract reserved to get what is actually free to promise.
  • Figures are live. They are recounted on every page load — the timestamp beside the title is when this page was drawn.
  • Setup is staff only. Adding warehouses, locations and seller links is hidden from seller accounts.
