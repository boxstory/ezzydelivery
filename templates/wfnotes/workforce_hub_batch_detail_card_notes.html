One hub pickup batch — the driver ride that collects this client's orders from a single pickup point and drops them at a hub warehouse. The left column is the controls: who is driving, what they are paid, and where the ride has got to. The right column lists the orders on board and, once the goods reach the hub, the last-mile delivery task raised for each of them.

What you can do here

Pickup route
The collection point and the receiving hub, each with a Maps link when coordinates are on file — handy for talking a driver in.
Assign
Puts an approved driver on the ride, or swaps the one already on it. A batch sitting at Pending moves to Assigned when you do.
Driver earnings
The flat amount for the whole ride. It is saved together with the driver — see the note below.
Update status
Moves the batch by hand when the driver's own app updates have not come through — for example marking it collected or delivered to the hub yourself.
Cancel batch
Calls the ride off. You are asked to confirm, and it cannot be undone.
Orders in batch
Order number, client, customer, the COD due on it, and the last-mile task once one exists.

Batch status

Pending
No driver yet — nobody is going to collect it.
Assigned
A driver is on it but has not accepted.
Accepted
The driver has taken the ride.
In Progress
On the way to the pickup point.
Arrived
Driver is at the client's counter.
Collected
All packages are with the driver.
At Hub
Handed over at the warehouse — this is what raises the last-mile tasks. Final state.
Cancelled
Ride called off. Final state.

Worth knowing

  • Editing the earnings box on its own saves nothing — the figure is only written when you press Assign, so change the amount and reassign the same driver to correct it.
  • Reaching At Hub creates one last-mile delivery task per order and flips those orders to ready-to-pickup. The tasks arrive for review and unpublished — you still have to publish and dispatch each one.
  • At Hub and Cancelled are terminal. The driver, earnings and status controls disappear once the batch is in either state.
  • The COD shown against an order is what the last-mile driver will collect. Nothing is collected on this pickup leg.
  • Orders cannot be added to or removed from a batch here — build the ride correctly on New Batch, or cancel and rebuild it.
