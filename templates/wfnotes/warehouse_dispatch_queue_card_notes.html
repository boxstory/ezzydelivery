The handover point between warehouse and road. Orders that have been packed and are not yet in a batch wait at the top; you tick the ones a driver is taking, pick the driver, and the system creates a dispatch batch to sign the goods over with. Recent batches are listed underneath.

Creating a batch

What qualifies
An order appears once its pick list is fully packed and it is not already in another batch. Anything still being picked or packed will not show.
Driver
The list offers approved drivers currently marked available. Leaving it blank creates the batch as ready so a driver can be attached later.
COD total
Summed from the orders as the batch is created — the cash that driver is expected to come back with.
Warehouse
Taken from the orders' own pick list, not chosen by you. Batch orders that were picked in the same building.

Batch status

Ready
Created with no driver yet.
Assigned
A driver is named but the goods have not changed hands.
Handed over
Every order in the batch has been signed across to the driver, order by order, on the batch page.
Dispatched
Confirmed as gone — the driver has left the warehouse.

Good to know

  • Only the twenty most recent batches are listed; older ones are still reachable by their batch number.
  • Creating a batch does not hand anything over. That happens item by item on the batch page.
