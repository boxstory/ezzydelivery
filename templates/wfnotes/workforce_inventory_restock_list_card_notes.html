The buying shortlist. It shows only the products that have run down to the reorder level or below, pulled from every active client, so you can see at a glance what needs ordering across the whole network. It opens as one card per low product, sorted lowest stock first; clicking a card narrows the page to that client and adds a suggested order quantity for each of their short items. For the full stock picture including healthy items, use Inventory Reports.

What you can do here

Seller filter
Types-as-you-go and hides any card that does not match. It matches on everything printed on the card, so a product name or a store name both work. It filters cards only — not the table you see after drilling into a seller.
Open a seller
Click a card to load that client's short products with SKU, current stock, reorder level, suggested quantity and a priority marker.
Back to all
Back to All Sellers drops the seller filter and returns to the network-wide card list.
Suggested qty
A calculated top-up that brings the item back to roughly twice the reorder level, with a sensible minimum. It is a hint for whoever raises the order, not a commitment.

Priority

Urgent
Stock is zero. The item cannot be sold or picked at all until it is replenished.
High
Stock is at or under the reorder level but not yet zero — still sellable, but it will run out shortly.

Worth knowing

  • Products with no inventory record at all count as zero and appear as Urgent. Check the product before assuming it really sold out.
  • The reorder level is one fixed threshold for every product across every client — there is no per-product setting behind it.
  • Only clients with an active account are scanned. Suspended clients drop off this list even if their stock is empty.
  • This page places no orders and reserves nothing. It is a list to act on elsewhere; stock only moves when someone records it in the product or warehouse screens.
  • An empty page means nothing is at or below the threshold — that is the healthy state, not a loading failure.
