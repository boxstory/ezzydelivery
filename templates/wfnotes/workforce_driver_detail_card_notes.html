Everything the office knows about one driver, in one place. The header carries the two switches that matter operationally — whether the account is allowed to work at all, and whether they are online right now — and the tabs below hold their delivery history, personal details, vehicles, identity documents, coverage areas and editable settings. This is the page you open to approve someone, take them off the road, fix a wrong phone number, or check why they are not being offered tasks.

What you can do here

Account
The badge in the header is a dropdown. Picking a value writes it straight away — there is no confirmation step.
Working
Puts the driver online or offline by hand. The button only appears for approved drivers.
Work preference
Edit opens their job type and the time slabs they want to work. Saving reloads the page.
Overview
Delivery split, COD summary when they have carried any, recent tasks and recent wallet movements, plus shortcut buttons.
Deliveries
Their ten most recent tasks; rows open the task. View All goes to the full delivery list filtered to this driver.
Vehicles
Add, edit or delete a vehicle — type, plate, model, colour, status and a photo.
Documents
QID, licence, passport and similar, front and back. Images preview in a viewer; other files open in a new tab. Expired dates are flagged in red.
Zones & Notify
The notification opt-in and the zone groups this driver covers. Both feed automated task broadcasts and dispatch suggestions.
Settings
Phone, WhatsApp, bio and wallet limit. The status field here is editable by super admins only.

Account status

Active
Approved. Only these drivers can go online and take work.
Pending
Applied, not yet reviewed.
Processing
Under review — documents being checked.
Suspended
Temporarily stopped, expected back.
Deactivated
Blocked from working, account kept on file.
Rejected
The application was turned down.

Working status

Online
Available now and eligible to be offered tasks.
On delivery
Currently running a job.
On break
Working today but paused.
Returning
Heading back — usually to the hub with goods or cash.
Offline
Not working right now. The account may still be perfectly active.

Worth knowing

  • Account status and working status are different things. Account decides whether they may work at all; working is just today's availability. A driver can be Active and Offline at the same time.
  • Only super admins can change status from the Settings tab. Everyone else gets Request Status Change, which records your reason on screen as a reminder — it does not notify anyone by itself, so tell a super admin.
  • The header dropdown applies instantly and silently. Treat it as a live change, not a draft.
  • Untick Receive task notifications to silence a driver without touching their status or availability — they stay approved but drop out of broadcast messages.
  • The wallet limit is the ceiling on how much COD this driver may hold; the current figure sits in the stats strip and in Financial Overview.
  • Success rate counts delivered against failed only. Tasks still in progress are excluded, so the figure moves as open work closes.
  • Deleting a vehicle or a document happens as soon as you confirm, and cannot be undone from here.
