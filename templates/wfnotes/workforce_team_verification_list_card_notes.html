Clients can invite their own staff into their dashboard. Each card here is one such team membership — a person, the business they were added to, and the role they were given. New memberships land as Pending and the person cannot use the client's dashboard until a staff member activates them. This page is where that decision is made, and where access is taken away again if it needs to be.

What you can do here

Filter pills
Switch between Pending, Active, Rejected, Suspended and All. The page opens on Pending, so what you see first is the queue waiting on you.
Activate
Grants the person access to that client's dashboard and marks the membership verified. Shown on any card that is not already active.
Suspend
Pulls access back from someone who currently has it. Only offered on active cards. The membership stays on file and can be activated again later.
Reject
Turns down the membership. Only offered while it is pending or suspended — an active membership must be suspended first.
Card details
Name, role, email and phone, the business and its code, when the membership was added, who added it, and how complete that person's own profile is.

Membership status

Pending
Added by the client, waiting on us. No access yet.
Active
Approved and verified — the person can sign in and work in that client's dashboard.
Suspended
Access withdrawn for now, membership kept.
Rejected
Turned down. The record stays visible under the Rejected filter.

Worth knowing

  • Every button asks for confirmation, then applies immediately and reloads the page. There is no undo — reversing a decision means pressing the opposite button afterwards.
  • Activating also sets the membership as verified. Suspending or rejecting later does not clear that flag, so read the status badge rather than assuming.
  • A membership is scoped to one business. The same person invited by two clients has two separate cards and needs approving separately for each.
  • The Pending and Active counters in the hero are totals across every business and ignore the filter you are currently looking at.
  • The profile-completion percentage is that person's own profile, not the client's registration — a low number is not a reason on its own to reject.
