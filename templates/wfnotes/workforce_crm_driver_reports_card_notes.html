A read-only scorecard for the driver recruitment funnel: six headline numbers, two charts and two breakdown tables. It answers three questions — is the funnel moving, which channel brings applicants, and who is deciding on them. Everything counts driver applicants only, every one ever created; client leads have their own scorecard under CRM Business and never appear in these figures. The monthly chart is the one exception to "ever created" — it covers the last twelve months. There are no filters here, so this is the summary view: the Driver Leads table is where you go to see the individual records behind a number.

What you can do here

Stat tiles
Total, Open, Approved, Rejected, approval rate and the average time an application takes to be decided — the state of the funnel in one line.
Pipeline by stage
How many applicants are sitting in each column right now. A pile-up in one bar is where recruitment is stuck — usually documents waiting on a review.
Leads per Month
Monthly intake for the last year, stacked by where the applicants came from, so you can see which channel is growing.
Per Staff
Volume, approvals, rejections and approval rate for each staff member with applicants assigned to them.
Per Source
The same idea by channel — how many applicants each source produced and how many were approved.
Board
Back to the live recruitment pipeline.
Business Reports
The same scorecard for the sales funnel. Separate page, separate numbers — nothing is shared between the two.

How the numbers work

Approved / Rejected
Read off this board's own terminal columns, not off the word "won". Rename or add an outcome column under Board Columns and these figures follow it.
Open
Everything that is neither approved nor rejected — so an incomplete application still counts as open work.
Approval Rate
Approvals as a share of decided applicants only. Ones still in the funnel are excluded, and the tile shows a dash until something has been decided.
Days to Decide
Average days from a card being created to it landing in an outcome column, over every applicant ever decided.
Conversion
Approvals as a share of all applicants from that source, including ones still open — a deliberately harsher measure than approval rate.

Worth knowing

  • This board files itself: a card exists for every driver application, so the totals here track the real applicant pool rather than what staff typed in. Most cards carry the Driver Application source for that reason.
  • Per Staff only lists applicants that have an assignee. Unassigned work is missing from that table entirely, even though it is in the totals above.
  • Approval Rate and Conversion are calculated on different denominators, so do not compare the two columns directly.
  • The tables carry the same figures as the charts. If a chart fails to draw, read the numbers underneath instead.
