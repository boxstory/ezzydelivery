A small internal noticeboard for the operations team: what the team has to get done today, notes colleagues have left for each other, and a form for pulling a day's activity into a printable report. It is a shift-handover page rather than an analytics page — nothing you do here changes an order, a task or a driver.

What you can do here

To-do list
Each line shows who it is assigned to and when it is due. Ticking the box marks the task done immediately and reloads the page; untick to reopen it. Items are created elsewhere — you cannot add one from this page.
Team notes
Read-only messages left by staff, newest information first, each stamped with the author and the time it was written.
Report date
The single day the report should cover.
Report type
Picks a ready-made shape for the report — everything, deliveries only, COD only, or fleet performance.
Custom
Choosing Custom reveals the extra panel: tick which sections to include, and optionally narrow to one delivery status and one driver.

Report types

Daily summary
Everything that happened on the chosen day, in one document.
Deliveries
Delivery tasks only — no cash and no driver pay.
COD
Cash-on-delivery movements only.
Fleet
Driver-side performance for the day.
Custom
You choose the sections and the filters yourself.

Worth knowing

  • The report generator is not built yet — pressing Generate & Print only shows a "coming soon" message. Nothing is produced, saved or emailed.
  • For real numbers today, use the dedicated pages instead: orders and delivery lists for volume, the COD and payout consoles for money, and the driver pages for fleet performance.
  • Ticking a to-do writes the change straight away — there is no Save button and no confirmation.
  • The to-do and notes blocks show No tasks for today / No team notes available whenever there is nothing to show; that is normal, not an error.
