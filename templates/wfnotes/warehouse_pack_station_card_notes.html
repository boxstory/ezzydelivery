Where a picked batch is sorted back into individual orders. The picker collected products in bulk; here the same items are shown grouped by order so you can box each one and mark it packed. Opening this page on a completed pick list moves it to packing automatically.

How to pack

One card per order
Each card lists exactly what that customer should receive, with quantities. Box what is on the card.
Mark packed
Marks every line of that order at once — an order is packed or it is not; there is no half-packed order.
Undo
Pressing it again on a packed order unpacks it, and the list drops from packed back to packing.
Finishing
Once every order is packed the list becomes packed — that is what makes it available to dispatch.

Good to know

  • Counter at the top is orders packed out of orders in this batch, not units.
  • Something missing from a card? Go back to the pick list and correct the line there — packing does not change quantities.
  • Packing does not move stock. On-hand falls when the order is fulfilled, not when the box is sealed.
  • Next step is dispatch, where packed orders are handed to a driver and signed over.
