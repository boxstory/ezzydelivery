Every row here is an inbound quote request — a business that filled in the public 3PL pricing form and is asking what we would charge to carry their orders. This is the triage screen: scan what came in, judge whether it is worth a call, and push each lead along the sales pipeline. Opening a row's Open link takes you to the full inquiry, where you edit the details, set the stage, assign an owner and log calls.

What you can do here

Tallies
Total leads ever received, and how many of them asked for COD or for fulfilment. These count everything, not just the rows currently filtered.
Search
Matches business name, contact person, phone number or product category. It searches as you type; the small cross empties it again.
Filters
Narrow by COD requirement, fulfilment requirement, or pipeline stage. They apply the moment you change them and stack with the search.
Reset
Appears once anything is filtered, and clears all four at once.
Expand row
Click anywhere on a row to open its drawer — the services requested, the order volumes they claim, their delivery and pickup shape, and their business profile — without leaving the list.
Website
Opens the lead's own site in a new tab, when they gave one. Worth a look before you call.
Open
Goes to the full inquiry, the only place where anything can actually be changed.

Pipeline stages

New
Straight off the form, nobody has touched it yet.
Contacted
Someone has reached them, but no numbers have been put in front of them.
Quoted
A price has been sent and we are waiting on their answer.
Negotiating
They came back on the price or the terms and it is still moving.
Converted
They signed up — the lead is now a live client.
Lost
They said no, or went quiet for good.
On hold
Still interested, but not now — park it and come back.

Needs tags

COD
They want us to collect cash from their customers.
FUL
They want fulfilment — either running from outside Qatar, or a hub in Doha.
SDD
They regularly need same-day pickup and delivery.

Worth knowing

  • Every figure on a row is what the lead told us about themselves — order volumes, average order value, current courier. Treat it as a claim to be checked on the call, not as data.
  • Nothing on this page edits anything. Stage, owner and notes are all set on the inquiry itself.
  • Changing an inquiry's stage there also updates the matching lead in the CRM pipeline. The sync runs one way — editing the CRM lead does not write back here.
  • A blank cell means the lead skipped that question on the public form, not that the answer is zero.
