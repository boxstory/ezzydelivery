The review queue for people who have applied to drive but are not yet approved. Every row is an application waiting on a human decision — you open it, check the documents and identity details, and either approve or turn it down. Approving moves the driver to Drivers › Active; rejecting moves them to Drivers › Inactive. Until that decision is made the applicant cannot take any work.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on first or last name, driver code, or phone number. Partial text works.
Documents
How many files the applicant has uploaded. A count of 0 means there is nothing to review yet — chase the applicant rather than rejecting them.
Applied / Updated
The date the application was created, plus a second line with the last edit date when it differs. A recent edit usually means the applicant just added a missing document.
Review
Opens the full application — documents, vehicle and contact details. The approve and reject decision is made there, not on this list.

Status

Pending
Submitted and untouched. Nobody has started reviewing it.
Processing
Review has started but is not finished — usually waiting on a document or a verification step. Still an application, not a driver.

Worth knowing

  • Applicants in this list cannot accept tasks. Approval is the gate — until it is given, they are invisible to dispatch and to the pickup pool.
  • Most rows arrive from the public driver sign-up page, so many are half-finished registrations rather than real candidates. Check the document count before assuming someone is waiting on you.
  • The list is newest first and includes both pending and processing, so an application someone is part-way through does not sink out of sight.
  • Rejecting is not a dead end — the record stays on the Inactive list with its documents, so the decision can be revisited later.
