The starting point for paying drivers. One card per approved driver, showing the money Ezzy owes them — delivery earnings and bonuses that have not yet been attached to a payout. Clicking a card opens that driver's payout worksheet, where the amount is checked line by line and the payout is actually raised. Cash the driver is holding for us is the opposite direction and is not on this page at all; it belongs on Fleet Transactions.

What you can do here

Search drivers
Filters the cards by name as you type. It hides cards on the current page only — it does not search the whole fleet, so page through if a driver does not appear.
Open a driver
The whole card is a link to that driver's payout worksheet — the itemised list of what they are owed, and where the payout is raised.
Export CSV
Downloads the cards currently visible after your search, as shown on screen. Same limit applies — it is this page, not the full fleet.
Print
Prints the page as displayed, for a paper run-through before paying out.

The figures

Pending payout
Earnings and bonuses with no payout attached yet. It comes from the same rows the payout invoice itemises, so the two can never disagree.
Deliveries since
Completed deliveries — delivered or partially delivered — since the driver's last payout. A driver never paid yet shows their entire history.
Last paid
The date the driver's most recent payout was settled. Never means they have not been paid through this system at all.
Top tiles
Totals across the whole fleet, not just this page: unpaid money, deliveries since payout, and the number of approved drivers.

Worth knowing

  • COD is never a payout. It is money the driver holds for Ezzy, so it is deliberately excluded here. A figure on this page is always money going out to the driver.
  • Drivers earn for the job done regardless of how the customer paid, so prepaid deliveries count towards earnings exactly like COD ones.
  • Only approved drivers are listed. A blocked driver still owed money will not appear — reach them through Fleet Transactions instead.
  • Nothing on this page pays anybody. The payout is raised on the driver's worksheet, and only that action stamps the last-paid date and moves the rows out of "pending".
  • A driver with zero pending payout still gets a card. That is normal — they have simply been paid up to date.
