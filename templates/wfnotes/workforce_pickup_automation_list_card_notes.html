This is the rulebook for first-mile pickups — the leg where a driver collects goods from the client's own shop or store and brings them into our network. Every active client has one row here. When one of their orders is created, the row's rule is copied onto the pickup job that gets raised for it, which decides who sees the job and what the driver does with the goods after collecting. Live jobs are tracked on Pickup › Pool Status.

What you can do here

Auto pickup
Turn the whole thing on or off for a client. Off means their orders never raise a pickup job — someone collects by hand or the goods are already with us.
Pool mode
Who gets offered the job — that client's own drivers, or every approved driver.
After collection
What happens to the goods once the driver has them in hand.
Fleet drivers
Manage opens the client's driver list — search and add an approved driver, pause one without losing the link, or remove them. Only active drivers count towards the number shown.
Save
Saves that one row only, so nothing else on the page can be changed by accident. Every change is written to the log with your username.

Pool mode

Assigned fleet
Only the drivers listed for that client are notified, and only they can claim it. Use it for clients who want the same familiar faces at their counter. If the list is empty, nobody is told — staff have to assign it by hand.
Public pool
Every approved driver sees it in their Pickup tab and the first to claim it takes it. Fastest coverage, no guarantee of who turns up.

After collection

Drop at hub
Driver brings the goods to the default warehouse. The delivery leg is raised there for staff to dispatch normally.
Deliver by self
The same driver keeps the goods and the delivery is handed straight to them — no hub stop.
Transfer to driver
The pickup driver hands off to another driver, who must confirm before the delivery becomes theirs.

Worth knowing

  • Changes apply to orders created from now on. Jobs already raised keep the rule they were created with — reassign or return those on Pool Status.
  • No pickup job is raised when the order has no pickup location, the location is a fulfilment centre (the goods are already in our warehouse) or is inactive, the client is not active, or the order is a hub delivery.
  • Cancelling an order cancels its pickup job too, unless the goods were already collected and moved on.
  • The pickup leg carries no COD and no driver earnings — cash and pay both belong to the delivery leg.
