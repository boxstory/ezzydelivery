This is the order-level half of the verification flow — one whole order laid out for a last read before it turns into a delivery task. Where Verify Address settles only where the parcel is going, this screen covers the record end to end: who the customer is, where they are, what is in the box and how much cash is riding on it.

What you can do here

Customer
Name, email and a tap-to-call phone number, so you can check the details with the customer without copying anything out.
Address
Zone and address as the client sent them, plus a map link when one was supplied.
Order detail
Business, order date, COD amount and parcel type — and the client's own order note, shown only when they left one.
Notes
Free text for corrections you made, what the customer confirmed, or special instructions for the driver.
Verify
The submit button. The page states that verifying creates the delivery task and marks the order ready for pickup.
Cancel
Returns to the Orders list without touching the order.

The checklist

Name
Spelled the way the customer will answer to — the driver reads this out at the door.
Phone
A reachable Qatar number. A dead line is the single most common cause of a failed delivery.
Address
Complete enough to find — zone, street and building, not just an area name.
COD
Matches what the client quoted. A wrong figure here follows the cash all the way through to settlement.
Pickup
The right business and the right collection point, so the first-mile driver is not sent to the wrong shop.

Worth knowing

  • This screen is a leftover scaffold — no view or URL in the workforce app currently points at it. The live path is the Orders list: Verify Address for the address check, Submit to Task to raise the delivery task.
  • The ticks in the checklist panel are printed reminders, not live validation. They are green whatever the order actually contains.
  • The COD figure shown is what the client quoted on the order, not what a driver has collected.
  • Nothing on this page is saved until you submit, and nothing here edits the order — corrections belong on the order or task edit screens.
