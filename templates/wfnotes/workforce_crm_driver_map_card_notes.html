The driver pipeline, on a map. Every pin is one applicant, dropped where they were standing when they submitted their application — so you can see at a glance whether your recruitment is clustered in one part of Doha, whether a zone you need drivers in has anyone applying from it, and which of the people near a gap are still live.

Reading the map

Pin colour
Blue is still in the pipeline, green was approved, red was rejected or lost. Colour follows the outcome of the board column a card sits in, not the column itself — a pipeline with nine stages would need nine colours nobody can tell apart.
Which stage
Click a pin. The popup names the exact stage, the phone number, the account status, who owns the lead, and when the location was captured — then links straight to the lead.
Filters
Stage, source and owner narrow the pins, and the count on the right tells you how many of the matching leads could actually be plotted.
Fit every pin
The map frames Qatar on open. People do apply from abroad, and one pin in another country would otherwise shrink the whole Doha cluster to a dot — this button zooms out to include them.

Why a lead has no pin

No location captured
The location is recorded once, by the applicant's browser, at the moment they submit. Someone who declined the permission prompt — or applied before we started capturing it — has no pin and never will. The count under the map says how many.
Not linked to an application
A card typed in by hand, or raised from an inbound WhatsApp message, is a lead with no application behind it yet. It gets a pin once it is linked to the driver record.

Worth knowing

  • This is where someone applied from, not where they live and not where they are now. Treat it as a rough catchment signal, not an address — and check the accuracy figure in the popup before drawing conclusions from a single pin.
  • A driver's live position while working is a different thing entirely and belongs on Tasks › Live Map.
  • The map is built from the whole filtered set, not the page you are looking at, so it answers "where are my applicants" rather than "where is page one". Very large filters are capped and the note under the map says so.
  • Business leads are a separate board and never appear here.
