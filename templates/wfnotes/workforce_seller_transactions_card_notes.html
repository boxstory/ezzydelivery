The client side of COD — money we collected from customers on a seller's behalf and now owe back to them. The page opens on a card per active seller, ordered so whoever is owed the most leads. Picking a seller swaps to their order ledger, where each order shows how much cash it carried and where that cash currently sits. Paying a client is done from the Settle COD to Business link on their profile, not from this ledger.

What you can do here

Filter sellers
On the landing roster, narrows the cards by business name or account code as you type.
Open a seller
Click a card to load that account's orders. Once inside, the pencil beside the name switches to another seller.
Filters
Date range or preset, settled versus pending, delivery status (pick several at once), and a minimum/maximum COD amount. Clear resets them but stays on the seller.
Sort
Click the Order Date or COD Amount headers to sort, and again to reverse.
Settle COD
Takes you to the settlement report for this client, already scoped to them — that is where a payout to the seller is actually raised.
Export
CSV lets you choose columns; Excel gives a fixed sheet. Both cover every filtered order, not just the page you are looking at.

Where the cash sits

With driver
Collected from the customer but not yet handed in to Ezzy. Chase it on Fleet Transactions.
With Ezzy
Handed in and verified, but not yet paid across to the client. This is the money the client is waiting for.
Settled
Paid out to the client. Closed on both sides.
Collected
The customer has paid in full, before the hand-in chain has been worked through.
Pending
No cash movement recorded yet — usually an order still out for delivery.
Card rail
The bar on each roster card is the same three legs — with driver, held by Ezzy, paid out — shown in proportion, so a long unpaid middle section is a client to deal with today.

Worth knowing

  • The table lists all of the seller's orders, including prepaid and undelivered ones. The totals above it are stricter: only delivered or partly delivered orders count, so an undelivered order can never inflate what we appear to owe.
  • Only active sellers get a roster card. A deactivated client we still owe money to has to be reached through the settlement report.
  • A card showing zero held does not mean no activity — the client may simply be fully paid up. Read the rail and the order count together.
  • Nothing on this page moves money. Marking an order settled happens through the COD business settlement flow, and the figures here follow it.
