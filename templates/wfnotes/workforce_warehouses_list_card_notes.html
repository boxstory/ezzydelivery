This page decides which clients can store goods in which warehouse. Each card is one warehouse; the businesses listed inside it are the ones allowed to hold stock there. Linking a client is what gives them a collection point in that building, so their orders can be picked from our shelves instead of collected from their own shop. Only clients with the fulfilment service switched on can be linked at all.

What you can do here

Search
Matches a warehouse's name, code or city. The dropdown next to it narrows to active or inactive warehouses; the cross clears both.
Expand
Clicking a warehouse card opens the list of clients linked to it. The count on the right shows how many links it has before you open it.
Link
Opens a picker of fulfilment-enabled, active clients. Choosing one and submitting creates the link straight away and reloads the page.
Unlink
Removes a client from that warehouse after a confirmation. The link row is deleted, not just switched off.
View client
The eye icon opens that client's record, for checking their fulfilment setup before you change anything.

What the labels mean

Active
On a warehouse: the building is in use. On a link row: that client may use the warehouse right now.
Inactive
Kept for the record but not in play — no new work should be routed there.
Star
The default warehouse — where goods land when nothing else is specified, including drop-at-hub pickups.
Default
On a link row: this is that client's main warehouse when they are linked to more than one.
Linked date
When the link was created, so you can tell a long-standing arrangement from something set up this morning.

Worth knowing

  • Linking a client does more than draw a line on this page — it creates their collection point at that warehouse, which is what orders and pickup jobs are routed against.
  • Unlinking is a delete, and it takes that collection point out of service. Existing orders already pointing at it will not tidy themselves up — check before you unlink a busy client.
  • A client missing from the picker almost always means fulfilment is not enabled on their account, or the account is not active. Fix that on the client record, not here.
  • The same client can be linked to several warehouses; one of them carries the Default tag.
  • Every link and unlink is recorded against your username.
