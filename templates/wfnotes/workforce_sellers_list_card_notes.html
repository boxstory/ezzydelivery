This is the master roster of every business signed up with us — one row per client account, whether they are shipping daily or never finished registering. Use it to find a client, see where their account stands, and open their full profile. It is the starting point for most client questions: the row tells you if the account is live and whether their paperwork was ever approved.

What you can do here

Stat cards
Each tile is also a shortcut — clicking one re-filters the table to that set (active, pending verification, all, not applied, inactive).
Search
Matches business name, business email, business phone, and the email on the owner's login. It does not match the business code.
Business Status
Multi-select — tick several states at once to see, say, pending and suspended accounts together.
Verification
Multi-select on the paperwork side, independent of account status. Combined with Business Status it narrows to accounts matching both.
View
Opens the client's seller profile — the place to actually change anything. This page is read-only.

Business status

Active
A live client. Orders and automation run normally for them.
Pending
A newly registered account nobody has actioned yet.
Inactive
Dormant — signed up but not trading with us.
Suspended
Deliberately stopped, usually for payment or compliance reasons.

Verification

Not applied
No verification profile exists at all — they never started.
Pending
Documents submitted and waiting for a staff decision.
Under review
Someone has picked it up and is checking it.
Incomplete
Started but something is missing; the client needs to supply more.
Verified
Paperwork accepted.
Rejected
Paperwork turned down.

Worth knowing

  • With no filter set at all, the table shows active sellers only — not everyone. Use the Total Sellers tile (or the All option) to see the full roster.
  • Business status and verification are two separate things. An account can be active while its paperwork is still pending, and a verified client can be suspended.
  • The tiles always count the whole database; they do not follow your current filter or search.
  • Rows are newest-account-first, not alphabetical, so a client you just created sits at the top.
