A read-only copy of the address book on our business WhatsApp account. Each row ties together three things: a display name, the real phone number, and the anonymised lid alias WhatsApp hands out in place of a number. That last part is why this page exists — a lot of inbound messages arrive stamped with a lid instead of a phone, and this directory is what turns them back into a recognisable person on the WhatsApp Inbox and on a lead's conversation panel. Nothing here is editable; it is a lookup table you consult when a number does not make sense.

What you can do here

Search
Matches on name, phone number or lid, and searches the whole directory rather than just the rows currently listed.
Show all
The link in the result bar switches between the default view (contacts with a name or saved in the address book) and the full directory, which is mostly bare number-to-lid mappings.
Last sync
The subtitle shows when the directory was last refreshed — how far you can trust it.
Inbox
Back to the unknown-sender queue, which is also where the Resync button that refreshes this list lives.
Board
Back to the leads pipeline.

Reading a row

Name
The best name we hold. Where the contact's own WhatsApp name differs from the one saved in our address book, the second is shown underneath as push.
Number
The real phone number, with country code.
Lid alias
WhatsApp's privacy identifier for the same person. Messages may be stored under either form, which is why both are kept side by side.
saved
The number is in the account's own address book, not just someone who happened to message us.
business
The contact uses a WhatsApp Business account.

Worth knowing

  • This directory is a mirror of WhatsApp, so it cannot be edited here. To correct a name, fix it in the account's address book and then run Resync on the inbox.
  • The default list deliberately hides the thousands of unnamed lid mappings. If a number seems to be missing, use show all or search for it directly.
  • A contact appearing here is not a lead and creates nothing — promoting a sender to a lead is done on the WhatsApp Inbox.
  • If a lead's conversation panel is empty even though you know you have chatted, look the person up here and use their lid with Link WhatsApp Chat on the lead.
