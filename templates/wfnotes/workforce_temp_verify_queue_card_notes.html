Every row here is one WhatsApp message we owe a customer asking them to drop a location pin, so the order gets exact coordinates instead of a typed address. Jobs are raised automatically — either by the auto-import stage when an order lands, or 10 minutes after a driver marks a delivery failed. A background worker drains the queue and sends the messages; when the customer replies with a pin, the coordinates are written back to the order on their own. This page is where you watch that happen and step in when it doesn't. Orders themselves live on Temp Orders, and the rules that raise these jobs live on Stage Config.

What you can do here

Messaging switch
The master kill switch in the WAHA banner. Turning it off stops the worker sending anything at all — jobs pile up as queued until you turn it back on. Use it when the number is misbehaving or a bad batch is about to go out.
Filters
Status and kind chips narrow the list in one click; the bar below filters by client, created-date range, and a search across phone, order number, and customer name. Reset clears everything.
Send now
Sends a queued or failed job immediately instead of waiting for the next worker tick or its scheduled time.
Re-send
Puts an already-sent job back in the queue so the customer gets the message again. Also clears the attempt count, so a job that ran out of retries gets a fresh start.
Re-queue all
The button in the red banner does the same thing to every failed job at once, one after another.
Apply pin
Copies the location the customer sent onto the order. Use Apply pin for this job's own order, or the shuffle button to attach the same pin to a different order by its numeric ID.
Mark verified
Closes the job by hand when you've sorted the address another way — by phone, say. No coordinates are written.
Cancel
Kills the job so nothing is ever sent for it. Use it when the order is void or the customer has already been reached.

Status

Queued
Waiting for the worker. Nothing has been sent yet.
Sent
The message went out and we're waiting on the customer's reply.
Verified
Done — coordinates were applied, or someone closed it by hand.
Manual review
A reply came back but couldn't be matched to the order on its own. These are the rows that actually need you.
Failed
Sending gave up after the retry limit. The reason is in Last Error.
Cancelled
Stopped by staff. It will never send.

Kind

Import
Raised when the order was imported, to get a pin before dispatch.
Recovery
Raised after a driver failed the delivery. The message carries the driver's reason plus a fresh link — hover the tag to read the note.

Worth knowing

  • While messaging is paused, Send now is refused — it is not a way around the switch. Turn messaging back on first.
  • Recovery jobs sit on a 10-minute hold after the failure so the driver can undo. The Scheduled column shows the earliest send time.
  • Applying a pin overwrites the order's coordinates, marks them exact, moves it to Address verified, and writes a line into the order's history. It cannot be undone from here.
  • Applying a pin to a different order also moves the job onto that order — the row stops belonging to the one it started with.
  • Verified and cancelled jobs can't be cancelled again, and cancelled jobs can't be re-sent.
  • The status counts on the chips ignore the status filter, so they stay useful while you're looking at one slice.
