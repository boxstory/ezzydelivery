Working one customer return from parcel to decision. Mark the return received when it lands, then judge each item: what condition it came back in, and what should happen to it. The inspection decision is the moment stock either goes back on the books or does not.

The two steps

Mark received
Applies to the whole return at once and moves it to received. It records arrival, not condition.
Condition
What you are looking at — sellable, damaged, and so on. It is written onto the line and onto the stock movement if it restocks.
Disposition
What to do with it. Restock puts the units straight back into the warehouse's stock with a return movement against this RMA number; any other choice leaves stock untouched.
Automatic resolve
Once every line is inspected, the return closes as resolved by itself.

The counters

Received / inspected
Lines arrived, and lines judged. Inspected is the one that decides when the return closes.
Restocked
Lines that actually went back into stock — always less than or equal to inspected, since not everything is sellable.

Good to know

  • Restock needs a warehouse and an existing stock row for that product. Without them the line is marked inspected but nothing is added.
  • Units go back at the location the stock row already uses, so check the shelf matches what the system now claims.
  • Inspect honestly. Restocking damaged goods puts an unsellable unit into the next customer's order.
