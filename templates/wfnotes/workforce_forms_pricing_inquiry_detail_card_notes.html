This is the full record of one 3PL pricing inquiry — everything a prospective client told us on the public quote form, plus everything we have done about it since. It is the working page for a lead: read what they need, correct what they got wrong, move them along the pipeline, hand them to a colleague, and keep a written trail of every call. Rows on Pricing Inquiries open here.

What you can do here

Call / WhatsApp
Dials or opens a chat with the business contact number straight from the hero.
Edit
Flips the left column from reading to editing — contact and business details, products and services, order volumes, delivery and operations, pickup details. Save changes writes them; Discard drops back to reading without saving.
Pipeline status
The stage buttons in Manage Inquiry. Pick one, then press Save Changes — clicking a stage alone does not commit it.
Assign to
Hands the lead to a staff member, or clears the owner with — Unassigned —. The owner also shows in the hero line.
Staff notes
One running internal note on the lead. It is overwritten each time you save, so it is a summary, not a history.
Activity log
Adds a dated entry to the timeline — either a plain Note or a Follow-up for a call you made. This is where the history belongs.
Timeline
Every note, follow-up and system-logged change, newest first, with who did it. You can delete your own entries; a superadmin can delete any.

Badges in the hero

Status
Where the lead sits in the pipeline — new, contacted, quoted, negotiating, converted, lost or on hold.
Partial
They abandoned the public form part-way. Expect gaps, and fill them in on the first call.
COD
They want us collecting cash from their customers, which changes both pricing and settlement.
Fulfilment
They want storage and picking, not just delivery — running from abroad, or a hub in Doha, or both.
Start immediately
They said they want to begin at once. Treat these as the ones to ring first.

Worth knowing

  • The Manage Inquiry panel and the Edit form are two separate saves. Each has its own button, and neither picks up what you typed in the other.
  • Every change is logged for you — a stage change, a reassignment, a notes update or a batch of edited fields all drop an automatic entry into the timeline with your username.
  • Changing the stage here also updates the matching lead in the CRM pipeline. The sync runs one way, so the CRM never writes back over what you set here.
  • Everything the lead supplied is self-reported. Volumes and order values are sales claims until someone verifies them.
  • Editing a field rewrites the inquiry itself. There is no per-field history — only the summary line the timeline keeps, and it records at most the first ten changes in a save.
