Where a driver's pay for a job is agreed. Every completed delivery — delivered or partially delivered, COD or prepaid — arrives here with a calculated figure. You check the job, adjust the figure if it does not fit, and publish it, which is the moment the money is credited to the driver's wallet. What happens after that is a payout, handled on Driver Earnings. The mirror of this page for what the client is charged is Client Charge Verification.

What you can do here

Filters
Narrow to one driver, one verification status, and a period of today, 7, 30 or 90 days. It opens on pending work.
Verified box
The editable figure — what the driver will actually be paid. It starts at the calculated amount; type over it when the job was not standard.
Set all selected
Writes one amount across every ticked row at once, for a batch that should all pay the same.
Verify
Marks the ticked rows as checked and saves your figures, stamping your name against them. No money moves yet.
Publish
Commits the figure and credits the driver's wallet. This is the one that actually pays.
Reject
Parks a row as not payable — a job that should not earn. It stays visible under the rejected filter.
Log
Opens the driver's activity trail for that task, so you can see what actually happened before agreeing a fee.
Row detail
Task number and date, driver, client, pickup and drop with their zones, job type, and the COD collected or Prepaid.

The four states

Pending
Delivered but nobody has agreed the fee. This is the queue to work through.
Verified
A figure has been checked and saved, but nothing has been credited. Still editable.
Published
Credited to the driver's wallet. The amount box locks.
Rejected
Ruled not payable.
Calculated
The system's own figure, read-only: pick & drop pays 80% of the delivery price, an ordinary delivery pays the flat rate shown in the header.
Pending earnings
The tile at the top — what the unagreed rows in your current filter would cost if you published them as they stand.

Worth knowing

  • Publishing moves money. It writes a wallet transaction against the driver, so fix the amount before you publish, not after — a published row cannot be edited here.
  • Re-publishing a row already published will not credit it twice; it is skipped and reported back as an error line. Treat that as a safety net, not a workflow.
  • Earnings do not depend on cash. A prepaid delivery earns exactly like a COD one, and COD collected is shown only as context for judging the job.
  • Anything typed in an amount box is saved as part of Verify or Publish — pressing neither leaves the change on screen only.
  • Maximum 500 rows per action, and a single amount above 10,000 QAR is refused as a typo. Fifty rows per page, and ticks do not survive turning the page.
