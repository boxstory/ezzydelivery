Every row here is a delivery task — the last-mile leg of one order, the thing a driver actually accepts and completes. This same table is used for several different task views: all tasks, fulfilled and non-fulfilled clients, incomplete tasks, tasks already published to the fleet, and reported (rejected/cancelled) tasks. The title at the top of the page tells you which slice you are looking at — the controls and the status words below are the same everywhere. Orders live one step earlier, on the Orders pages; a task only exists once its order has been published.

What you can do here

Filters
The Filters bar opens a panel for task/order/reference code, customer name or mobile, driver name, date range, client, client status and task status. It only appears on the slices that support it, and it opens by itself when a filter is already applied. Apply reloads the list, Reset clears everything.
Quick presets
One-click shortcuts for Today, Yesterday, and the common task statuses. They fill the filter fields and submit immediately.
Zone / accuracy
Only on the all-tasks list: narrow to deliveries with no zone, or by how the coordinates were obtained. Arriving from the dashboard's location card also shows a yellow banner for the combined "location issues" filter.
Sorting
Click a column heading — seller, task number, order, date, last updated, customer, COD, status or driver. Sorting is applied to the whole filtered result, not just the page you are on.
Open a task
Clicking anywhere on a row (or the eye icon) opens the full delivery-task page.
Bulk actions
Tick rows to reveal the action bar: assign a driver, print, publish to fleets, publish to the app, set a status on all of them, or export the selection. The AI toolbar next to it can parse addresses out of free text for the selected rows.
Update status
The small dot button beside the status badge opens the status modal for that one task, carrying the driver, customer and any failure reason.
Publish
The paper-plane icon appears only on rows that are still Not Published and pushes that task to the fleet drivers.
Retry
Shown only on failed tasks with fewer than three attempts; it puts the delivery back in play. After three it is replaced by Max Retries and the task cannot be retried again.
WhatsApp
Opens a pre-written message to the customer with their location-verification link already in it.

Task status

Before a driver
For Review and Pending — the task exists but nobody owns it yet.
With a driver
Assigned (given to a driver), Accepted (the driver took it), Picked Up, Start Ride, Out for Delivery and In Transit.
Customer contact
Contacted & Confirmed means the customer is expecting it; Non Reachable means the driver could not get hold of them.
Closed
Delivered, Partial Delivery, Failed, Rejected, Cancelled and Drops Own Lost.

Other columns

Published
Whether the task has been pushed out to the fleet. A task that is not published is invisible to drivers no matter what its status says.
Zone / address
The pin is solid when the order has coordinates and hollow when it does not. Below it, the zone/street/building numbers and the accuracy label — who or what set the point: client, staff, driver, exact building, street, landmark, zone centre or an AI estimate.
COD / verify
The cash to collect, plus where that cash currently is: Not Collected, Collected, With Driver, With EZZY or Settled with the client.

Worth knowing

  • Publishing and task status are two separate things. A task can read Assigned and still be Not Published — nobody in the fleet can see it.
  • The Unpublished button in the header is the shortcut to everything still waiting to go out to the fleet; the number on it is a live count, not a filter of this list.
  • Most of these lists only show tasks for clients whose account is active, so a task can be missing here simply because its client was deactivated.
  • Bulk status changes apply to every ticked row at once and are not undone by refreshing — check the selection count before you confirm.
  • A row with no zone or a landmark/zone-centre coordinate is one a driver will struggle to find. Fix it on the task page rather than leaving it for the driver.
