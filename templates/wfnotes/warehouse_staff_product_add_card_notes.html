Puts a product in a seller's catalogue without waiting for the seller to add it themselves. It creates the item only — no stock. Units reach the shelf through Receive Stock, which is also what writes the movement into the ledger.

  1. Choose the business the product belongs to.
  2. Fill in the details — name is the one that matters, the rest can follow later.
  3. Save. The product id is generated for you, scoped to that seller.
  4. Receive stock against it to put units on the shelf.

Fields worth care

Business
Cannot be changed afterwards from here — a product created against the wrong seller has to be fixed in the product admin.
SKU and barcode
Optional, but they are how the item is found on the receiving and picking screens. Worth entering now.
Photo
Shows on the receiving sheet and the stock list, so whoever handles the box can match it to the row.
Product id
Generated on save as <business>-<counter>. Not something you set.

Good to know

  • It starts at zero. A new product holds no stock anywhere until it is received into a warehouse.
  • Save & Add Another keeps the business selected, for typing a delivery note's worth of new items in one go.
  • The seller sees it too. This is their catalogue, not a warehouse-only list — it appears in their own product pages.
  • Staff only. The page can write to any seller's catalogue, which is why it is not open to seller accounts.
