Every client who keeps their orders in a Google Sheet has a row here. A row ties one sheet tab to one business, and syncing it reads the rows and stages them as temp orders — it never creates real orders on its own. Staff still review and import those rows on the Temp Orders page. A sheet only produces usable rows once its columns have been mapped to our fields in the Mapping Manager.

What you can do here

Add source
Pick the business and paste the sheet's link. Share the sheet with the integration's Google account first, or reading it will fail. A tab GID is optional — blank uses the one in the link, or the first tab.
Mapping
Opens the Mapping Manager for that business's Google Sheet platform, where each column is pointed at one of our order fields.
Sync
Reads the sheet immediately and reports how many rows were created and updated. Disabled while the source is inactive.
Edit
Changes the sheet URL or the tab GID. The business a source belongs to cannot be changed — delete it and add it again.
Power button
Flips the source between active and inactive. Inactive sources are skipped by the scheduled sync and cannot be synced by hand.
Delete
Removes the source and every temp order staged from it. Orders already imported are untouched.
Import history
The header link opens the import log already filtered to Google Sheet runs — go there to see why a sync produced nothing.

Reading a row

Headers
How many column headings were seen the last time the sheet was read. Zero means we have never successfully opened it.
Mapping
How many columns are mapped to our fields. Amber zero means the sheet will be skipped on sync — set the mapping first.
Status
Active is in the sync rotation, Inactive is parked. New sources are created active.
Last sync
When a row was last staged from this sheet, and how many temp orders it has produced in total. Never means nothing has ever come through.

Worth knowing

  • Syncing stages rows only. Nothing reaches a customer until someone imports them from Temp Orders.
  • A sheet the integration account cannot open fails every time, silently on the schedule and loudly here — use Sync to see the real error.
  • Deleting a source also deletes its staged temp orders, so anything not yet imported is lost and has to be re-synced after re-adding.
  • Rows already staged are matched and updated on the next sync, so fixing a mistake in the client's sheet before import does carry through.
  • Changing the tab GID points the source at a different tab — the previously staged rows stay, which can look like duplicates.
