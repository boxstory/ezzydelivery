Everything about one fulfilment centre: its details, the stock it is holding, and the internal locations it has been given. This is also where the capacity setup is reached from — the step that turns an empty building into addressable bins.

What is on the page

Stock summary
Total units on hand and how many of them are reserved, across every product in this building.
Locations
The zones, racks, shelves and bins that exist here, grouped by type. An empty list means capacity was never generated.
Configure capacity
Sets how the building is divided and how locations are named, then previews and generates them.
Edit
Name, address, contact, coordinates, active and default flags. Superadmin only.

Good to know

  • Reserved is part of on hand, not extra. Subtract it to see what is genuinely free in this building.
  • Only bins hold stock. Zones, racks and shelves exist to address them.
  • Regenerating locations can delete existing ones if you choose that option — check nothing is stored in them first.
  • Seller links live on their own page; a warehouse with none cannot receive stock.
