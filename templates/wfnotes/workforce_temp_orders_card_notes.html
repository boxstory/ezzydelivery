This is the staging area between a client's own system and ours. Each row is one line pulled from an external source — a OneDrive spreadsheet, a Google Sheet, a Shopify or WooCommerce store, or a public order-entry link — and nothing here is a real order yet. Staff check the rows, then import the good ones, which is what creates the actual order records. Rows are grouped by business, ten at a time. Only businesses with temp orders switched on appear here.

What you can do here

Sync now
Pulls fresh rows from every configured source straight away instead of waiting for the scheduled sync. New rows land as New; rows already staged are updated in place.
Filters
Narrow by source, status or business. The counts in each dropdown are the totals across all enabled businesses, not the filtered view. Clear appears once a filter is on.
Sorting
Date, business and status column headers sort the whole list. The default is newest date first.
Eye button
Opens the full detail of that row, including the raw source line it came from and, once imported, a link to the order it became.
Preview & import
Tick rows, then step through the mapping preview and the field check before transferring them into real orders.
Auto-import
Imports the ticked rows without the field-by-field check. You first pick which pipeline stages to run; the defaults come from each business's own settings, and mixed selections are flagged.
Delete
Removes staged rows — one from its row button, or all ticked ones from the bulk bar. Only rows that are not yet imported can be deleted.
Next 10
Pages within one business only, so working through one client's backlog does not move everyone else's rows.
Shortcuts
Browse is the flat searchable list, Config is the per-business temp-order settings, and the Pipeline Queue tiles jump to the publish and address-verification queues.

Row status

New
Staged and waiting. These are the rows the New Orders tile and the sidebar badge count.
Imported
Already turned into an order. The badge links to that order where the link was recorded.
Skipped
Deliberately passed over — a duplicate, a test line or an unusable row. It stays visible but is not queued for import.

Source badges

OD / GS
OneDrive workbook or Google Sheet — spreadsheet rows read through a saved column mapping.
SP / WC
Shopify or WooCommerce store orders pulled over the store's API.
PL
Public link — a row someone typed into the client's shared order-entry form.
