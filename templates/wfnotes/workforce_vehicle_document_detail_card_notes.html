This is one driver's vehicle sheet — who they are at the top, then a block for each vehicle they have registered with us, newest first. You land here from the Vehicle Documents list, usually because a plate is wrong, a driver has swapped bikes, or you need to take a vehicle out of service without touching the driver's own account. Everything on the driver block is read-only; the vehicle blocks are the part you can change.

What you can do here

Edit Vehicle
Opens a small form over the page for that one vehicle. Save writes the change straight away and reloads the sheet; Cancel, the Escape key, or a click outside the box closes it and changes nothing.
Plate number
Required. This is what staff and clients match against on delivery photos, so keep it exactly as it reads on the vehicle.
Type
Bike, car, van, pickup, pickup 3-ton, or pickup for big items. This decides what the driver can physically carry, so it matters more than it looks.
Model & colour
Free text, used for identifying the vehicle at a gate or on a doorstep. Both can be left blank.
Status
Active or inactive. Use inactive to park a vehicle that is off the road without deleting its record or its history.
Back to Vehicles
Returns to the full vehicle register. Note it goes back to an unfiltered list — any search you came from is not kept.

Worth knowing

  • You can edit but not add or delete from this page. Drivers register their own vehicles; there is no remove button here on purpose, so history is never lost.
  • Every field in the form is saved on submit, including the ones you did not touch — so if the form opened with a blank model or colour, saving keeps it blank.
  • The driver's own status at the top is read-only. Change it on the driver record or through the verification page; approving a driver there also flips their inactive vehicles to active.
  • Despite the page name, no document file is uploaded or stored here — this is registration detail only.
