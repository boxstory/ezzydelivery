A read-only scorecard for the whole CRM pipeline: six headline numbers, two charts and two breakdown tables. It answers three questions — is the funnel moving, which source is worth the effort, and who is closing. Everything is counted across every lead ever created, business and driver alike; the only exception is the monthly chart, which covers the last twelve months. There are no filters here, so this is the summary view — the Leads table is where you go to see the individual records behind a number.

What you can do here

Stat tiles
Total, Open, Won, Lost, win rate and the average time a lead takes to close — the state of the pipeline in one line.
Pipeline by Stage
How many leads are sitting in each stage right now. A pile-up in one bar is where the funnel is stuck.
Leads per Month
Monthly intake for the last year, stacked by where the leads came from, so you can see which channel is growing.
Per Staff
Volume, wins, losses and win rate for each staff member with leads assigned to them.
Per Source
The same idea by channel — how many leads each source produced and how many of them were won.
Board
Back to the live pipeline.

How the numbers work

Open
Everything that is neither Won nor Lost — so On Hold leads still count as open work.
Win Rate
Won as a share of decided leads only. Leads still in the pipeline are excluded, and the tile shows a dash until something has closed.
Days to Close
Average days from a lead being created to it being marked Won or Lost, over every lead ever closed.
Staff win rate
Wins as a share of that person's decided leads, on the same basis as the headline rate.
Conversion
Wins as a share of all leads from that source, including ones still open — a deliberately harsher measure than win rate.

Worth knowing

  • Driver leads are counted here alongside business leads, so Won includes approved drivers. These are pipeline figures, not pure sales figures.
  • A card is created automatically for every driver application and filed under the Manual source — that inflates Manual volume well beyond leads staff actually typed in.
  • Per Staff only lists leads that have an assignee. Unassigned work is missing from that table entirely, even though it is in the totals above.
  • Win Rate and Conversion are calculated on different denominators, so do not compare the two columns directly.
  • The tables carry the same figures as the charts. If a chart fails to draw, read the numbers underneath instead.
