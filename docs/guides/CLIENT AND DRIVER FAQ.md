⭐ 2. How does the delivery process work?

You send us your daily order list (API integration or manual).

Our team picks up from your store/warehouse or from our fulfilment centre.

Packages are sorted, routed, and dispatched in batches.

Drivers deliver to customers with live tracking & notifications.

COD is collected and settled weekly.

We will handle:

Storage

Picking & packing

Invoice printing

Delivery dispatch

COD management

This is ideal for brands outside Qatar wanting to sell locally.

⭐ 4. Can I send items from another country to Qatar for fulfilment?

Yes! You can ship your products in bulk to our warehouse.
We manage everything after arrival—stocking, order processing & delivery.
You don’t need any local staff in Qatar.
