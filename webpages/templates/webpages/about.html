Our Story

Founded in 2017, EzzyDelivery began with a simple mission: make e-commerce delivery easy and reliable for businesses across Qatar. What started as a small delivery service in Doha has grown into Qatar's trusted logistics partner, serving over 600 businesses and completing 1,000+ deliveries daily.

Our journey started when we noticed the challenges faced by online sellers in Qatar - unreliable delivery partners, poor tracking systems, and complicated COD (Cash on Delivery) management. We set out to build a solution that would solve these pain points with technology, professional drivers, and a customer-first approach.

Today, we operate across all major areas in Qatar including Doha, Al Wakrah, Lusail, Al Rayyan, and West Bay. Our fleet of professional drivers, combined with our advanced tracking system and ShipDay DMS integration, ensures that every package is delivered safely and on time.
