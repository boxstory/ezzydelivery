What EzzyDelivery does

EzzyDelivery is a technology-driven last-mile logistics platform based in Doha, Qatar. It connects online sellers, e-commerce stores, and businesses with a managed fleet of delivery drivers for reliable parcel delivery across Qatar.

  • Same-day delivery — pickup within 2 hours, delivered the same day across Doha and beyond.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) — secure cash collection with daily settlement and reconciliation reports.
  • 3PL & fulfillment — warehousing, pick-pack-ship, and inventory management in Doha.
  • Express courier — priority handling for urgent documents and parcels.
  • E-commerce integrations — native Shopify and WooCommerce sync plus a REST API.
  • Real-time tracking — live GPS tracking with SMS and WhatsApp notifications.

Frequently asked questions

What does EzzyDelivery do?

EzzyDelivery is a B2B delivery and logistics platform in Qatar. It provides same-day delivery, COD collection with daily settlement, 3PL fulfillment, and last-mile delivery for e-commerce and online sellers across Qatar.

Which areas in Qatar does EzzyDelivery cover?

All of Qatar, including Doha, Al Rayyan, Umm Salal, Al Wakrah, Lusail, Al Khor, and Al Daayen.

Does EzzyDelivery support Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes. COD is available across Qatar, with amounts settled daily to the seller's bank account and full reconciliation reporting.

Can EzzyDelivery integrate with Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes. EzzyDelivery offers native Shopify and WooCommerce integrations plus a REST API. Orders sync automatically and tracking updates push back to the store in real time.
