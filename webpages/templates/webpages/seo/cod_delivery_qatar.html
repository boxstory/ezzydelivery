COD Service FAQs

COD service allows your customers to pay with cash when they receive their order. Our driver collects the payment on your behalf and transfers it to your account within 3-5 business days.

Our COD fee is only 2% of the collected amount - the lowest rate in Qatar. For example, if you collect QAR 100, you pay QAR 2 as service fee.

COD payments are settled within 3-5 business days via bank transfer. You can track all pending and settled payments in your dashboard.

Our drivers carry change for COD orders. They can provide change up to QAR 500 for customer convenience.

Standard COD limit is QAR 5,000 per order. For higher amounts, please contact our team for special arrangements.
