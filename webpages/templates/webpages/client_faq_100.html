Core Services
Questions 1-12 | Essential service information

1What services does Ezzy Delivery Qatar provide?

Ezzy Delivery Qatar provides comprehensive logistics solutions including:

  • Last-Mile Delivery - Fast and reliable delivery to end customers
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) - Secure payment collection service
  • Reverse Pickup / Returns Handling - Efficient returns management
  • Same-Day / Next-Day Delivery - Flexible delivery timeframes
  • Fulfilment & Storage - Complete warehousing solutions
  • Bulk Shipment Handling - High-volume order processing
  • E-commerce API Integrations - Seamless platform connectivity
  • Dedicated Support & Tracking - Real-time order visibility
2How does the delivery process work?

Our streamlined delivery process follows these steps:

  1. You send us your daily order list (via API integration or manual upload)
  2. Our team picks up from your store/warehouse or from our fulfilment centre
  3. Packages are sorted, routed, and dispatched in optimized batches
  4. Drivers deliver to customers with live tracking & automated notifications
  5. COD is collected securely and settled weekly with detailed reports
3Do you offer fulfilment and storage services?

Yes! Our comprehensive fulfilment service includes:

  • Secure Storage - Climate-controlled warehouse facilities
  • Picking & Packing - Professional order preparation
  • Invoice Printing - Automated documentation
  • Delivery Dispatch - Coordinated shipping
  • COD Management - Complete payment handling

This is ideal for brands outside Qatar wanting to sell locally without physical presence.

4Can I send items from another country to Qatar for fulfilment?

Absolutely! You can ship your products in bulk to our warehouse. We manage everything after arrival—stocking, order processing & delivery. You don't need any local staff in Qatar.

Many of our clients successfully operate from Kuwait, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Turkey, India, and other countries.

5Do I need to be physically in Qatar to use your services?

No! You can operate your business completely remotely. Our clients successfully run their Qatar operations from:

  • 🇦🇪 UAE
  • 🇹🇷 Turkey
  • 🇮🇳 India
  • 🇶🇦 Within Qatar

We handle all local operations, allowing you to manage your business from anywhere in the world.

Pricing
Questions 13-22 | Transparent pricing information

13How is the delivery price calculated?

Price is calculated based on:

  • Expected Volume - determining your pricing slab
  • Distance Zone - determining the base rate

You are charged only for successful deliveries (Per Successful Delivered model).

14What are the pricing slabs?

Standard Low-Volume (10+ weekly):

  • Less than 10 km (Doha): QR 15
  • 10.1-15 km (Outside Doha): QR 18-20
  • 15.1-25 km (Outer Doha): QR 20

Mid-Volume (150+ monthly):

  • Less than 10 km: QR 20
  • 10.1-20 km: QR 25
  • 20.1-30 km: QR 30

High Volume (300+ weekly):

  • Flat rate of QR 20 for all deliveries up to 25 km!
15Do you charge for failed deliveries?

No! We operate on a "Per Successful Delivery" model. You only pay for successfully completed deliveries. Failed delivery attempts are not charged.

16Are there any hidden fees?

Absolutely not! We believe in complete transparency. Our pricing includes:

  • Delivery fee (based on distance and volume)
  • COD collection (no handling fee for cash)
  • Basic packaging materials
  • GPS tracking and notifications

Additional charges only apply for:

  • Express delivery surcharge (QR 10 for under 20km)
  • Card payment processing (2.5% service fee)
  • Custom packaging requests
  • Special handling requirements
17How does Cash on Delivery (COD) pricing work?
  • No Handling Fee for COD transactions paid in cash
  • Card payments incur a 2.5% service fee
  • Alternative digital payments via Fawran accepted
  • COD funds are settled weekly with detailed reports
18What is the Express Delivery surcharge?
  • Additional QR 10 for deliveries under 20 km
  • QR 1 per additional kilometer for deliveries over 20 km
  • Guaranteed 4-hour delivery window
19What is the pricing for remote areas?
  • Low-volume: Flat rate of QR 35 (max 3-day delivery)
  • High-volume: QR 20 base + QR 5 per additional km past 25 km
20Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes! Our pricing automatically scales with your volume:

  • 10+ weekly orders: Standard rates
  • 150+ monthly orders: Mid-volume discounted rates
  • 300+ weekly orders: Premium flat-rate pricing (QR 20 up to 25km)

As your business grows, your per-delivery cost decreases!

21How much does warehouse storage cost?
  • First storage unit: QR 250 per month
  • Additional units: QR 150 per month each
  • Unit size: 100 x 100 x 50 cm (500,000 cm³)
22What payment methods do you accept from clients?

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

  • Bank transfer
  • Credit/Debit cards
  • Digital wallets (Fawran)
  • Monthly invoicing for established clients
