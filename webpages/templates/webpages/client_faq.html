Client FAQ

For eCommerce Sellers & Online Stores

What services does Ezzy Delivery Qatar provide?

Ezzy Delivery Qatar provides:

  • Last-Mile Delivery
  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • Reverse Pickup / Returns Handling
  • Same-Day / Next-Day Delivery
  • Fulfilment & Storage
  • Bulk Shipment Handling
  • E-commerce API Integrations
  • Dedicated Support & Tracking
How does the delivery process work?

The delivery process is simple:

  1. You send us your daily order list (API integration or manual)
  2. Our team picks up from your store/warehouse or from our fulfilment centre
  3. Packages are sorted, routed, and dispatched in batches
  4. Drivers deliver to customers with live tracking & notifications
  5. COD is collected and settled weekly
Do you offer fulfilment and storage?

Yes! You can send bulk inventory to our warehouse. We handle:

  • Storage
  • Picking & packing
  • Invoice printing
  • Delivery dispatch
  • COD management

This is ideal for brands outside Qatar wanting to sell locally.

Can I send items from another country to Qatar for fulfilment?

Yes! You can ship your products in bulk to our warehouse. We manage everything after arrival—stocking, order processing & delivery. You don't need any local staff in Qatar.

Do I need to be physically in Qatar to operate?

No. Many of our clients operate from Kuwait 🇰🇼, Jordan 🇯🇴, Saudi 🇸🇦, UAE 🇦🇪, Turkey 🇹🇷, India 🇮🇳, and within Qatar 🇶🇦. You can run your business remotely while we handle all operations.

Do you integrate with Shopify, WooCommerce, and other stores?

Yes, we provide:

  • Shopify Integration
  • WooCommerce Integration
  • API Integration
  • Order Import Solutions
  • Automated label creation
  • Automated status updates

Manual upload is also available.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) settlement work?

COD amounts are collected and settled every week with a detailed report of:

  • Delivered orders
  • Returned orders
  • Amount collected
  • Pending balances
What is your delivery timeframe?
  • Same Day (within Doha limits)
  • Next Day (Qatar-wide)
  • Express 2–4 Hours Delivery (optional)
Do you deliver all over Qatar?

Yes. Ezzy Delivery covers Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Rayyan, Umm Salal, Al Khor, Lusail, and all major zones & areas including remote locations.

How can I track my orders?

You get access to a Client Portal with:

  • Order status
  • Driver route
  • Delivery proof
  • Payment status
  • Real-time updates

Customers also receive delivery notifications.
