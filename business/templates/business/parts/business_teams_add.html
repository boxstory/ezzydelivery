How to Add a Team Member

Step 1: Find the person you want to add using one of these:

  • Email — Their registered email (e.g. john@example.com)
  • EZZY ID — Their unique code (e.g. EZZY2026303473), found on their profile
  • User ID — Their numeric user ID

Step 2: Choose a role for the member:

  • Manager — Full access to orders, products, customers, reports
  • Staff — Can create and edit orders and products
  • Viewer — Read-only access to business data

Requirements: The person must have an EzzyDelivery account with a fully completed profile (100%). Their profile must include:

  • Username
  • First name & Last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Zone / Area
  • Address
  • Nationality
  • Date of birth

After adding: The team member will be in pending status. A staff member must verify and activate their team membership before they can access the business dashboard.

Ask them to sign up and complete their profile at ezzydelivery.qa first.
