Frequently Asked Questions
How much can I earn as a driver?
Earnings vary based on the number of deliveries you complete. On average, drivers earn QAR 50+ per delivery, with additional bonuses during peak hours and for completing multiple deliveries.
When do I get paid?
Payments are processed monthly. You'll receive your earnings directly to your registered bank account within 1–2 business days.
Do I need my own vehicle?
Yes, you need your own vehicle (car or motorcycle) with valid registration and insurance in good working condition.
What are the working hours?
Complete flexibility — work whenever you want. Peak slots (10 AM–2 PM, 2–6 PM, 6–10 PM) typically have higher demand and bonuses.
How long does the application take?
Once submitted, our team reviews your application within 1–2 business days. After approval you can start delivering immediately.
What is the notice period if I want to leave?
A 1-month notice period is required before discontinuing your driver account. This ensures your pending earnings are settled and all outstanding deliveries are properly handed over.
What support do drivers receive?
24/7 driver support via phone and in-app chat, plus access to our driver community, training resources, and dedicated account managers.
