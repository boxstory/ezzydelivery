What do you need to become a delivery driver?

  • A vehicle you can drive in Qatar — car, motorbike or van.
  • A valid Qatar driving license.
  • Any 2 ID documents: QID, passport, driving license or Istimara.
  • A smartphone with WhatsApp and a Gmail account to sign in.
  • Basic knowledge of Doha roads and neighbourhoods helps, but is not required.
