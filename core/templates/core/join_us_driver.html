Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn as a driver?
Earnings vary based on the number of deliveries you complete. On average, drivers earn QAR 50+ per delivery, with additional bonuses during peak hours and for completing multiple deliveries.
When do I get paid?
Payments are processed weekly every Monday. You'll receive your earnings directly to your registered bank account within 1-2 business days.
Do I need my own vehicle?
Yes, you need to have your own vehicle (car or motorcycle) with valid registration and insurance. The vehicle should be in good working condition.
What are the working hours?
You have complete flexibility! Work whenever you want - mornings, evenings, weekends, or weekdays. Peak hours (12 PM - 3 PM and 7 PM - 10 PM) typically have higher demand and bonuses.
How long does the application process take?
Once you submit your application with all required documents, our team reviews it within 1-2 business days. After approval, you can start delivering immediately.
What support do drivers receive?
We provide 24/7 driver support via phone and in-app chat. You'll also have access to our driver community, training resources, and dedicated account managers to help you succeed.
