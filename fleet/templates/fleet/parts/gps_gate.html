Turn on location

EzzyDelivery needs your location while you work.

  1. Swipe down from the top of the screen and turn on Location.
  2. Tap Turn on location below, then choose Allow.
  3. Still blocked? Open Chrome › ⋮ › Settings › Site settings › Location and set {{ request.get_host }} to Allow.
  1. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services and turn it on.
  2. In the same list open Safari Websites and choose While Using the App.
  3. Come back, tap Turn on location, then choose Allow.
